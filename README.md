## The Series

*The Fractured Path* is a 15-book LitRPG fantasy series following Cael, a young man whose Kindling at age 14 produces an unprecedented classification: [SHATTERED]. In a world where your Path determines your rank, your access, and your worth, an unclassifiable practitioner has no place — and in the history of Valdris, the last time [SHATTERED] appeared, eleven people died.

Over fifteen books Cael discovers that his Fractured Path is not a defect. It is what Paths were before someone decided they needed to be controlled.

**Tone:** He Who Fights Monsters × Iron Prince × R.A. Salvatore — dry humor, earned progression, vivid combat, found family, long identity arc. Clean and shareable.
