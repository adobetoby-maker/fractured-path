# Chapter 24 — The Message

The days after the fight with Darrow Innes changed Cael's standing in Ardenmere exactly the way he'd hoped they would, though the shape of the change was quieter than he'd expected — less a dramatic shift and more a gradual settling into something that had already been building for months, now simply confirmed beyond argument.

Vell's ledger listed him, for the first time, by name rather than by rating — *Cael Hesk-ward, assessed-Copper, def. Darrow Innes (Bronze R1, Iron Path)* — a small administrative detail that carried, in the circuit's own logic, more weight than any amount of praise could have. He was, unmistakably now, a known quantity in a district that had learned, slowly and then all at once, to notice when he was there and to remark on it when he wasn't.
