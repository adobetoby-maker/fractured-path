# Book-Scope Review — Book 8, *Before the Paths*

**Seat:** EDITOR (Penname Codex v3)  
**Scope:** assembled manuscript, 24 closed chapters  
**Verdict:** **STRUCTURAL_HOLD**

The book's continuity spine, reveal architecture, future plants, internal arc, roster, inventory, and violence discipline are sound at assembled scale. The hold is mandatory because Gate 27 is binary and Chapter 10 gives a companion a deliberate, successful lie without naming that conduct as wrong or charging it to the liar. ## Findings, ordered by severity

### 1. BLOCKER — Gate 27: a companion's useful lie is rewarded and not named as wrong

- **Location and quote:** `books/book-08-before-the-paths/chapters/chapter-10.md:83` — “He's the carter's man. He was a factor once, up west; the name's a habit and the title's a joke he tells.”
- **Confirmation in the narration:** `books/book-08-before-the-paths/chapters/chapter-10.md:91` — “had been stopped from it by a defector's tradecraft and a lie about a carter.”
- **Book-scope judgment:** Seln is one of the six companions recorded in `universe/STATE_LEDGER.md`, so the conduct falls directly under Gate 27's requirement that the protagonist and companions act honestly. The deception works: it preserves access, the clerk accepts it, and Seln's next instruction is “Be nobody” (`chapter-10.md:97`). Vastin pays for his reflex, but Seln does not pay for the lie, and the narrative calls it a lie without calling it wrong. This fails the binary text at `craft/THE_AUTHOR.md:659-660`: “FAIL on any single violation.”
- **Repair target:** preserve the door tension and Seln's competence, but replace the invented biography with a technically truthful deflection under the scholars' provision (count, function, and lack of standing), or explicitly name the deception as wrong and give Seln an immediate, traceable moral cost. Re-audit the revised exchange under Gate 27 before lifting the hold.
