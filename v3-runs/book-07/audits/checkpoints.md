## Deviations departed-and-returned (charter §4.8)

Places where the drafted prose departs from the card text and the departure is **not** covered by
EXC-B7-001 … EXC-B7-005. Each is logged with chapter, line, the prose quote, and the card's wording.

1. **Box 28 — hold pricing chapter.** Card: "the holds price them (Ch9)". Prose: Ch9 has no hold-pricing beat; the holds ask for the crew by name in Ch8 line 25 — "\"Holds came to the ferry,\" Pike said, to the page. He had not looked up. \"Asked who. I said you.\"" *Returned:* the beat itself is delivered, one chapter earlier than the card places it.

2. **Box 28 — artifact-recovery chapters.** Card: "artifact recovery (Ch8, Ch20–21)". Prose: Ch21 contains no recovery; the chapter's take is wall copies — Ch21 line 177: "\"Walls,\" Karis said, from the coping. \"I've the walls. Twelve sheets.\"" Recovery of the kit is Ch20 only (Ch20 line 117: "The kit went up the stair on Brom's shoulder."). *Returned:* two recovery beats delivered (Ch8 posting, Ch20 recovery), not three.

3. **Box 28 — monster-clearance chapters.** Card: "monster clearance (Ch3, Ch10–11)". Prose: Ch10 is trials and cataloguing only; the fight is Ch11, and it is a fighting withdrawal from a recovery contract, not a clearance — Ch11 line 247: "they live in there because nothing that walks in can fight, and I walked in, and I could, and I took their floor away". The book's one paid clearance is Ch3. *Returned:* the combat is delivered; the contract type is not clearance.

4. **Boxes 29 and 425 — count of failed surface readings.** Card: "three surface readings that return nothing (Ch6, Ch7, Ch7)" / "three failed surface readings — Ch6, Ch7, Ch7 — before the deep reading that completes, Ch13". Prose runs four before Ch13 — Ch12 line 87: "The ribs, twice; the wrist; this. Four times. I don't say a finding four times to a patient and change it on the fifth." *Returned:* the pattern and the Ch13 completion are as carded; the count is one higher.

5. **Box 31 — self-reading chapters.** Card: "He uses it, quietly, to study his own (Ch14, Ch16, Ch22)". Prose: Ch16 contains no self-reading; it carries the outward rule instead (Ch16 line 173: "*The reading turned outward. I ask first. Every time.*"). The self-readings are Ch14 line 187 and two in Ch22 (lines 11–41, and line 49 — "He read himself again before dawn, on the hundred and twentieth"). Checkpoint 427 states the correct pair (Ch14, Ch22). *Returned:* three readings delivered, on the days Oryn set, in two chapters.

6. **Box 32 — Long Stair location chapter.** Card: "the Long Stair, Ch20". Prose: the site is sighted and its line called in Ch18 line 127 — "A floor. The same grey — he would have known it from a mile — round, level, lipped … And in its middle … a stair." Ch20 is the chamber's interior. *Returned:* three further sites located, as carded.

7. **Box 33 — second staging chapter for the anomaly.** Card: "The convergence is staged with total honesty in Ch14 and Ch16." Prose: Ch16 carries no anomaly beat at all (grep for "anomaly", "still place", "session nine" across Ch16 returns zero). The second and third stagings are Ch22 line 45 ("And the anomaly's there. Not a fragment. Not a flow. A still place") and Ch24 line 69. Checkpoint 428 states the correct set (Ch14, Ch22, Ch24). *Returned:* convergence-without-resolution fully delivered, in different chapters.

8. **Box 34 — survey-stake chapters.** Card: "At the Fallow Ring (Ch11) and again at the Long Stair (Ch20), the companions find registry survey stakes". Prose: the Ring stake is Ch10 line 65 — "\"Registry stamp,\" Seln said. \"And a number. Thirty-one.\"" — and the Stair's pair is Ch18 line 137. Checkpoint 424 states the correct set (Ch10, Ch18, Ch23). *Returned:* both plants delivered, one chapter earlier each.

9. **Box 35 — Arbiter-plant chapters.** Card: "Cael's Arbiter … does not change inside the Quiet, because there is nothing to silence (Ch11, Ch22)". Prose: Ch22 carries no Arbiter beat; the plant runs Ch9 line 229 ("Mine didn't. Mine's been stopped since I was fourteen."), Ch11 line 249, and Ch16 line 9. Checkpoint 424 states the correct set (Ch9, Ch11, Ch16). *Returned:* plant delivered three times.

10. **Box 35 / 424 — wording of Karis's finding.** Card: an Arbiter should not "fail like a piece of equipment on a circuit". Prose uses a different image — Ch15 line 65: "should not fail the way five looms fail when somebody shuts the sluice on the wheel that drives them." *Returned:* the claim is identical; the figure is not.

11. **Box 37 — the Ch8 contract's nature.** Card: "a genuine recovery contract for a survey kit the Compact abandoned five years ago". Prose: Ch12 line 179 — "For a kit that was never here. There's nothing inside that circle a person left except the stake, and the stake's where they measured to, not where they lost anything." (Scored as the PARTIAL gap on box 37.)

17. **Box 435 — where "home is Lowmarch" lands.** Card: "the last chapter's 'home is Lowmarch' is earned". Prose: the sentence is in Ch23 line 139 — "Home is Lowmarch now.
