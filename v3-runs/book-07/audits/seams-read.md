## 7. FINDINGS

### HIGH

**F1 — The Lowmarch↔Thornwater road takes two days everywhere in the book except Ch9, where it takes three; and Ch8 contradicts itself two lines apart.**
Ch8 L215, Karis, with the map out:

> "Thornwater's east gate." Karis had the map out … "**Two days from here to the steading. Two on from it to the rise. Four.**"

Ch8 L217, Brom, the very next line:

> "**The ninety-first tomorrow**," Brom said. "**Thornwater the ninety-third.**"

Ch9 executes Brom: L3 "He marked it at the plank on the morning of the **ninety-first** … the five of them went east off the landing"; L41 "He read Ephram's letter at the wash, **at noon on the ninety-second**"; L83 "**Thornwater on the ninety-third day**." Three calendar days.
Every other rendering of that road is two: Ch1 L65 "Grain, one cart, **two days**" → depart sunrise d42 (L81), gate d43 (L185); Ch3 L29 "**Two days back up the road** … Thornwater's fence at the end of the second day" → depart d52, ford at dusk d53 (L243); Ch5 L49 "**Two days up the Thornwater road to the fan**" → d71 to d72; and the return in Ch17 L9/L7, with the same mule and the same three boxes, is **d111 → d112**.
*Severity:* HIGH — the internal contradiction is on the page inside one exchange, and it contradicts closed prose in three earlier chapters and one later one.
*Minimal repair (two edits, no cascade; keeps d93 as the anchor for Oryn's "nine days"):* (a) Ch8 L217 → "We mark it tomorrow and walk the day after," Brom said. "Thornwater the ninety-third."; (b) Ch9 L13 → "…and the five of them went east off the landing" becomes "…and at first light on the ninety-second the five of them went east off the landing." Karis's "Two days … Four" then stands, the wash at noon on the 92nd stands, and the 35-day and 15-day counts at Ch9 L85/L93 stay correct.

### MEDIUM

**F2 — Cael's rib clock gains two days between Ch9 and Ch12 and stays two days fast.**
Break: Ch6 L123/L133, "**on the seventy-eighth day** … Cael: three ribs, left side, low." Taping: Ch7 L3, "She taped his ribs **on the eighty-first morning**."
Correct: Ch7 L115 "**Six days.** Those feel like a fortnight" (d84 − d78 = 6 ✓); Ch9 L93 "**Fifteen days**," she said. "**Since the break**" (d93 − d78 = 15 ✓).
Wrong: Ch12 L219 "*Ribs, **tape twenty-one days**.*" on d97 — 19 from the break, 16 from the taping. Ch13 L17/L19 "**Twenty-three days** of boiled linen round the ribs" / "**Twenty-three days.** A rib is six weeks. These are done." on d99 — 21 from the break, 18 from the taping.
Separately, Ch8 L7 on d90 calls it "the tape round his ribs … **twelve days old**" — twelve counts from the break, but the tape went on on the 81st, so the tape is nine days old.
*Minimal repair:* Ch12 L219 "twenty-one" → "nineteen"; Ch13 L17 and L19 "Twenty-three" → "Twenty-one" (both instances; "A rib is six weeks. These are done" survives intact); Ch8 L7 "twelve days old" → "nine days old" (or "twelve days since the bench").

**F3 — Ch23's farewell scene counts five people and two mules where there are four and one.**
Ch23 L37: Oryn "had **the mule's rope in her hand** and the roll on her back and her face square to the north-west track." Then L41:

> Fifty paces, from where **the five of them stood with the mules** to where the two tracks parted at a stone that was only a stone…

and L43: "She looked at **the five behind him**, once, the way she looked at a line of patients at a table's end"; and L51: "he stood at the stone until the gap in the hills had her, and **went back to the five**."
Cael has walked out with Oryn, so four stand behind him (Lira, Brom, Karis, Seln), and Oryn's mule is at her hand, leaving one. The party is two mules from Ch18 L41/L81 and one from Ch23 L53 ("Brom picked up **the mule's** rope") onward.
*Minimal repair:* L41 "the five of them stood with the mules" → "the four of them stood with the mule"; L43 "the five behind him" → "the four behind him"; L51 "went back to the five" → "went back to the four" (or leave L51, which reads as the crew-label; L43 is the un-rescuable one).

**F4 — "A hundred and eight in hand" never moves across forty days, through two on-page cash purchases.**
The figure is exact when set: Ch7 L105 "**One hundred and sixteen in hand**, the smith's six still owed" (46 + 70 ✓); Ch8 L129 "**A hundred and eight in hand**" — which is 116 minus the smith's six *and* minus Lira's boot, "the right one a carter's, **bought at Oxhollow's gate for two marks**" (Ch8 L7). The book therefore tracks a two-mark boot.
It then reports 108 in every log entry from Ch8 L253 to Ch24 L63 — through Ch14 L141, where the steading's woman "named a price, and Lira **paid it out of the pouch** one-handed," buying the common room, a kid roasted whole, apples and a whole cheese; and through Ch24 L57, "Karis took the inn's back room for the sheets, **at a price the woman who kept the inn named and Karis paid without counting**."
*Severity:* MEDIUM (arithmetic), borderline HIGH given the book's own two-mark precision.
*Minimal repair:* drop the figure by a named amount once at each purchase — Ch14 L203 "*A hundred and eight in hand*" → "*A hundred and two in hand; six to the hold for the room and the kid*"; Ch24 L63 → "*Ninety-six in hand; six to the inn for the back room*" (and adjust Ch15 L151, Ch16 L123/L199, Ch17 L71/L141, Ch20 L133, Ch22 L43, Ch23 L137 from 108 to 102).

**F5 — Oryn's lateness numbers do not advance, and once run backwards.**
(a) Ch12 L113, d97: "The route's **eleven days north of here** … It was **nine** when I sat down at your fire at the bend" — the bend fire is d95, so +2 in two days, and she states the rule that it only grows: "Every day I sit here the route doesn't get shorter. It gets longer at the far end." Ch15 L25, d104, seven days of standing still later: "I'm **nine days late here** and eleven at the next."
(b) Ch18 L53, d113: "Thornwater's next visit is **eleven days late**" — a projection assuming "the day south and the two back." She then spends nine days instead of three, and Ch23 L37, d122, is still "**I'm eleven days late there**," with Ch24 L107's letter closing on "Nobody died of **eleven days**."
The d104→d110 pair ("eleven at the next" +6 = Ch16 L127's "**seventeen days late**") is exact, so the ledger is capable of moving; these two are the places it doesn't.
*Minimal repair:* (a) Ch15 L25 → "I'm **sixteen days late here** and eighteen at the next," and Ch16 L127/L193 "seventeen" → "twenty-two"; **or**, cheaper, make Ch12's number a distance rather than a lateness: Ch12 L113 "The route's eleven days north of here" → "The next hold's **four days** north of here, and I'm **two days** late to it already." (b) Ch23 L37 "I'm eleven days late there" → "I was eleven days late there when I turned south. I'm twenty now" — this preserves Ch24 L107's letter if that line is also changed, or leave the letter and change Ch18 L53/L67's projection to twenty so that eleven never becomes the canonical number.

**F6 — Ch17 equates "the ferry came in" with the report going west, but the mail ferry came in on d91 and runs monthly.**
Ch17 L53, Pike:

> "**Posted the day after the ferry came in — the day after your report went west**, if you're counting … it's been on that nail **nine days**."

d112 − 9 = d103, and Ch16 L111 puts the westbound run at d102 ("The ferry went west **eight days ago**, with Pike's copy in the sack") — so the *departure* half is exact. But the arrival half is not: Ch9 L9 has the sack coming off the ferry "at first light" on **d91**, Ch4 L169–171 establishes the service as "**mail, once a month**, carried to the Line by the ferry and back," and Ch4's previous arrival was d60 (31 days ✓). An inbound run on d102 is eleven days after d91 and breaks the cadence.
*Minimal repair:* Ch17 L53 → "Posted the day after the ferry went out — the day after your report went west, if you're counting."

**F7 — Ch24 calls Lira's sling six weeks old at thirty-three days.**
Ch24 L9: "Lira, one-armed, **the sling gone grey with six weeks of road**." She was bitten at the Ring on d96 (Ch11 L49) and this is d129 — thirty-three days, four weeks five days. The same page is otherwise exact ("she had walked **six days** from the fourth site," d123 → d129 ✓).
