# FUTURE SERIES MAP — The Fractured Path Universe

**Do not develop until the main 15-book series is mature.**

---

## Sequel possibility — THE OPEN PATHS

Set 10–20 years after *Unclassified*.

Cael's redesigned system has been operating for a generation. The first cohort of practitioners who Kindled under the revised Arbiter system are now adults. The cap is gone; the falsification layer is gone; [UNBOUND] potential can now form legitimately.

The question the series left open: *Was the Architect wrong about what unlimited potential produces without structure?*

A new protagonist — not Cael — navigates a world that is still figuring out what the revised system means. Cael exists as a historical figure and, possibly, as someone still alive and watching.

**Tone:** Sequel in the Salvatore sense — new generation, same world, original characters available but not central.

---

## Prequel possibility — THE ARCHITECT

Set 400+ years before *The Shattered*.

The Architect — as a living person, before the system was built — witnesses the [UNBOUND] conflicts that motivate the system's creation. A tragedy in the classical sense: a person who was right about a problem and catastrophically wrong about the solution.

**Tone:** Darker. This is where the deaths happen that justify everything the main series is fighting against. It must make the Architect sympathetic without making their choice defensible.

**Constraint:** Do not write until Book 14 is complete. The Architect's full characterization in Book 14 defines what this prequel can and cannot do.

---

## Side series — SELN

What Seln was doing during Books 12-13.

A short series (2-3 books) following Seln's parallel operation during the Storm arc — the intelligence work he was running from outside the companion group, the specific relay points he dismantled in Book 15, and the cost of making the choice to leave.

**Tone:** Spy thriller. Shadow Path as a lens on what it costs to operate alone when the thing you believe in requires you to be somewhere else.

**Constraint:** Cannot be written until the main series is complete, because Seln's actions in Books 12-15 must be fully defined before his perspective can be rendered.
