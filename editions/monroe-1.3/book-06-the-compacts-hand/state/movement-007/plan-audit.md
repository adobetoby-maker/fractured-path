The protected words still govern M8. M8 must not use them to relabel Vastin as Halcenvane's evaluator of record; in the surrounding prose, Gault remains the house's evaluator and Vastin remains the Archmarshal/certifying office. If the coordinator does not accept “evaluator of record” as the minute's certification-role label, that protected line—not M7—needs an owner ruling before M8 is drafted.

### 9. New minor canon — RATIFY ALL FOUR GROUPS

#### Iron Skin in use

**Page.** “It always began at the wrists”; the surface stops creasing and takes a dull grey under lamplight (`manuscript/chapter-45.md:37–39`). Under the bar, the load travels through forearms, shoulders, back, hips, heels, and floor (`manuscript/chapter-45.md:43–51`).

**Governing text.** Book 2 says Iron Skin practitioners “raise a hardness of the surface of the body at need” (`../book-02-iron-circuit/manuscript/chapter-18.md:21–25`), shows a blow's weight going through Brom “into his heels” (`../book-02-iron-circuit/manuscript/chapter-18.md:181–185`), and maps hardening over forearm, foot, chest, and shoulders rather than the whole body (`../book-02-iron-circuit/manuscript/chapter-20.md:193–201`). The §9 “Iron Skin watcher” restriction is an untouched person/thread, not a ban on describing Brom's Path (`BOOK_MAP.md:640`).

**Ruling.** The visible change is a compatible close description of already-established surface hardening and load routing. It does not identify or touch the reserved watcher.

#### The sigil frame “under load”

**Page.** Brom's schedule requires “a reading in the sigil frame, under load … in the presence of the three assessors” (`manuscript/chapter-45.md:11`), with the assessors to set the load at the title figure and a little over (`manuscript/chapter-45.md:19`). The practice frame is carefully distinguished: “A sigil frame … read what the practitioner's Path did under the weight. This one read nothing. It only pressed” (`manuscript/chapter-45.md:35`).

**Governing text.** Book 4 establishes the district frame and sigil (`../book-04-copper-crown/manuscript/chapter-60.md:69–77`) and a Path-specific advancement protocol of sequence, load figures, then frame (`../book-04-copper-crown/manuscript/chapter-60.md:77,115–137`). The closed M1 coordinator ruling fixes a standard advancement panel as three assessors plus a sigil frame (`STATE_LEDGER.md:152`).

**Ruling.** “Under load” is a permissible Iron Skin-specific extension of the already-canon advancement apparatus, not a replacement for Lira's Wind protocol. The page distinguishes the crude training press from the reading instrument and creates no conflict with Books 1–5.

#### Farm, orchard, gardener

**Page.** The south-slope farm, walled kitchen gardens, two orchards, stream, water meadows, track, and cottage are laid out at `manuscript/chapter-42.md:65–67`; the unnamed gardener has kept the house's trees for thirty years (`manuscript/chapter-42.md:101–105`).

**Governing text.** Halcenvane already has a kitchen garden in Book 3 (`../book-03-no-path-given/manuscript/chapter-17.md:9`; see also `chapter-15.md:231`). M7 is assigned a new open road-craft exercise and the author owns its exercises and weather (`packets/MOVEMENT-007.md:25,42–44`); the plan requires Seln's road-craft but fixes no contrary bluff geography (`BOOK_MAP.md:353,379–380`). The role remains unnamed as required by the packet.

**Ruling.** This expands an existing campus edge without displacing any mapped structure. The orchard and gardener are safe minor canon.

#### Withrow's four “release” letters

**Page.** Of thirty-four letters, four advise Withrow “to let you go” (`manuscript/chapter-44.md:225–229`), using “*Release*” and “*Come to an accommodation*” (`manuscript/chapter-44.md:233`); she answers all thirty-four herself (`manuscript/chapter-44.md:241–251`).

**Governing text.** M7 expressly requires “Withrow's stack of letters from other chancellors; she answers them herself” (`packets/MOVEMENT-007.md:29`), and the supporting-cast plan makes her independent choice to answer part of her arc (`BOOK_MAP.md:408`). The packet keeps the other chancellors unnamed (`packets/MOVEMENT-007.md:7`), and §9 imposes no further release limit on these institutional-pressure letters.

**Ruling.** Four advising release is within the author-owned content of the mandated stack. It sharpens the clean-door pressure without making Withrow release anyone, naming another chancellor, or changing the proceeding.
