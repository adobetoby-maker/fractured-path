### Located continuity defects

1. **Chapter 41, lines 61 and 89 — recess census.** The refectory contains “all forty of the house's remaining enrollees,” but Bracken's same-morning daybook says “In residence: forty-one enrolled, by slip.” Both are presented as exact totals. Because counting is part of Cael's and Bracken's characterization, the mismatch is more visible than an ordinary crowd estimate. Choose one total and carry it through the paragraph; the daybook's documentary forty-one is the stronger anchor.

2. **Chapter 42, lines 103 and 111–131 — open-approach exercise.** The gardener is told that five people will approach his door one at a time, but the exercise gives exactly four approaches: Lira, Karis, Cael, and Brom, with Brom explicitly “last.” Seln supervises and had already visited that morning. Change “five people” to “four people,” unless Seln is intentionally meant to demonstrate and is added to the scene.

These are line-level count defects, not structural problems.
