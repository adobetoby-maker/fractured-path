The next expected event is the opening of Seln's case in the annex. The last line—“Tomorrow, Seln's annex. Hesk says mind the shape. I think tomorrow is the shape.”—creates a clean forward pull. I would continue.

## Repair brief

### 1. Correct the seal-number deduction

- **Location:** chapter 30, cutters' issue ledger, lines 41–43 (“The two numbers were one apart” / “the number between them”).
- **Observed issue:** the arithmetic language is self-contradictory. If the two registered numbers are consecutive, no number lies between them.
- **Effect on the reader:** the key proof that the facilities-office seal was never issued becomes momentarily impossible, weakening trust in Havel's otherwise exact method.
- **Proposed scope:** revise only the two-number relationship so the registered entries clearly sit on either side of the absent number, with no ledger line available for it. Recheck the three later references to the number and gap; no scene rewrite.
- **Strength to preserve:** the three-way register check, Havel's refusal to interpret beyond evidence, and the decision that there will be no seventh sheet.
