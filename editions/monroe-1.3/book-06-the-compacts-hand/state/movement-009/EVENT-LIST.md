## What I decided where the packet is open

- **Order at the counter:** Lira, Brom, Cael, then Karis last (her forfeiture needs the room; she comes down from locking it). Seln is not in the sequence: he is staff, "outside any form" of Bracken's, and is seen at his copying table that day so that the next morning's absence lands.
- **The margin longhand** goes on Cael's leaf, in Bracken's hand, timed by the counter clock he has just wound: withdrawn *freely*, of the enrollee's own motion, *and not by any officer of this institution* — and before any notice of date was served on the house. It is Bracken protecting Withrow's four days.
- **The five words in the enrollment book:** *Withdrawn. Resigned in good standing.* (Withrow's "five words in his hand", ch53.)
- **Brom signs his withdrawal with the pen that has waited on the counter since the fourth day of the year for his advancement signature.** The finding stays on his table, unsigned, beside the sealed Velmere envelope. Neither is touched again.
- **Brom's own two boxes** (roped under his bed since the week of the gate): unpacked on the page into one pack at Seln's thirty pounds; what will not go is left with the porter; the two empty deal boxes he carries up four flights to the reading room for the assistant, who inherits the room. Five boxes that day: two up, three down.
- **The assistant's three things** are three things she says at the counter, numbered on the stair. Karis's unwritten letter stays unwritten.
- **The rotation's book** (shut for good in ch53) goes into the corridor stove. It is the one record that could turn *compliant* into something else for the house.
- **Lira's last class** is the year's closing seminar on panels and formats, sitting on the Seventh-day because the house keeps no rest day in its last week. Cael waits on the corridor bench and hears the thanks through the open door; Lira's window (~1,100) is the class and the corridor, and holds her dawn sentence to Rooke and the red book's two words.
- **The red book's two words** (unread since M7): *Late.*, struck through, and under it *Hers.* Rooke shows her without comment.
- **The cart and the horse** are bought (Book 7's default C1 sells them in its first week): the old second cart and a brown gelding "who has stopped liking hills", from Lira's carter with the good horses, spoken for at first light on the day of the bout, led up the bluff by Lira after her class. No name for the horse.
- **Help refused in shortening words:** the stableman ("No. Thank you. They're mine to carry."), a porter ("No, thank you."), the district officer ("No."). Ephram walks the third trip beside him with his right arm slung, carrying nothing, and offers nothing.
- **The district officer** is the stout, fair, kind man from the north-road station (ch50). His notice is to the Chancellor, to produce the enrollee on the Fourth-day; by the time he is shown in, the enrollment book has four new lines. He is decent about it. His horse stands at the lodge ring.
- **Where the four sleep on the Seventh-day night:** the wool-factors' guesting-house in Ostrand; the cart in its yard by the press.
- **How the five meet:** Seln gives Cael a floor sheet across the wing's counter with one line in his own hand (*First-day. The crossroads past the eighteenth stone, east. By dusk.*), takes it back, and puts it in the stove.
- **Seln's duplicates** (nineteen bundles under the copying table): burned in the wing's stove through the Seventh-day, a posting at a time. The morning clerk finds the cupboard swept and more ash than a Seventh-day makes.
- **The summaries:** the wing's weekly floor summaries in Seln's square hand, pinned in order above the copying table; the clerk leaves them; they are still pinned eleven years later.
- **Seln's road:** out by the farm track, the orchard gate and the meadow gate (M7's night road); the east road past Ostrand by the upper lane; he looks back from the first rise at the lamp in the library's top window, standing in the middle of the road like a man who wants to be counted.
- **The second visit to Denvash** (needed so the machine's week can make a third): one clause — the same two men, in the recess, to see whether the answer had changed.
- **The station is unstaffed:** its keeper was withdrawn four years ago; the station's stamp lives in the last waystation keeper's drawer (so the carters' "stamps as far as the station" stays true); the evaluation book stops four years back on an entry begun and not finished. Somebody (carters) sweeps it and has mended the roof.
- **The five crossings:** the road runs between the station house and the frame-house over a stone sill with two sockets for a bar that is gone. Each crosses the sill in a sentence; Brom's gets the room.
- **The Line** is where the station's writ ends — "a morning's walk from the sigil" in the code, measured once by a keeper and never marked. Lira has the place from her third carter: the churned circle where the wagons that will not go on turn round. Nothing official marks it. Anchor, which asks at every threshold, asks nothing there.
- **"Nobody's watching."** is Brom's (he checks; the last watcher nodded to him), after Seln, who has looked for watchers on other people's behalf for fifteen years, asks that somebody else say it.
- **The first night past the Line** is a wet night under the cart's canvas with no fire; the shelf's fire on the second night is the first. Cael writes the reply to Daeva that night by one of Karis's pin-head flames.
- **"Both promises ride east with me."** is said aloud by Cael, to Lira, when the rider has gone.
- **The purse** is read out by Karis and is more than any of them expected (Seln's share the largest); no figure is put on the page (Book 7 stages what remains).
- **Ephram's absence, once:** Brom sets out six cups from habit and puts the sixth back.
- **The two things the page does not say:** whose the quiet thing was; and what rides in the case.
- **The anomaly's shorter procedure:** the old volume opened at session nine (his own hand at fifteen), the still place checked where it lies, the three words written.
- **The closing window:** a faceless clerk of no place closes four lines on a file and finds a fifth already advised on a slip that carries no office's mark; he enters it as advised.
- **Closing-exchange speakers (my attribution; the source page was not consulted):** Cael's protected first line; Lira "Let them escalate. They'll need better maps."; Brom "And better roads."; Cael "Noted."
