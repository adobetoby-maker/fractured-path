# Editorial review — Movement 9 (Chapters 55–60)

## Status and verdict

This is a **separate-model informed editorial review** of all six supplied chapters in declared order, with the compiled canon, ledger, movement packet, protected-wording map, formula instructions, measured metrics, and source-overlap report. It is not author self-review, not a blind fresh-context read, and not a real human audience test.

**Verdict: REPAIR REQUIRED, bounded.** The movement is structurally and emotionally successful, and the binding ending state is intact. It does, however, contain one direct Tier A wording violation and two runs that the supplied overlap report classifies as unprotected source reuse. Those are release blockers under the brief. Two small clarity repairs would improve one-pass chronology and action staging; no broad rewrite, scene deletion, or fight compression is warranted.

## Unscored reader response

Interest catches immediately in Chapter 55: Bracken's clock, four leaves under the lamp, and the mismatch between four light forms and the eleven-pound answer make procedure feel dangerous before any explanation can take over. It strengthens through the four distinct departures: Lira writes herself out in her own words, Brom refuses a future the frame has honestly found for him, Karis surrenders the institutional scaffolding around her work without surrendering her tier, and Cael receives the margin that says nobody pushed him.

The people who matter most in this movement are Lira and Seln. Lira's “Same door. Same leaving. Mine this time.” condenses her whole institutional arc into a choice she owns. Seln's true-but-empty reports, bad backward look from the crest, prepared fire, and willingness to sleep when his own pupils out-argue him turn a watcher into the fifth traveler by acts rather than confession. Brom and Karis remain independent centers of care: he carries, checks, and refuses to read the family request; she turns lost privileges into the first archive nobody can close.

Interest slips briefly in Chapter 58. The institutional aftermath advances through the week after the academic close, then the narration returns to the party leaving Ostrand on the earlier First-day without explicitly marking the rewind. The opening remains thematically strong, but the reader must reconstruct the time shift. Chapter 60's long inventory also becomes denser than the surrounding prose, though its emotional point—the names, the protected source, and the honest blank—still lands.

I expect the first eastern hold, where five people must price labor and trust without registry categories, and I expect the escalated file to find a new route toward them. I would continue. The book closes its local argument completely while opening a materially different world rather than merely postponing the same hearing.

## Strengths to preserve

- The four resignations are not repetitions: each person relinquishes a different thing in a different personal language.
- Bureaucratic procedure remains dramatic because every form, margin, key, and blank changes someone's options.
- The quiet road earns its length. The fords, follower, failing infrastructure, tired horse, boxes, and switchbacks make departure physical rather than symbolic shorthand.
- The five-person group forms through reciprocal competence. Nobody becomes a generic helper or comic appendage.
- Motifs transform across the movement: true records protect and expose; doors close, open both ways, or prove never to have existed in the ground; counting shifts from surveillance to belonging.
- The ending holds both poles without pretending the first was foolish, then gives Cael self-definition without erasing institutional pursuit.

## Repair brief

1. **Location:** Chapter 60, the recalled first-pole entry beginning “I can work within the system.” **Observed issue:** Tier A text has been changed from “a stamp renewed four times” to “two renewals on paper,” contrary to BOOK_MAP §8 and its explicit pending-owner default. **Effect on the reader:** It breaks a protected callback at the moment the book reconciles its central argument and creates edition-to-edition wording drift. **Proposed scope:** Restore the full protected sentence character-for-character; change it only through an owner ruling that updates BOOK_MAP protection. **Strength to preserve:** Keep Cael's realization that both poles are true and that his earlier self was early, not foolish.

2. **Location:** Chapter 60, inventory paragraphs 102–103 (“Wind-adjacent — Lira…” and “Each with a name beside it…”). **Observed issue:** The supplied overlap gate classifies a 104-word run and a 35-word run as unprotected source reuse. **Effect on the reader:** The edition's required new-prose standard fails at its final progression accounting, regardless of the passage's story accuracy. **Proposed scope:** Recompose only these two runs from the fixed fragment order, names, seal state, and intentional blank; preserve all protected short wording. If exact preservation is intended, obtain and record an owner protection ruling before rerunning overlap. **Strength to preserve:** Keep the nine-fragment payoff, the unnamed Anchor source, the protected Shadow source, and the blank as an ethical act of record-keeping.

3. **Location:** Chapter 58 at “They left Ostrand at first light,” and Chapter 60's first scree-descent pass. **Observed issue:** The road thread resumes after a future-facing institutional sequence without a rewind marker, and the descent assigns all five travelers while locating only the second of three boxes. **Effect on the reader:** Both moments cause avoidable one-pass timeline or spatial reconstruction in an otherwise exceptionally controlled movement. **Proposed scope:** Add one compact temporal anchor in Chapter 58 and one compact sequencing/location sentence in Chapter 60; do not restructure the machine sequence or shorten the road challenge. **Strength to preserve:** Preserve the dramatic irony of the machine closing on air and the detailed, cooperative logistics of the descent.
