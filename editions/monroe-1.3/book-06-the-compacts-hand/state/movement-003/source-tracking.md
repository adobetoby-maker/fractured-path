## New entry points and beat orders for dense scenes

“Dense” here means four or more tracked passages in a manuscript scene. The suggestions preserve
the packet's required facts and protected lines while changing the dramatic question, entry point,
and order in which the facts acquire meaning.

- **14 scene 1 (9):** Enter after Vastin has already signed the petition. Let the crooked press or
  a missing routing impression make him reconstruct why the courtesy copy disturbed him. Reveal
  the adopted sentence, the two old cases, and the daughter's letter out of chronological order as
  he checks what authority his seal actually carries.
- **14 scene 2 (12):** Make the clerk's paused pen the scene's trigger. Vastin must decide what to
  disclose to the clerk before the reader learns about the unasked twelfth question. Put the
