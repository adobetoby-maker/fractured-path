## Story and continuity

### What works

- The movement delivers the planned escalation in the correct order: Vastin is refused; public attention becomes an economy; the cost reaches the circle; the service-side pattern reaches Denvash; the house wins validity and still proceeds toward re-evaluation.
- Cause and effect remain legible. The observers cause Rooke to close the floor; that costs Lira her useful daylight; the honest floor then lets her discover the lane's shadow under lamps. The new learning is an earned consequence, not compensation that makes the loss fair.
- The recurring instrument metaphor develops rather than repeats. Vastin writes a finding with no proceeding; Umber refuses added meaning; Daeva files witness testimony; Jent withdraws his own false overreach; the seat can use every honest product without listening to its maker.
- The whole-chapter Vastin cutaway is purposeful and stays inside his allowed knowledge. He notices the Kindling mismatch but gives it no theory. He learns nothing about the cache, integration, the sub-layer, or the faceless faction.
- Jent remains especially effective. His correction of his own overstatement in chapter 20 prevents the conflict from collapsing into good people versus liars and makes Cael's search for the unseen “handler” credible.

This is the second new Velmere letter of the book. The book state says two more arrive in Book 6, while `BOOK_MAP` §4e #10 reserves the second letter of the year as the one still unopened four weeks before the ruling in Movement 7. As written, Movement 7 would require an unplanned third letter or would lose the unopened-letter beat. This is a plan/ledger conflict, not a contradiction inside these seven chapters. The clean repair is to let this same chapter-16 letter remain sealed and carry forward.
