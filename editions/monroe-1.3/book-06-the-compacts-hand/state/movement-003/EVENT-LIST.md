## Ch14 — Vastin (whole chapter, his POV; no age; forty years; "the Archmarshal")

1. **Way in: after the fact.** Day 41, a little before noon. The petition is already written, signed and pressed. The crooked press has bitten the seal in at a slant and left one edge of the device faint. He turns the sheet to the light and asks himself whether a faint edge can be challenged, and from there, whether his seal can carry the thing at all. That question walks him backward through the morning: the printed slip (*per standing distribution*: told, not asked, for the first time in three years of papers); the minute showing the proceedings sat yesterday without him; his own advisory set inside paragraph seven, under *adopts*. Then further back, out of order: the daughter's letter first (a careful hand; his reply *no*), and only after it the two files it belonged to (a hill station keeper's false returns; a coast officer's bought rank) — the only two times a finding of his set chapter fourteen moving. The pigeons and porter are present tense around him. He decides the faint edge is still his seal.
2. **Way in: the clerk's pen.** The clerk stops with the pen over the routing column, and Vastin must choose what to say to him. He chooses to give the route only, and to tell the man nothing about why; he watches the clerk write it and knows the outer office has never sent paper *toward* a proceeding. After the wallet goes down, he tries to steady himself by counting: four times in forty years he has lent a finding to proceedings that didn't call him, and four times it was taken. The counting doesn't steady him. What does is going to the drawer for the boy's nine leaves, the clerk's ruled interview sheet, the eleven numbered questions, the boy's own question going the other way, and the twelfth that is on no paper — what the private records hold — left unasked because asking would have been collecting. He is still sure he was right. He notices that being right has left the proceedings one thing of his to use, and they've used it. Scene ends on the nine leaves, not on a forecast.
3. **Way in: the copy in *held*.** Day 43, evening. A copy of somebody else's file lies in his *held* tray and he has moved it twice. Backfill the afternoon: the northern junior, young and good and nervous; a girl's station entry four years old that her floor work outruns; an hour of tolerances; *continue observation*; relief going down the stair. Then the evening again: Vastin reading one line too many times; no word for it; leaves it in *held*.
4. **Way in: the denial already filed.** Day 45, mid-morning. He opens the boy's folder for something else and finds the denial lying on his nine leaves, and cannot at once remember putting it there. Work backward: the pressed seal (a grade of reply); the clerk's single unbroken tread up the flight; then the two sentences [P]. Then the letter-book test: his own refusal from his eleventh year laid beside the seat's — the same school, the same bench. Then the reading as a sentence and not a reply; the craft he taught; the missing citation; *fully constituted* meaning full before he asked; cost to its writer nothing. He reads it one last time and shuts the folder.
5. **Way in: the next morning's consumables ledger.** Day 46, the clerk enters one candle to the Archmarshal's name and remembers the evening: the plain sheets; the request to put it under his name not the floor's; the clerk's one sentence off the work ("Will you be getting home tonight, sir? It's set in to rain.") and the answer. Then Vastin, the same morning, opens the lowest drawer to check the folder is there and reads the tab [P] *Pending a proceeding worth sending it to.* The four claims of the finding given in reverse (the honest instrument in their hands; a procedure dressed as an evaluation; the silence is the instrument's limit; the protocol cannot read him), the third page's sentence about frames said now for a person, the last paragraph. End on the addressee line, left bare.
