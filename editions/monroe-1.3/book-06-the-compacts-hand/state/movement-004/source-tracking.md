## New entry points for dense scenes

### Chapter 21, scenes 4–5 — the small room

Begin after the council, with Karis alone copying the corrected forty-day figure and discovering
that Lira has left the three carters' numbers in the margin. Reconstruct the argument through what
each participant altered, refused to alter, or carried away. That reverses the source's straight
sequence—room inventory, leaving question, carter recap, Karis's precedent, Seln's correction—and
lets the coastal ruling arrive as Karis's private counterexample rather than as the same standing
speech in the same place.

### Chapter 22, scenes 1–3 — boxes, the first pole, and both roads

Open with the kettle finally boiling after the debate. Let the physical evidence of the undecided
night lead backward: Brom puts away a cup beside the word *box*; Lira tests the floor metaphor;
Karis identifies the induction; only then does Cael give the receipts, out of chronological order
and under interruption. End on Brom's left pocket rather than beginning with his three-sentence
middle. This changes both entry and causal order instead of paraphrasing source speeches in turn.

### Chapter 22, scenes 4–5 — Seln's dark-wing cutaway

Enter through the post: Seln handles one of his own empty returns and realizes it will be filed
beside Bracken's true book. From there move to the key, then the people on the dairy step, then the
maintenance records, and disclose the service totals only as labels on old bundles. The present
choice should interrupt memory instead of replaying the source's order—career tally, followed
subjects, lawful discovery, delayed corridor understanding, Cael's file, case/key, cost audit.

### Chapter 23, scene 4 — Rooke's brief

Keep the bread, but begin with Lira tearing the bottom slice out of the stack and asking what fails
first. Let Rooke answer from the fourth exchange backward: range denial, the third exchange's false
gift, the second's exam, then the first's deliberate loss. Put the four-count inside her own attempt

and first-to-fourth forecast disappear.

### Chapter 25, scenes 1–2 — service and the hall's adjustment
