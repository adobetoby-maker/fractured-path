## Ch21 — The Landing

1. **Lira (cutaway), the three carters.** Way in: the second carter's question back to her ("You buying a wagon or selling one?") — then fold back to how she set it up. Her rule from the floor: never go to the same corner three times; a carter remembers a girl who asks the same thing twice. Three errands that are real (Rooke's crate of frame springs off the river barge; the Stone's letters to the river post; a parcel of tape). The grey coats at the landing look past her — she's the program's Wind with tape on her wrists, not the subject; she prices that and feels insulted and glad.
   - Carter one (day 58): old, mule team, freight. Subject: manifests. Every chartered station stamps a manifest; past the last one nobody stamps anything, "and you don't know you've crossed till you look for the stamp and there isn't one". He goes past.
   - Carter two (day 61): a woman with a covered wagon, wool west and salt back. Subject: days and fords. Four days to the last station loaded, three if light; the fords; the woman's verdict on what's past it: nobody asks your tier because there's nobody with a book. She goes past.
   - Carter three (day 64): young, quiet, a new wagon. Subject: the Line itself — where it is. He tells her exactly where the last station stands. He turns at it. Asked why, he looks at his horse's ears and says he turns there; won't say more. She doesn't push; she enters it.
   - Lira on the bluff road that evening adding it up alone; what she wants (her own feet out of a room) beside what she has (Rooke's card, the Stone, a ladder). She writes the note on the board in the morning — numbers not names.
2. **Cael, day 66.** He reads the board at the covered walk; knows the five is Seln. At the wing's counter Seln, filing, gives him the inventory in his ferryman economy: the five drawers are full and shut. Cael doesn't ask who told him.
3. **Day 68, the small room.** Way in: Brom coming down the residence passage with the common room's one good chair on his shoulder (humor: "It's for the council." He sits in it). The room: an old farm store under the residence — one door, a shuttered slit on a wall six feet off, a drain in the corner, a table that came in before the door was narrowed; voices die in it. Seating: Seln on the bench's middle with nothing in front of him; Karis's papers face-down at the far end; one lamp. Cael counts.
4. Lira asks: why are we still answering? [P-sense; own words] Then the carters as a sum (brief, since we've seen them): the Line is where the last station stands; manifests stop being stamped; two go past; the third won't.
5. Karis answers standing. Way in: she lays one copied sheet face up — a default ruling, two paragraphs — and reads the second paragraph first: the respondent's own letter quoted as evidence. Then who he was: a coastal guild practitioner, an integrity matter, winning, who wrote a good letter saying he'd lost faith in the process, and stopped attending; the registry thanked him and ruled by default, citing him. Then the registry's word: *fled*. Then her list of times text held (in reverse: Norhold's clause, Halcenvane's true book, Greyvane's hearing, Vell's ledger at Ardenmere, Denvash where there was no text). Her price: leaving is a move the machine has a form for. Seln's one correction, eyes shut: forty days after the fourth sitting closes, not the next sitting; the code counts days. Karis corrects the notebook.

2. Cael argues the first pole at full strength. Way in: Karis says "You're holding one back" before he's finished, and he starts again from the bottom: Denvash — a woman with a brass plate, eleven seconds, no text (Pellin as a woman per Book 1); then forward: Vell's ledger, the Greyvane hearing and the clause, the grant and two renewals on paper, an inspection that found nothing, a tournament their own clause put him in, an instrument whose keeper wrote that no number goes there. 4. Lira: her Fenmark form — correct, stamped, filed by somebody kind, and it opened under her anyway. [P] "It's not ground. It's a floor." Floors stand on something; she can't see what. Check what it stands on. *Both roads.* Cael agrees to that and concedes nothing; the room hears it.

6. Tea. Brom makes it; twenty minutes; nobody leaves — that's the finding. Lira works her landing-leg hip. Karis: on the record, we decided nothing. Lira [P] "It was the night we asked." Karis writes it. Cael's log line about Seln: a bill, not an argument; a question mark.
7. **Seln (cutaway), the dark wing.** Way in: the side door key that sticks; no lamp; the fire-watch lantern under the north arch puts a square on the counter.
