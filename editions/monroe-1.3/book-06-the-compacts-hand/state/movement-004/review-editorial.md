## Continuity, story, and canon audit

### Concrete finding: Lira's burst arithmetic breaks

The bout establishes burst one in exchange one (`chapter-24.md:41`), then identifies the east-rope attack as her third (`chapter-24.md:91`). In exchange three, however, she explicitly “burst[s] off” the crossways boards (`chapter-24.md:117–119`), then “burst[s] a fourth time” and “a fifth time” to escape (`chapter-24.md:125`). That puts her on six, while Brom and the narration immediately say she is on five (`chapter-24.md:135–139`), and exchange four calls the failed attempt her sixth (`chapter-24.md:157–161`).

This is not a cosmetic ordinal. The five-burst floor budget, the sixth borrowed against tomorrow, and the resulting half-stride loss are the causal engine of the bout. The cleanest repair is to make the crossways-board launch at `117–119` an ordinary push or drive rather than a Wind burst. That preserves bursts four and five as the two-part escape and leaves the final attempt correctly sixth. The same-author repair should confirm that the revised physical action still plausibly reaches the finished structure.

### Cross-movement calendar conflict

The M4 manuscript's internal clock is unusually exact and internally coherent:

- The council is sixty days after the day-eight courier and a fortnight after the day-54 preliminary (`chapter-21.md:191`), placing it on day 68, a Fifth-day.
- Brom's still-sealed letter arrived twenty-three days earlier (`chapter-22.md:55`), matching the day-45 Velmere letter.
- Lira will be on the strange floor in three days (`chapter-22.md:131`); the party leaves on Sixth-day (`chapter-23.md:3`), travels two days, and the rescheduled meet falls on day 71.
- The following fourth sitting and Sixth-day gate visit then follow cleanly.

The closed M3 ledger nevertheless says twice that Lira's meet is day 63. That entry does not accommodate M4's on-page eight-day postponement (`chapter-21.md:95,151`; `chapter-23.md:177`). This is a verified project-evidence conflict, but the prose itself does not need calendar surgery if day 71 is accepted: the inherited M3 ledger/state line should be corrected and the M4 state report should record the authoritative chain. If day 63 is meant to remain binding, several M4 intervals would need coordinated revision, not a single line fix.

## Repair brief

### 1. Correct the Wind-burst ledger in the Silver bout

- **Location:** `chapter-24.md:117–139` and `157–161`.
- **Observed issue:** The crossways-board attack is called a burst, after which two more escape bursts are numbered fourth and fifth; the text then says Lira has spent only five and calls the next attempt sixth.
- **Effect on the reader:** The central progression cost becomes impossible to count, weakening the causal reason she finishes half a stride short.
- **Proposed scope:** Make the launch at `117–119` an ordinary push/drive rather than a Wind burst, or otherwise revise the smallest possible action unit so that she has spent exactly five before exchange four. Recheck every ordinal, Brom's count, Rooke's bill, and the “borrowed against tomorrow” consequence. Do not shorten the fight.
- **Strength to preserve:** The false quarter, the two-stage escape from a finished course, the Silver's adaptation, the clean E3, and the earned half-stride loss.
