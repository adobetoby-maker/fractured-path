### 4.2 Continuity errors against supplied canon (must fix — all line-level)

1. **Hesk's clock contradicts closed Book 5 three times.** ch1:7: "Hesk's travelling clock sat at his elbow, an hour fast, as it had been since the day it came out of its pine case. He had never set it right. It was easier to subtract an hour than to explain to Hesk why he had changed it." Also ch5:215 and ch7:237, "gave it its minute by Hesk's clock, an hour fast". Book 5's motif is the opposite on both counts: the clock "had never once told the right time in all the years he had owned it" because it loses (ch53:239; ch33:5 "it lost a little more of the hour every day besides"); Cael himself sets it an hour fast in tournament weeks (ch13:109 "ran an hour fast in tournament weeks because Cael set it so"; ch27:293–295; Hesk's letter ch26:77 "Set it an hour fast as you always do and you will be right by accident"); and on finals morning he put it back and "For once it was right, and he had made it right himself" (ch54:5). So "never set it right" and "an hour fast since the pine case" are both wrong, and at Halcenvane on an ordinary night it is not an hour fast. Scope: one sentence in ch1 and two tags. Keep the callback that is canon — "a full minute by Hesk's clock" (ch60:33) — and make the clock wrong "in the usual direction" and honest about minutes (ch33:5), which is exactly the joke the author wants.
2. **The Storm notice was first read on the stone, not in a healers' corridor.** ch1:89: "He had read those words in a healers' corridor with his left side strapped from shoulder to hip"; ch4:209: "he had read each of them in a corridor or on a stair with nothing else to look at, and the one from Norhold most of all." Book 5 ch56:189–204: the notice arrived while he was on one knee on the floor with the healer's fingers in his left hand, in front of eight thousand, and "He read it through twice"; the strapping came afterward in the healers' corridor (ch57:181). One clause each ("on one knee on the stone, with a healer's fingers in his hand"; "where each of them found him — a yard, a stair, a shut room, a stone floor").
3. **Ephram's captaincy "from tomorrow".** ch1:25: "Ephram: cohort captain from tomorrow"; ch1:181: "He was carrying the captain's file now, thick and new". Book 5 ch28:53 has him "a Blade who had become a captain this year without noticing", and the reconciled B6 entry ledger carries him as cohort captain at entry (C1). One clause: the year's file is new, the office is not (captain again of the whole cohort; or simply "the captain's file for the new year").
4. **Where Seln's arithmetic was said.** ch3:103: "after Seln told him across a counter that his figures were too tidy." Book 5 ch13:93–97 has it said at the document table by the arch of the mill-town hall, in the one empty hour, to the file. One phrase ("across a document table").
