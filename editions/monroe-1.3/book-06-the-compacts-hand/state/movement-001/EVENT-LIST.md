# M1 working event list (author's own notes; drafted from this, not from the source page)

Calendar: day 0 = the night before the year opens. Day 1 First-day (convocation; the instrument is dated this morning). Day 5 Fifth-day (Gault signs; wing-three bout). Day 7 Seventh-day (rest). Day 8 First-day: served. Answer due fourteen days from service = the twenty-second day. No season/month words; weather only (warm, dry, dust).

Ch1 The Interval
- Night 0 at the window, Hesk's book and steel pen, Hesk's clock an hour fast. Short interval log: two months since the ferry; the old volume full; the house half empty then full tonight; the five people (Lira's program opened in the recess; Brom's summons still in the coat, "the recess's problem" and the recess is over; Karis's room has a lock now; Seln at the copying table and the middle of the bench; Ephram captain); the coats two-and-two, older grade, the two faces from the bridge gone. Vastin said a sitting would come after the houses went home; nothing up the road yet. Inventory: seven, Storm's flag still up ("nine of ten" now), one anomaly uncounted. Seventeen.
- Dawn day 1: can't sleep; the second floor's boards before light. The suite in order, each with its limit and bill: Wind (five free on boards; landing beat; hip), the read (floor joists, the building's loads), the push on the post (right shoulder, four blows a sitting public, five at Norhold), the give (the heavy bag swung into the forearm), the spark (copper basin, Karis's standing leave), the lane (fifteen feet, a third deep; ears; dust runs). Then the Wind again after the lane: clean. Nothing pulls. No notice — notices only come for beginnings.
- Relief entered as relief. The costed rule recalled: now Storm can be priced. Drift in the gap (Shadow), held by attending to the fire-watch boy. Log line.
- Convocation: seat fourth row north, pillar. The intake watches the delegation. Ephram's rota joke (the second-years' rota and the Fifth-day stamp). Withrow: three minutes of business; Rooke's program chartered as a standing track and she waits out the applause; "a number to defend". Cael logs load-bearing calm.
- After: Bracken at the lecture range's east window looking at the road. Karis: "Twice this morning." Cael files the look.

Ch2 Heavy
- Day 2 dawn: east hall, Rooke's Silver floor. Painted lines, frames rigged for Silver load, the rating table and the drill-book. The river-academy instructor (Stone, newly Silver; lost to her at the wool town; asked for the posting). Two third-years incl. a Force advanced to Rank Seven at the last sitting (plant M2). Lira wrong, wrong, right, right fast. Three bursts a bout by Rooke's rule; five free on boards a day. Rooke's one word.
- Water break: "It's heavier than it looks from the rail. Don't write that down." He doesn't. Ladder by rating at an advancement evaluation; Rank One the foot. Left arm carried close from habit. Rooke gave her the east hall key for the dawn drills (plant: private floor).
- Day 2 afternoon: Karis in the doorway of the top-floor reading room. The card; new lock fitted over the recess; key on a cord; budget line; the second-year assistant corrected once a morning; "the first one I've ever wanted to stay in"; suspicious of the wanting. The tournament charter on the shelf, provisions unopened since the road; the slip; "A note for the cold term."
- Day 3: the wing's counter. Queue; new green form in the seventh pigeonhole; desk clerk at his ledger; Seln rises from the copying table; the case on the shelf above it. His one counter sentence: senior men; keep the documentation current. "Thank you for the form." "Mm."
- Evening day 3: the north wall at the change of the bell; the coats counted; older; men who already know what they wait for. Log.

Ch3 The Band
- Day 4 supper, refectory: Seln at the middle of the bench; second-years' rota at the next table. Bracken brings Brom's docket himself (convenes the panel on paper inside the ninety; hearing the year's last Fifth-day at the district seat). "It is a formality… Your family's ledger closes." Brom folds it into the pocket with the summons and the Velmere letters. Lira at the rail after.
- Day 5: wing three placed. Gault and two instructors with cards; Ephram at the rail with his chart. The partner: a Shield, Iron Rank Five. Pre-bout costing: the rotation's line for today (concede an exchange when the light's behind me).
- The bout, four exchanges: band at the Halcenvane rate (bursts within six-on-oak rate; two contacts; give shut; push flat). E1 his touch? E2 conceded with the east light behind him; E3 the moment the honest suite would end it, declined, paid in a bruise; E4 his touch. Two to one. Figure posted.
- Ephram's chart: a year; never past twenty-seven; it breathes. "Norhold had no number for you." Ephram doesn't ask. Wants the true measurement someday; keeps charting.

Ch4 The Honest Floor
- Day 6 night: Lira's key; east hall; gallery shutters; two lanterns; Brom; Karis reading (will take figures at breakfast).
- Lira and Cael: Wind past the board budget, the sixth and seventh bills. Brom and Cael: the give drinks; the push at fighting load moves Brom's plant.
- Storm's first full load since it settled: lane between them; the read at Gold does air or body, not both — reads the lane, loses Brom, is caught across the chest. Correction: lay the lane first, then read. Second try works. Cost: heavy behind the eyes; everything slow for a count of ten.
- Rest: a drift; Lira "One"; held by watching Brom unwrap. Shadow sealed for a debt unspoken. What the band hides. Brom's line.
- Day 7: rest day. North wall. Bracken at the records hall's upper window with a glass; seven days. One line of talk.

Ch5 Served
- Day 8 second bell: the covered walk; the escort on the switchbacks; Bracken already watching. Lodge-man stands; escort rides through to the records hall without dismounting.
- Records hall: courier in plain grey with a seal; protocol card; joint service; Withrow at her ordinary pace; signatures; the Compact's seal; she reads standing; first page aloud. "Given under the Compact's hand." "I have signed that line myself. Nine hundred times." Copies tonight; "Go and tell Karis."
- Karis's room: she reads it three ways; her one sentence of feeling; sends the assistant for the code (three volumes), the Greyvane transcript, the four prior rulings from Bracken's locked press; tea.
- The bluff learns: Lira off the Silver floor; Brom with bread; Seln's form for the records hall, night, stamped before anyone asked; Withrow's one-line answer posted.
- Afternoon at the window: "They filed…" entry.

Ch6 The Records Hall
- Night: the long table; Bracken's three volumes; the house's counsel (not the inspection's); Karis; Cael at the end; assistant sent to bed; lamps entered late in the fire-watch's oil book by Bracken; Withrow coming and going.
- Withrow's registry copy of chapter fourteen; two underlinings, two inks; "At its discretion"; no deadline in the chapter: Bracken counts none.
- Cael's tallies of the instrument.
- The counsel reads up from the remedy; four sentences of recital; four prior challenges from the press; first finding (Karis): no fraud; second (counsel): the clause conceded.
- Third: Vastin's advisory cited with approval. First time they see its text. Karis afraid of a citation. Cael on official channels.

Ch7 Corridor
- The integrity provision aimed at a person; the remedy; the counsel's warning to Cael; "I'm counting."
- Chapter fourteen in Bracken's flattest voice (three steps; asset-restriction); "in thirteen years". Withrow's working definition: "It's expulsion that follows you." The two she administered.
- Bracken's arithmetic: dated the first day; served the eighth; due the twenty-second; a late answer is no answer.
- Karis's architecture in order; "It's not a question. It's a corridor…"; "We answer it anyway." The stratum note sideways. Question mark.
- The residence awake for her: Lira on the hearth with the drill-book, Brom in the good chair, the wing window lit (Seln). Karis counts the room, gives orders, goes up. Cael tells it. "Corridor." "What if the people didn't fail this time?" The first pole at the window; the records hall lamps out in pairs; his copy on the sill, first page up, waiting for better light.
