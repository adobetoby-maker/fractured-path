### 4.2 Continuity errors against supplied canon (must fix — all line-level)

**1. The first renewal's room and minute contradict Book 4's "Renewed" (ch8:143–151).**

Book 4 ch54–55 put the semester evaluation on the oak under instruments for about three hours: frames, the board, the pendulum, the read, and the copper pot. The east wall held four chairs: the delegation's counsel, Ilsev, Havel, and in the fourth the Archmarshal, "with nothing in them… no folder, no case, no pencil" (ch54:79–87). Afterward he wrote twice in a small notebook (ch55:162, 198). Gault read the note at the trestle, the Ash instructor signed, the Mire instructor added four lines, and "The clerk pressed the stamp down. Dull. Exact. Stamped." (ch55:173–190). Bracken entered the line two days later (ch57:43). Cael's own ledger for the day is on Book 4's page whole ("*Ledger, day twenty-two. The twentieth of Reaping.*… *Paper says: five up, one level, every trial inside tolerance. Renewed.*").

Ch8 has five things wrong against that:
- **"three cards" (ch8:151).** That is wing three's house format from M1, not the semester evaluation.
- **The room.** "Gault at the trestle with his case, the two instructors, the delegation's officer at the end of the row with a pen he never once uncapped" (ch8:143). This is a boy who remembers rooms "by its count", yet the memory misses the counsel, Ilsev and Havel and demotes the Archmarshal to "the delegation's officer". The pen is wrong as well.
- **"held for countersignature… before it was sealed".** Book 4 has it stamped at the trestle.
- **A Log line Book 4's entry does not contain** ("*Renewal sat. Gault, Ash, Mire, three cards, found. Minute held for the delegation's officer.*").

The nine days themselves are the source's event (source ch3: the minute "held… open for the visiting officer's signature") and the packet's beat, and they can stay. The fix is to re-anchor them:
- The house's stamp went on at the trestle, as Book 4 has it.
- What waited was the registry's file copy. The delegation's counsel asked that it go up with the delegation's countersignature before the registry sealed it; she is the one who proposed one of the delegation's people in writing for the interview (B4 ch56:23).
- It came back sealed nine days later, and Cael initialled its receipt in Gault's office (the 29th of Reaping falls inside Book 4's term, ch62:5).
- The memory recounts the four chairs and the empty-handed fourth.
- Two short Log lines are placed so that they can sit beside Book 4's long entry.
- The table's sources (ch8:177) stand as written: the house book for the sittings and the registry file for the seals.

Scope: four to six sentences.

**2. Fenmark to Vell's yard "three streets on" (ch9:229).**

"I walked out of the front… Three streets on, I heard a noise like somebody hitting a boiler with a chain… It was Vell's yard." Fenmark Academy is another town. Book 1 has Lira "ever since she came to Ardenmere" (ch38:61), four months in Ardenmere when she meets Cael (ch9:79), with four losses at Vell's before she learned (ch10:303). Book 2 has the year between: "I asked six of them, the year after. I walked to two of the halls" (ch10:31; ch11:229). The packet's rule is "Lira's Fenmark: as canon gives it."

Keep "Nobody sent me there. It wasn't on any form" and "round the back of everything". Move the hearing of the noise to Ardenmere, after the guild halls ("A year on, in another town, with six guild doors shut behind me…"). Scope: two sentences.

**3. The lane's third bill is measured from the wrong zero and named in the wrong unit (ch11:177–193, 215).**

M1's unit is "ten counts of everything slow… At eleven it was gone", recorded as "ten" (M1 ch4:193; Lira's palm ch4:269). Ch11 has the third lane late at eleven and on time at twelve. By M1's unit that is eleven, but it is recorded as "twelve" four times (ch11:187, 193, 213).

The count is also measured from the wrong zero. Cael "lost the count" at "somewhere about six" (ch11:177). Lira then crosses the floor and "began to count aloud… one number for each of her own breaths" (ch11:181), apparently from one. Her eleven is therefore not counted from the lane, and the true bill is longer than anything the page records.

This is the number the author asks the owner to bless for M6–M7 (flag 2), so it has to be measured right. The minimal fix:
- Karis, who came "to take the figures as they happened" (ch11:133), has been counting from the lane and gives the numbers; or Lira picks the count up where his lips stopped.
- Make it slow through twelve and clean at thirteen, so that every "twelve" stands.

Scope: two or three sentences.

**4. "A week ago" twice, and "the night it settled" (ch11:155, 211, 209).**

The honest floor where he learned to take the read off the lane, and where Brom said "So you don't forget it's yours", was day 6 (M1 ledger; M1 ch4:179, 259). Ch11 is day 27, which is three weeks. The Storm settled at dawn on day 1 (M1 ledger; ch1). Make these "three weeks ago" (twice) and "since the morning it settled".

**5. Karis's six slips are in the back of her notebook (M1 ch7:129 "tucked them into the back of her notebook"; M1 ledger).**

M2 puts them in the front matter three times (ch11:95 "beside the six slips"; ch13:23 "under the six slips and the two dockets"; ch13:93 "take my six slips off the front of my notebook"). The front matter is where the stratum note lives (M1 r1). Either say "back" in three places, or add one clause in ch11 saying she moved them to the front on purpose. The second is more interesting.

**6. Havel at Greyvane: where he sat, and how many looks (ch13:167, 223).**

I could not confirm Greyvane's two looks in Book 3 by grep. They are the author's, and they are compatible once the place is right.

**7. Ardenmere was not where the name was "stamped on him" (ch8:89).**

The author report says that "the house calls the pair 'the annex'". That sentence is not on the page. M5 ("the annex chosen") and M6 ("Three routes; the annex") hang scenes on the word. One clause belongs where the lumber room is cleared (ch8:25–29). See §4.3 item 7.
