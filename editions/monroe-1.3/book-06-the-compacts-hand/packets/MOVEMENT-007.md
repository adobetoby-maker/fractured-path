## What this movement is for

Make the reader live inside a corridor the characters know is maintained — winning arguments they know do not matter, because leaving early would hand the machine its confession — and price each stake slowly so the walk-out is earned rather than announced. A long stretch compressed into episodes [B5-reconciled 2026-10-05: "A season" struck; audit A1]; log-voice skips are welcome.
