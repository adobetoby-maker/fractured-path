## Where we enter

The morning after the last bout, four days before the academic year's close [B5-reconciled 2026-10-05: season word struck; audit A1]. Four withdrawal forms squared on Bracken's counter. Seln has nothing to file.

## What this movement is for

The walk-out in its exact images, and its discipline is smallness. Then the empty desk, the machine's paperwork closing on air, four days of road on which the apparatus thins to nothing, the unmarked Line, and the first fire no Arbiter has measured. A deliberately quiet movement: its physical challenge is distance — the ford, the ridge with a blowing horse, scree switchbacks, a camp made from nothing.

Consequential turns:
- **Bracken's counter**: four leaves in the order they present (Lira, Brom, Cael; Seln absent from the sequence); *freely… and not by any officer of this institution*, longhand in the margin; **Karis's forfeiture read line by line** — certification retained ("they can only decline to count it"), the restricted-collection half-second, the key into its pigeonhole; Bracken off procedure; **Gault's last note**. The assistant's three things. Cael's room counted out.

All four."**; **Rooke: "Doors open both ways."**; the watchers log it; the bluff road at dusk. "Four, closed complete. Tomorrow, the fifth."
- **Not At His Desk** — *Seln simply wasn't at his desk the next morning.* The morning clerk's window; keys in a row; the summaries pinned eleven years later; the personnel query; **the apparatus window** — *what did he have*; fifteen years of true, empty product requisitioned. **Seln's window** (~3k): the east road in the dark; the last null report drafted and not filed; the case's history; looking back, badly; firewood for people who will be cold.
