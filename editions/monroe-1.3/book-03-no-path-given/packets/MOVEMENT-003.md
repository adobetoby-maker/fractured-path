## What this movement is for

The observer fights in public for the first time, wins at a chosen size, and gains a witness instead of a friend; then learns, from the outside reading of a careful mind, that the thing in him might have a handle — and has to decide what that makes him.

Consequential turns:
- The council: Naveth can neither dismiss nor grant; Quenna converts the petition into a public exhibition logged as the month's assessment; the stakes named honestly; Naveth's three audiences (students, panel, a file read later by people not in the room); "Winning at a chosen size."
- The academy treats it like sport, not a verdict.
- Six days on Edran: Glass Path's discrete commitments; the recommitment window between second and third declarations is the shape of the discipline, not his mistake ("Patience, not correction"); Lira: "You're always sure by the fourth day."
- **The exhibition** (2,500–3,000): four exchanges; the forearm graze that confirms what watching said for free; the closing in the seam; Edran asks for a fourth exchange and narrows the gap mid-fight; a rib graze; closed cleaner.
