## What this movement is for

Deliberately the quietest movement: a documents war. The adversary's shape fixed; a buried word found, copied and kept unused; and the night before, when each person who loves him gives him one true thing. Physical anchors: the deposition fought as a duel of answers, Prynn's clause-by-clause defense, Lira's dusk spar.

Consequential turns:
- The delegation a week ahead: Yorlan (procedure embodied; two questions); Ilsev (from B1's senior evaluation; famous in the digests for ruling against her own side when its paper fails); Havel, promoted one grade, recording officer. "Institutions re-use people."
