### The Glass rule as now stated (ch18, once, then obeyed in ch19 and ch21)

1. **A structure forms whole.** Each shell or edge is one discrete declaration. It cannot be reshaped once it stands, and it ends by bursting.
2. **At most two at once, one per forearm.** Edran enters every sequence with a **ready pair** built at his mark before he moves: a shell on the left forearm, an edge on the right. Each takes his whole attention.
3. **No full structure while another stands.** He cannot build a full structure while another still stands, because building one takes the whole of a Glass fighter's mind.
4. **The sliver.** The third structure of any sequence must be made cold, after the second bursts. In that interval both forearms are bare. That interval is the recommitment window, and every Glass fighter carries it.
5. **Edran's fix (exchange 4, and his rebuild in ch21).** He starts the third on the free left arm *while the second still stands*, with half his mind. The gap narrows, but the third comes up thin.

How the exchanges obey it:
- **Exchange 1:** the ready pair is up. The shell is spent on Cael's guard at the sixth strike, and Edran presses with the edge alone. The shin-shell is removed.
- **Exchange 2 (Lira's cutaway):** a fresh pair. The shell is spent short and cramped, then the edge bursts, and the sliver leaves both arms bare. The third forms cold on the right and grazes Cael. Karis's chart line becomes "eyes on petitioner's right forearm, bare, in the interval between second and third structures".
- **Exchange 3:** the ready pair, then the sliver with both forearms bare, then the palm on the breastbone.
- **Exchange 4:** the third is called on the left while the edge still holds. The ch19 sentence "Glass could not form on a forearm that still had glitter on it" is gone. The explanation is now ch18's: "nobody had a whole mind to spare while another structure still stood… had decided to pay for it."
