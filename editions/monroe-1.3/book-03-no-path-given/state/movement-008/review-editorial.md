## 1. Reader response (unscored)

**Where interest caught.** The first bell: Lira sliding the card out from under Karis's fingers "the way she would have taken a heel of bread off a market stall," and Brom's whole contribution being to say nothing clever (ch54 s1). Cael counting the seven strangers and reading the woman in dark blue by where she chose to sit (ch54 s2). Naveth taking his spectacles off in the chalk square (ch56 s2). The whole of the schedule reading from Havel's desk, and the moment Havel realises what he is making: a roll of every word the Compact has for what a person can be, with one word that will never be on it, and no mark in a recorder's craft for an absence (ch57 s4). Ilsev's two questions and Coss's answer (ch58 s3). The sampler of crooked birds and the struck line to Coss's daughter (ch59 s4). Prynn's hands stopping over box seven, and the width of one volume left open in the run (ch61 s4).

**Where it slipped.** Ch54 s3 is a second room-portrait after s2 has already done the room; a reader who has just been walked twenty paces across the floor is walked across it again by the furniture. Ch60 s3 (the correspondence, Naveth's eleven letters, Wray and Edran glimpsed) is told, not shown, and reads as a recap. Ch61 s3 (the Vell letter) narrates a letter being composed rather than giving it; the one copied line carries it, but the paragraph before is a summary. Ch56 s3, after the pivot, follows a sequence of images the reader of the current edition will find familiar (see §6).

**Which person matters.** Coss. He is the best-drawn figure in the movement: the masonry of the first sitting, the stipulation tried "as calmly as he would have taken the weather," "There isn't one," the five minutes, the report written into silence, the daughter's letter. Prynn second: the slip on the empty place, her own copy of the code carried in like something that might break, the gap left on the shelf. Among the four, Lira's recess line ("I didn't know that sitting on a bench while the other man carries out his half would feel like this. Nobody told me. I'd like to have been told.") is the truest thing anyone says.

**Which event I expect next.** The watchers follow them down the hill; somebody above Coss hurries an amendment; Karis finds the positive basis she has only the shape of. The movement plants all three cleanly and promises none of them early.

**Would I continue.** Yes.

**Thirteen-year-old lens.** The hearing holds because it is staged as a fight: fingers folded under the table for a scorecard, "He fights like you," "Someone who commits later," the stipulation as the last door tried. The "help me find it in the schedule" device, Oona mouthing the names, and nine/six/eleven/four make the law legible without any law being explained. Likely to lose the thread briefly in Coss's rebuttal (ch58 s2) and in the "silence as consent" exchange, which is the one place the argument is abstract rather than enacted; likely to skim ch60 s3. Will want the Ember session (ch60 s6) and gets it: three sizes, the same cold every time, "It doesn't care." The wall at dusk lands.

**Adult genre reader lens.** A courtroom set piece that respects its own procedure and never cheats: the respondent concedes everything, the panel declines a stipulation in favour of a demonstration, the senior seat asks the only question that matters and is answered honestly. The honourable antagonist is the movement's achievement. The two coda chapters (ch60–61, about 10,400 words together) carry a lot of closing-out, and the reader will feel the term being tidied; most of it is earned (the page returned, the slip paid to Vell, the gap), some of it is inventory (the letters, the calendar board). The faceless coda is exactly as featureless as it should be.

## 4. Rulings on the flagged items

**Karis asks to read the Power Log (ch60 s5). KEEP.** Book 4 ch1 line 293 requires an asking that has already happened ("that had been the arrangement since the day she'd asked for it"), and P5's "three people who know all of it" is only true at the close of this book if the asking is on the page. **Who is examined at the first sitting. ACCEPT as staged; ledger it as procedure canon.** The M7 witness list says the challenge enters no witnesses because it "rests on documents."
