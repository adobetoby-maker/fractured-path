## 4. Continuity, canon, physical state, power limits, knowledge boundaries, reserved disclosures

### 4.1 Verified canon violations or contradictions (must repair)

**C1 — Karis's research timeline contradicts itself and the series calendar (ch9).**
- "One afternoon in late winter" Marlowe tells the joke. "It took her three weeks to find a real thread and four months to stop finding contradictions." Then: "she spent a winter killing the entries one at a time." Then: "The last ledger copy arrived at the start of the summer and included one more result. An Iron Rank Eight… beaten in front of something like six hundred people." Then the digest scene: "the same gap she had been staring at all winter." Ch11 (Karis at the table): "since a lecturer told a joke in a hall at Ternhall in the middle of last winter."
- Internal: late winter + three weeks + four months is early summer; a second winter cannot follow it; "middle of last winter" and "late winter" disagree.
- Canon: the Iron Rank Eight is Reydan, and the Reydan bout is Book 2's climax. BOOK_MAP §1: "Book 3 opens on the third morning of the road east from Ardenmere… No time skip… early autumn"; §12.6: the Reydan fight "ended just before this book." M1's ledger has Compression "days old" at arrival. Coss's own file in ch13 calls it "a bout fought only a few weeks ago," and the carter says "End of the season." A ledger copy carrying that result cannot have reached Ternhall "at the start of the summer."
- Effect: the one place a careful reader can lose trust in Karis as a measurer is her own dates.
- Fix is four phrases: make the Marlowe lecture one season (pick "late winter" or "midwinter" and use it in ch9 and ch11); "spent a winter killing" → "spent the spring and summer killing"; "arrived at the start of the summer" → "arrived in the last days of the summer, a few days before the digest" (or restructure so the digest and the last ledger copy arrive in the same week); "staring at all winter" → "staring at since the winter." Keep "eight months" (ch10) — late winter to early autumn fits it.

**C2 — Karis speaks of the closed sittings as if she had seen them (ch16, line 51).**
- "That's the page I wrote at your first session, when you were running the framework. I timed how far ahead of Hobb's strikes you moved, at the sittings, from what you told the panel and what I could see from the stool."
- Karis was not at either sitting; ch14 states it flatly ("The sitting was closed, and nothing she could have written about it was anything she had been allowed to see"), and in the first session Cael ran the framework "against the empty air" (ch12) — there were no strikes to time, and Hobb was not in section four. She also cannot know "what you told the panel"; Cael told her only "Satisfied."
- This is a knowledge-boundary breach inside the movement's closing scientific plant ("It fits something"), which M3 will build on.
- Fix: one sentence. Let her have timed the framework's lead off Cael's own set-ups in session one (he supplied his own strikes out of the air at the working pass; she could time the interval between the moment his shoulders and eyes commit to where a strike would be and the moment the step begins), and let her say so. Remove "at the sittings" and "from what you told the panel." The finding (same quarter-breath margin as the calls on Brom) survives intact.
