## Chapters

**37 — An Educational Period.**
- The movement opens on the knock tally inked on Cael's hand: nine of twenty-two, worse than two in five.
- Brom notices the knocks that answer come with Cael's foot down. He also notices they come when he has *meant* it. Cael sees he has been carrying two halves of one thing: the gaze knows *when*, the knock knows *where*.
- Dace tells him Keth has named him for the empty square, with time to decide and an invitation to the dawn ring ("Bring nothing").
- Dace mentions a coast courier asking for the season card, the first in four years.
- In the ring Cael is a newcomer with an ash stick. He sees from a forearm off that Keth slows every part of the chain but the join, because he cannot see it.
- He keeps the read shut (a drill is not a bout; no leave).
- Keth gives the tall lad "an educational period" (his own eleven-loss second year) and crouches by the fallen girl.
- On the bench Keth says what he wanted: a floor is the most public place in the world; what a man can see from a bench is his. He asked for Cael to see what three months of watching bought. "Tell me first."
- Lira on the landing: "You like him." / "Good," because you fight the fight, not the man, but "that part you just pay."
- In the grey book, under *the finger*, Cael writes *I like him.* once, turns the leaf face down, and never writes it again. Realization: liking a man does not change the page, it changes what the page costs.

**38 — Second Into Third.**
- Cael tells Lira and Brom the same day that he has a seam on Keth. He will not say where, because Keth hears it first. He will test it on two Blades.
- The consent question is settled aloud. On a floor in a bout, the lean is the room's, like a man's feet. Going in is not. "The knock's only the skin."
- Lira picks the two Blades off Dace's wall.
- **Test one**, the barge lad, who chains threes and finishes short:
  - the join is there but a hair thin, so it is the chain's, not only Keth's;
  - the knock and the hip will not share ("knock, shut, then go"), at the cost of a forearm;
  - he wins in the third without entering the join;
  - two of five knocks answer;
  - afterward he gives the lad a habit for nothing.
- Alcove mornings with Brom and the dock partner (asked; "I've had worse on me than a boy's eyes") add the rule: knock at the meaning, the fourth hook, from a planted foot. The tally climbs six of fifteen, eight, nine of eighteen, eleven of twenty.
- **Test two**, Orvet's square woman, who learned on a bigger blade and finishes long:
  - the join is as wide as Keth's;
  - he refuses to go in when the knock gives smear;
  - he goes in when it answers, and wins in the fourth;
  - three of six knocks answer;
  - she asks, and he tells her it's the chain, not her wrist.
- At dawn he says yes to Keth first. Keth knew he had tested it, and it means he thinks it's real.
- Dace chalks the name and clears the main floor for "Thursday week". Two coast seats are wanted.

**39 — The Line at Iron** (bout, part one).
- Bout day: no breakfast, Lira's "lie down and don't." Brom sits on the bed: "I'm saying it so it's said."
- The main floor is cleared and lit with every lamp. Dace stops counting at 450. Faces: the coast pair, the academy-coat man, Keth's newcomers at the front.
- Cael's **first sight of the well-dressed woman** in grey at the back, with a closed small book.
- Dace at the arch: Keth has never asked for anybody in four years. Every lamp is lit so nobody can blame the light.
- The tall lad and the girl bring Keth's message ("he's had his breakfast…"). The girl: "Don't hurt him. Don't let him hurt you either."
- Vell keeps the pen herself and waves off the junior keeper.
- **Exchange one, the survey:**
  - Keth's chosen count blinds the gaze's clock;
  - the Wind fires with no reach, calm, charging nothing (no hip line, breath in its count) — *First time*, distinguished from the old Log's unasked flinches;
  - the join opens at full speed from four sides, always two paces off;
  - Keth takes the centre.
- **Exchange two, the argument:**
  - Keth comes in runs, asking whether the step is a thing or a trick;
  - the knocks give smear, then a clean answer and a step early;
  - one knock gives smear with Cael's hope painted on it, and he waits on it. Keth's short off-count cut opens a straw-width line above his left elbow;
  - Cael reads the short cut as a coat Keth does not value: the long finish is still under it.

**40 — Done** (bout, part two, and the record).
- **Exchange three, the temptation:**
  - Keth offers false openings (a slow return, a wide stance, a dropped guard);
  - Cael reads the countersign, a heel that comes up too quickly, with the real strike a breath late;
  - the gaze costs a "band of fire" and he is blind past ten degrees;
  - inside one gift the true join opens at two paces and more. The entry would be long and off the beat, so only the giving face could make a strike count, and off the beat it goes up the old left wrist; the knock does not reach. He declines. ("Don't go in on the eyes alone.")
  - Keth stops of his own accord: "You're waiting." / "Yes."
- **Exchange four:**
  - Keth all in. The faster he goes, the less he chooses; every second cut finishes long;
  - the Wind arrives unasked twice more, uncharged;
  - a fourth cut at the knee strips leather off his boot (feet, a hair slow);
  - at a pace the join opens and Cael's body goes in ahead of the knock's answer. The answer comes late (*set*), the hip carries him the last half body, and in the lock he sees the open shoulder;
  - a held-back strike to the front of the blade-arm shoulder joint. Keth goes down, rolls, lifts his left hand: "Done."
- **After:**
  - Keth gets up unaided and names "the end of my second" himself;
  - Cael explains the long finish and the join;
  - the protected line follows, then "Does that bother you?" / "No." / "It should." — because it goes both ways, and one of the 450 will have the patience. "And I'd guess there's something in you that you don't know is there either";
  - "Come find me sometime." He'd rather buy the rest of the book.
- **The one recording scene.** Vell writes the bout line, then fills Cael's empty narrow Path column for the first time: *Iron-equivalent. Cael. No Path designation.* Then "First time I've written that." / "Does it matter?" / "In this room? No." … "Outside? Different question."
- Lira's cloth on the arm ("I'm still allowed to mind").
- The grey-coat woman writes one short line, closes the book, and is gone.

**41 — A Face People Know.**

**42 — The Other Book.**
- **Bede** (Shield, Copper card, Iron-equivalent) comes to Cael at the market pump in the rain to tell him first: he has kept a tally-book on Cael since the season's first week, and he has asked Dace for him.
- Cael had him down as *the tally*, "a clerk doing his sums."
- Cael feels the fairness first (the mirror of the grey book), and that Bede was fairer for saying so.
- Lira: "I like him."
- Cael writes the bench's page on himself under a new mark, *the hip*, in eight lines. Lira adds the breath out through the nose. Brom asks only "Can you" change them.
- A week of trying the variations, each priced:
  - going first;
  - eyes off the shoulder (the knock does their work);
  - the strike moved off the ribs;
  - going right on his feet;
  - the fill moved early.
- **The bout (late card, ~200 watching):**
  - Cael goes first and lands a glancing touch, and the rim takes his forearm;
  - Bede walks to his own right to keep the rim on Cael's left (the fan) and beats the angle-denial footwork;
  - going right on his feet costs the thigh twice;
  - a read burst round the rim is met in the lock (*count two*) across the ribs;
  - the woman in grey writes only after things he has never done before;
  - exchange two: the knock (unseen by any bench) reads the rush, and the strike to the club wrist disarms Bede once. Bede learns to stop his rush halfway;
  - exchange three: walked into the corner, Cael must chain two. The early fill stays visible as raised shoulders. Bede ignores the first burst and stands where the second must land; the rim takes Cael across the chest; the club is held a finger above the collarbone; Cael lifts his hand.
- **After:**
  - Vell: the line doesn't move ("That's what an Iron does against an Iron"; "I don't write things twice");
  - Bede shows him the page, a mark of a single line, ending *Doesn't know about his shoulders. Knows about his chest.*, and asks to see his own someday;
  - Dace on the woman: "She's been here before." She comes only for Cael's bouts and writes when he does "something you've not done before." She gave a name at the door nobody knows;
  - "There'll be more like him."
- **Night.** Brom on the lean (a habit, fakeable at a beat) versus his recovery (the shape, guarded by throwing people far). The Log's two columns: *Habit (train it)* and *Shape (guard it)*. Realization: *I'm a page now.* *He came and told me first. Do that…*

**43 — Provisional.**
- Dace books **Maud** (Force, Bronze-equivalent, ropewalk forewoman, oldest name at Bronze on the wall) for Lira's second confirming bout. Lira chose a Bronze over a kind Iron.
- Brom: "She'll find your hands."
- Lira refuses Cael's offer of a page on Maud ("I want this one to be what I did") and asks him not to go into Maud or her, not even the knock.
- From the arch, unseen, Cael watches Brom drill her middle at dawn eleven times with a hand flat in her turn ("There." / "I know."). On the twelfth there is no middle. It is theirs; he leaves.
- **Lira cutaway:**

  - exchange one: eleven sends round a woman who never leaves her footprints;
  - exchange two: Maud makes the floor small; at the rope Lira must catch; the second blow comes into the turn across the backs of both hands;

  - exchange four: Maud thinks;
  - exchange five: a short push at the old height. Her hands go home to the square catch on their own, the staff goes, and the bar is held by her ear. Hand up;
  - Maud returns the staff: "Feet are past it… Your hands remember where they used to live… Takes a year. Took me two."
  - Vell keeps her **provisional** and says why to her face. A confirming bout is a question; twice she didn't let them, once she did. She has asked Dace not to book a third until the middle is gone;
  - Lira's reckoning in the dark alcove: the circuit measured her to the finger, truly, and cannot move the card. Only an Arbiter, and a guild that won't answer, can. She stops asking. She lights the lamp and begins.
- **Cael closing:**

  - through the door: no third bout till the middle's gone; "I'm done walking to their gate";
  - Hesk's reply (found under a carter's bill): a known face is a coat; mind the girl's hands;
  - Dace: a courier from two cities up the river asking for one line by name, and a man wanting to look at "the unranked boy's" alcove ("not a menagerie");
  - a mail cart goes out the river road;
  - Log: *They know my name two cities up the river. I didn't send it. It went.*
