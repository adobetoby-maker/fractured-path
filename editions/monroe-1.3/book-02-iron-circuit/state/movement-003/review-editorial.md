## Continuity, canon, and story findings

### Result

No verified canon contradiction was found. The movement fulfills the packet's required turns and ends at the correct pressure point. The only substantive editorial finding is pacing through repeated tactical recapitulation, not a state or rule error.

### Power rules and knowledge boundaries

- The chain of two requires a visible preload; the attempted third fails at a real cost. The movement does not silently expand the Wind-adjacent deployment range.
- The informed/reactive burst distinction is demonstrated twice—first under live pressure in ch19, then under controlled conditions in ch20—before Cael rules it true. This is good progression causality.
- Pressure's delivery face remains capped at three working uses and is locked away against Brom for a stated mechanical reason. Cael does not test the fourth past the safe side of the boundary.
- Brom reads Cael as uncategorizable—“a shelf that isn't there”—not as someone who integrates witnessed abilities. Cael withholds the fragments and Power Log. The Book 3 mechanism is not disclosed early.
- Coss sees an unsigned, above-grade marker and chooses not to query it. He does not learn its origin, the Level 4 language, the registry sub-layer, or any intention behind it, and he writes no note. This respects both the movement brief and the reveal schedule.
- The movement establishes Iron Skin through witnessed behavior, hearsay, and limited books. It does not grant Cael privileged guild knowledge.

- Brom's honesty is active rather than merely asserted: he declines to poison the latency data, pays newcomers, pays Lira's partner again, answers only to the edge of what he has actually observed, and lets Cael study him at a cost to his own bout advantage.
