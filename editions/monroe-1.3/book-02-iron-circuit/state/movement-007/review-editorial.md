### Findings

1. **The last-day countdown is one day late.** Ch45 establishes the announcement at first light Wednesday and the bout as “two weeks from yesterday. Tuesday fortnight.” The common-room plan occurs Wednesday evening; ch46 is therefore the first training morning on Thursday, and ch47 explicitly calls the following session “the second morning.” Counting from that first training morning makes ch51's eleventh, twelfth, and thirteenth days Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday. Yet the thirteenth day is repeatedly “the night before,” with Dace saying “tomorrow” and Brom saying the same. The bout was already fixed for Tuesday. This is a verified continuity error, not merely plan drift.
2. **Two line-level read-aloud defects occur in ch51.** Dace's paragraph ending “Go away. You're one more body, and I'm counting you.” lacks its closing quotation mark. The final Wind inventory has malformed emphasis in “The lock, two* ands*”; it should render as “two *ands*.” Both are small, but the edition is audio-first and the first can make Dace's speech appear to continue into narration.
3. **No other canon contradiction was found.** Reydan remains twenty-two, Iron-tier Rank 8, burst-compression Pressure; the scout remains unnamed and does not approach; the pre-bout fragment count is correct; Lira remains provisional; Cael is fifteen; the Ironyard remains stone-floored, low-ceilinged, and lamp-lit.
