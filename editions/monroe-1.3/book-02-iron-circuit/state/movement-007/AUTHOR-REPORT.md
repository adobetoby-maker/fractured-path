## Chapters

**44 — Standing Room.**
- The Tuesday evening, nearly three weeks after Maud. Cael holds a lamp to Lira's yellowing hands (the left second knuckle still "talks"). She tells him that his slow right is filed in the wrong column: the hip is a shape, the feet are only a habit. He pencils a question mark beside it.
- **Reydan cutaway:**
  - four coppers' standing room, paid in coppers so the door won't remember him;
  - a wool wagon down the river, a room above a saddler's;
  - the room judged first: the foundry stone, the lamps, the wall's grammar, a keeper who stands to write, a rule both fighters say aloud;
  - the river-academy coat on the east bench ("Usually it was him");
  - bout one, a Stone against a losing Blade, watched for her feet between exchanges (her back foot has stopped going forward);
  - bout two, a quick lad's half-step too deep and a hard fall, as informative as the clean finish;
  - his history: his father's paid yard at ten, the academy at eleven, a master turning his head from blades to feet, the city's two academies redrawing the invitational three autumns, then a withdrawal over a "wrist";
  - the wasted trips (a salt-town Copper, a river-port Blade);
  - the story from a guild hall in the spring (a Bronze R1 put down by a boy with no Path). This time he had the lamp-oil keeper in his city write down the river for the line, and Dace's copy came back: *Iron-equivalent. Cael. No Path designation.*;
  - with Dace: the request, "He's Iron-equivalent now", "I know that. I want to fight him anyway". He declines Dace's test (he won't ask where the boy trains), and the message ends on the protected line;
  - walking up the hill he can judge the room and the line, not the boy, and finds he is smiling.

**45 — The Alcove Wall.**
- Dace at the arch at first light: Reydan, up the river, Iron-tier rank eight, burst Pressure, twenty-two, academy; two weeks, main floor, a clear card till then.
- Dace owns the cause: his own copied line went up the river. The carters' word: a yard at ten, the academy at eleven, never the same way twice. Nobody has seen him fight within three hundred miles. "You could tell me no." / "I know." / "But you're not going to." / "No."
- **Dace's beat:** he writes up the river to ask that keeper for anything written, the first time in twenty years he has asked another keeper for anything.
- The names in the first volume against the rank (Feryn Bronze R2, Darrow Innes Bronze R1, Sarel Bronze R1 underlined, Talis Copper R4). He doesn't believe the arithmetic, and sees the method problem: a file is a record of what a man does again.
- Against the dock partner the weight hangs back in the heels for a half-beat, three times in thirty minutes. Logged under its own heading.
- The market: the pie woman's silence, carters' "Up the river, is it?", the paper-stall man's "feeling well, these days?" (a wager put politely), the betting man unable to price "a man nobody's seen".
- **Supper:** Lira's charcoal grid on a carter's bill ("No wasted mornings"; mornings and an hour after supper hers; middles Brom's); "Tell me what you're actually afraid of"; the eat line; Brom's washouts search; Brom's own paid yard at Velmere ("It makes you have fewer holes"); "it's not only about winning", it's what people decide you are; the heavyset man: "I'll be there."
- Night: the thinnest page he has ever opened on anybody; *Thirteen days.*

**46 — No Warning Beat.**
- **Lira's first morning:** no good morning, no beat; "Be moving when it comes" (what Maud taught her with the backs of her hands); his hip used reflexively is met in the lock ("Bede counted two"). Rule: the hip when you know; the feet when you're frightened. He keeps the knock shut (no leave, not asked).
- The dock partner, who went red, names a coast-house Bronze who trains at the cooper's and fought "your man". He won't take them, and would say no himself.
- **Session one (at rest):** Brom's plan (rest, moving, defending, tired); "May I?" / "You may… Ask anyway. I like being asked."; the knock described; **12 in 20**.
- Brom hates "going to a man's door to ask him for the worst day he ever had" (he did it at eleven for his father).
- **Ansel** at the cookshop: twenty-four then, a coast-house Bronze; two exchanges at the autumn invitational against a nineteen-year-old ("He doesn't build… You see it finished."). He was hit "before I'd finished deciding"; not kept that winter; "Survive first. Think second."; pays for his own stew.
- Log: survival is what buys the method its time. Lira: "That's you, said badly."

**47 — It Compresses Inward.**
- **The drop and roll**, Lira's answer to the right: drop into the left knee and roll the weight across, low and over, with no step. Four in sixty the first hour. **Lira's lamp goes dark for the fortnight** ("It's not a present").
- **Session two:** Brom's simulation, the protected line, a gathering felt by the knock as "inward"; **14 in 20**.
- **The read's limit:** "It tells me when… The direction doesn't exist yet when I knock."
- **The redirect:** Brom's boat-shed redirect sends back; Cael has no wall, so the taking face is passed, not gathered, ridden in. Three reps, cost all at once after (shoulder, ribs, behind the breastbone); a fourth would teach it wrong. In the Log in ink: *This is not an evasion. It is a purchase.* Brom: "You've three people's worth of worse days to hand out."
- Bruises as a record; the covered bowl and the sister's *EAT. L. SAYS.*; Vell notes the visitor keeps away ("Are you eating?").
- **Session three, 16 in 20 (rest). Session four, moving, 9 in 20.** Six of the eleven misses were knocks sent with the foot still coming down. Redirects four to six. *Plant, arrive, then ask.*

**48 — Declined to Be Interesting.**
- The betting man's line splits in two: practitioners against Cael, the row nearly even.
- **Session five, moving, 13 in 20.** He finds the gap between arriving and the next step.
- Dace brings the packet from up the river: a clerk's nine-page report written for a house five years ago, lent on condition it is returned ("That's what a trade costs").
- At the kitchen table: no visible wind-up ("gathered already, all the time"); a failed approach is never used twice; four rematches, each a different fighter; the conclusion that opponents run out of plans first. The one loss: an opponent who "declined to be interesting" for four exchanges, and in the fifth Reydan overcommitted; he won the rematch in two.
- **Lira's beat:** "That's Maud." Her loss becomes his lesson. Brom: you sat four months on Keth; faster isn't better against a man who answers people in a hurry. Grey book: *I don't wait any more. I used to. Find out whether I still can.*
- **Session six, moving, 16 in 20.** Redirects seven to nine; three kept for the eleventh day. Brom: "You've got older this week."
- **The sixth evening:** the hand on his shoulder once ("I'd rather you paid it here, to me, where I can see what you're paying"). Then the drop goes into his legs. "Now we do that four hundred more times."
- Walking home, Lira says that what went into his legs is what she is after in her hands ("It took me four thousand").
- Log: *Going right* is struck from *Shape* and written into *Habit*, with its cost.

**49 — Ansel's Round.**
- **Ansel comes on his own** after six sleepless nights. His terms: one round of three exchanges at his old speed, no kindness, anything Cael has, and on the open side floor before the benches, not in an alcove. Cael asks leave for "a thing I do close in" (weight and meaning only), and Ansel gives it: "He didn't ask."
- **The round, about a dozen watching:**
  - exchange one: Cael doesn't try to work him out; the drop takes the first hook; a hook to the ribs lands; a knock at a pace and closing answers, and he steps out and does not hit;
  - exchange two: Ansel reads him, the way he was read, and Cael **declines to be interesting**. For the longest he has ever stood on a floor without trying to win, his weight goes twice and is stopped, while the benches mutter. Ansel runs out of waiting and overcommits. A read burst, and a strike held a finger from his ribs;
  - exchange three: at full speed. A forearm and the redirect shoulder hit; smear means don't go; the last knock answers clean, and it ends with fist against shirt and hook against ear: "Even."
- **Ansel's beats:** "I beat me". He tells the newcomers his story and says Reydan's name plainly on the floor, buttons his coat, walks off at an even pace, and takes back his two coppers ("first time in three years he'd said that name on a floor and not wanted the floor to open under him").
- Lira: "You didn't go. That's the thing I couldn't do against her."
- Log: Ansel, with leave, six knocked, four answered, one smear, one late.
- **Session seven, defending, 10 in 20** (Brom's boat-shed first month).
- **Lira's leave**, for mornings, in the alcove, till the floor, each answer called aloud: 13 knocked, 11 answered ("like a bird going off a sill"). The drop and the knock won't share.
- **Session eight, 15 in 20.** "I'm frightened of everything. It's only that I've stopped minding."

**50 — Session Nine.**
- The mending stall's pennants. Dace sells stew room inside his doors to two eel-market men, his first broken rule in twenty years, kept "once". The morning's knock with Lira's leave: 13 knocked, 12 answered.
- **Session nine:**
  - Brom carries the breath to gather in a third of the time;
  - **ten of the first twelve** answer;
  - the **thirteenth simulated burst**: a gathering under a strike, no gap for a knock. Read and answer are one motion, meeting the gathering itself. Brom's push comes apart before it is sent, and his hands rest on Cael's shirt with nothing behind them;
  - **no cost found**, and that frightens him (Hesk at the bench, Cael aged nine: *anything that charges you nothing at the counter means to send the bill later*);
  - seven tries (closer, farther, no strike, eyes half shut, knock first): nothing, and a real redirect bill to show what a price feels like.
- **Brom's read:** the gap that everybody has was not there. Tide-adjacent, with every limit: he has never read a Tide; there is none on the hill; one man by a boat-shed fire; "I can't tell you what it was. I can only tell you how it felt from my side." It is not the third-exchange absence ("In the third I read nobody. Today I read somebody."). It stays in the alcove.
- Lira hears it the same day: "a cold feeling in my back".
- **Log, new back section *Anomalies*:**
  - the event, the cost (none found) and the seven failed tries;
  - Brom's basis, quoted;
  - three possibilities: something new arriving and showing itself before it is whole; something already in him doing a thing never found; *the architecture itself showing a glimpse of its own shape*;
  - *Can't choose. Not enough to choose with. Evidence insufficient. / Filed as anomaly. Monitor.*
- Night: he lets himself think, plainly, about **winning**.

**51 — The Night Before.**
- **Session ten, tired** (after an hour and a half of Lira), **12 in 20**. Redirects ten to twelve; the shoulder's done till Tuesday.
- Chapel day: Lira halves the morning and keeps the speed. **Session eleven, everything at once, 16 in 20.** The Log tallies the fortnight (rest 12/14/16; moving 9/13/16; defending 10/15 and ten of twelve; tired 12/16): *About one in five fails, at worst… It gives me when. It doesn't give me where.*
- The thirteenth day:
  - Dace's little ledger of promised places ("the street, which never asked anybody's leave in its life");
  - stew in the corner;
  - pennants over the side door;
  - the betting board in two columns that disagree ("That's not a line. That's an argument.");
  - Vell's two evenings down her chains: a new nail at the bottom of the ladder if it runs clean, and the floor itself on trial if it doesn't. "I'll be writing standing. Don't make me sit down."
- **The night:**
  - every lamp lit; the Monday card moved to the main floor;
  - Reydan alone on the west bench, watching the room;
  - **Cael's gaze finds no leak.** When the man's eyes pass the east post at exactly the pace they pass the stew pots, Cael concludes: *He's not leaking because he's decided not to*;
  - he does not reach with the read (what a crowd gives is one weight; going in is the man's to allow);
  - Lira: "like watching two cats on a wall… Calm as if it's all been settled somewhere else."
- **Reydan cutaway:** he finds the boy by the quality of his stillness and gives the good look nothing, an academy habit since fourteen. He reads the boy: hungry once, forearm bruises old and new, favouring the left shoulder, watching the room as Reydan does. He judges what he sees and does not guess what the boy is. He knows the scout's kind of book and does not make her his business.
- **The scout:** a credentialed academy pass, the first she has shown; Dace honoured it; "It doesn't say"; "She's been here before." She writes when Cael looks at her and gives back only the top of her head. She does not approach.
- **Brom at the arch:** "You can win this." / "I know." / "…I mean win." / "I know." The second one comes out different: the way out at the back of the plan has stopped being there. "I'll be at the north rope. She'll be at the south."
- **Alone in the alcove:**
  - the body and the resources (Wind, the feet, the knock, three redirects, the giving face, the gaze);
  - the count, *Three integrated fragments, one anomaly.*;
  - under the anomaly, *Note: Session nine. Could not reproduce. Still don't know what that was.*;
  - the plan page and the protected plan line;
  - fear let in "the way he would have let a dog lie down at his feet".
- The man up the hill who has never needed to hurry.
