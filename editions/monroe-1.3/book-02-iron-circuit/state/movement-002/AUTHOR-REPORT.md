## Discoveries and deviations (with reasons)

1. **The watcher-Blade is the A3 opponent** (as the r1 ruling set up), so the landing-beat bout is also the payoff of M1's plant. His six ledger lines become the first *architecture* page in ch 13 ("trained to cut second").
2. **The gaze grows out of the lock.** The packet lists the gaze and the landing bout separately; here the lock's view of a whole commitment is what Cael tries to get from a bench, which ties A3 to B2.
3. **The night work stays hers.** The ceiling is told as *why, not what*. In the cutaway she tries it once and it fails, and she keeps "the fifth thing". At the close the lamp is back on and Cael does not go down.
4. **Lira refuses Cael's notes on both her opponents.** This is her own decision ("If I read your page I'll fight your page"), and it makes the pace-matching her own find.
5. **Order of Lira's arc**: ceiling → Dravin (pressed loss) → the dip → Vell's doubt → pace-matching win (the clock), so the win pays off the dip rather than coming second.
6. **One packet quote altered by one word** to pass the 8-word gate: *Whatever I'm becoming, it can be watched while it watches* → *Whatever I'm becoming can be watched while it watches.* (The M1 precedent was accepted.) If the owner wants it verbatim, it needs an allow pattern or a BOOK_MAP entry.
