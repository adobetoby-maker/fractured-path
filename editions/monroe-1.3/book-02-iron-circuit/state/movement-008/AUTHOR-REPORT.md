## Chapters

**52 — Six Hundred.**
- Dace at the main door with his little book: 504 inside, the street "call it a hundred", about six hundred in all. The heat, the extra lamps, the stew pots, the pennants, the four girls and the dock partner. Vell standing. Brom at the north rope and Lira at the south, as promised in ch51. The woman in the grey coat at the back.
- Reydan steps over the rope and holds his coat out to nobody, and somebody in the front row takes it. He stands "ready for everything at once". Vell's call is the edition formula.
- **Exchange one:** five knocks, four answered, one smear on a whole-body feint; four bursts evaded (Wind left twice, the drop right once, one short ugly step right); two openings seen and declined; nothing thrown. Reydan watches him land each time.
- **Exchange two:** the same gathering, a new line, fired into the landing. The left forearm comes up in the lock; the impact puts him **on the stone**; he gets up.
- **Lira cutaway:** she has seen Reydan counting the landing ("Bede counted two") and cannot call out under the coaching rule. Pairs of bursts herd Cael to her rope. She wills the drop; he spends **redirect one** (left, crooked, the forearm already hit) to get off the line, ugly.

**53 — The Fourth Exchange.**
- Between the second and third exchanges: four knocked, three answered, one smeared; "The knock doesn't care where"; "A repeat is the most dangerous thing he can give me." Two reads spent (where I land; the rope), and the third is "a boy who stands off". The cost column is weighed: the giving face shown in public. "Spend it… Just not before it asks." Brom's chin.
- **Exchange three:** he goes first, on the Wind as an approach (three bursts: in, in, out); Reydan fires at the lock and finds it empty. Inside, the knock has "grain". He steps into the dead space behind the firing arm. The **giving face** lands three on the beat: right to the ribs, left hook to the front of the right shoulder, right into the thigh on a gathering, which breaks it. The hollow is full; both hands are paid.
- Between: "End of the exchange." The room, struck through as three lines. The three guesses (everything in one / hands / many). The body as a purse. Reydan brings his feet in and stops reading: "He's going to work."
- **Exchange four:** half-weight bursts on a count; the fan falls a hand short. Hesk's mill wheel; the count of *ands* shows every burst landing on the *three*. Two knocks confirm it, and the bursts all come at his right side. **Redirect two:** left, clean, the cleanest he has made; the left shoulder is done. **Redirect three:** the right shoulder, never drilled. It begins as the drill, then stops, gathers in the joint, turns, and faces home. Brom's "you've no wall" is recalled. It waits; he could let it spill. He drives forward and does not aim it. The burst goes home; the right arm goes dead; he **locks his knees on purpose**. Reydan goes down, tries in order, and lays his head on the stone. Silence; the stew pot ticks. Vell's line exact.
- Aftermath: the room comes apart (the ladle, the girls, the betting man wiping both columns, the river-academy man's open hands). The count, and the right shoulder "like a flue after a chimney fire". "Whatever that was, it isn't in the book."

**54 — On the Stone.**
- **Reydan cutaway:** he is a clerk before he is a fighter, and counts himself (right ribs, the front of the right shoulder, the thigh, and his own burst sent back to him). What he feels is interest. He gets up in order. Then come the protected exchange and the scout ("I know the kind of book"), and "Find me later…". He walks home by the river road. In the room above the saddler's he runs the bout back: three pictures built and spent; he went to the post; the boy counted along. He has no name for the last. He has seen grievance carried home (the boy the other house withdrew from the final) and **chooses to be more interested than hurt**: "Then I'll ask."
- Cael: Lira's hands on him and "You did it."; Brom's "What did it feel like?" and "Like I had enough." The floor clearing; Ansel's nod; the dock partner's hand; Brom's chin acknowledged.
- **Quenna's approach** (named, Greyvane, senior teaching-practitioner, Silver-tier, irregular Path development assessment):
  - six nights in all, two general and four on him, and the pass shown properly to Dace tonight; the drawer of eleven years' notes;
  - the demonstration-provision track and the provision's words; the file, and Greyvane's people "whose whole work is that sort of trouble";
  - monthly assessment by demonstration, "two of the assessment staff";
  - three candidates in eleven years: one went home to keep bees, two stayed, one teaches; "Two masters and an afternoon, every month";
  - Lira's question and the protected answer; the re-certification track ("It is not a kind road"); Brom looked at once and not addressed; three days, at the inn at the top of the market row.
- Brom: "She wasn't surprised by a single one of your questions."

**55 — The Accounting.**
- The walk home: a scarf sling; chestnuts; the street telling the bout in pieces. The heavyset man's "Win?" and the bowl on the stair; Lira holds the spoon.
- **The Log, bout night** (left-handed): knocks 14 knocked, 12 answered, 2 smeared; Wind 6, all left; giving face 3; redirects 3, the third "did not stay a redirect"; "The going in was mine. The rest wasn't." The plan held, the answer was not enough, and "Something else finished it."
- **Anomalies:** the half-second is opened as a second entry and laid against session nine point by point (gap / order / force sent / cost). "Filed separately. Monitor both." Session nine is untouched. A thing with a price is a thing he knows how to live beside.
- Day 1 dawn: Brom reads the right shoulder, with leave: "quiet… like a room after a lot of people have just gone out of it", and at the end he felt something "going back to him". The forearm is "Plum". Lira will light the lamp tonight; the woman's offer waits for the table.
- The district walk: what he knows of it (none of it written down). The mending-stall woman ("They'd better feed you"), the betting man ("Best night… worst night"), and the Stone woman from the tannery lanes: "You'll tell them where you learned it." The keepers' web; the district as a ladder people build themselves. He finds the decision already made.

**56 — I'm Going.**
- The lamp in the north window is relit; Cael listens from the alcove as "her feet remember where they lived". Brom brings a pot from the cookshop.
- Lira's landing table; the fourth bowl; Lira **"I'm going"**, said before he has got past the first piece. Her reason is not the ceiling but Maud: the hill "never once asked me the same question twice"; "I want a stranger with a slate making me earn it again, every month, from nothing."
- **Brom's questions**, one at a time, ending on the real one: "Does the form ask for your family?" He will not be measured again by Velmere's paper. "I want to go in on what my hands can do."
- Day 2, Quenna's inn: she knows his line. She withheld an offer from him on purpose ("I would have been carrying him through it"). Standard enrollment asks for the record attested and a showing at the gate, never the house; letters from Velmere "will go in a drawer".
- The alcove: "Then I'll walk in at their gate like anybody else… On my hands." Then the protected exchange, and his quieter reason: a home he picked, "I'll not let it go because it's moving three days east." Lira pulls his ear, and he goes red.

**57 — Find More of Them.**
- The Wednesday letter to Hesk, dictated to Lira: "I'll not put my name to anything till I've heard from you" (*L. wrote this… He's eating.*). Day 3 at the inn: Quenna waits for the grandfather.
- Dace's wall: he has known since Wednesday. The Ulric slot (asked for a month before Reydan) is offered for wiping and refused: "I know how this floor ends a bout without anybody hitting anybody." Dace draws the "watch this one" mark and rubs it out.
- Day 5: **Hesk's letter**, three sentences and *H.*, exact. Cael remembers the joint at ten and the seven pins: "Every pin you drive is a thing you're telling me you don't trust." Lira: "I'm one… Of the people." His reply: *I've two already… I'll look for more. I'll write from the gate.* Brom's nine-page father and "what he left out".
- **Acceptance** at the inn, signed left-handed on the demonstration-provision track: "the first time… that any paper has called you anything at all and you've agreed with it." Quenna: the Tuesday coach, the gate will have three names, come within the fortnight, before the first snow on the hill road.
- The **binder** from the paper stall (three iron rings; "Bring it back full"). Lira cuts the Log's stitches, and the pages go under the rings with room behind.

**58 — Wind Only.**
- The mending week. Brom reads the right shoulder each morning: "Still quiet… somebody's begun to sweep." It lifts to shoulder height.
- Day 6: he watches Ulric. The drop is smaller and later; Ulric keeps the middle and turns on the spot. "Good."
- **The final low-stakes bout** (Day 7, a Tuesday card, fourteen lamps, about three hundred people). Ulric promises not to go for the arms. **Wind and feet only**: no knock, no giving face, no redirect. The turn walks a hand's breadth each time. In the third exchange: the heavy cut, the hip, the lock, and Ulric steps back over the rope. Vell: "Cael. Win. Third exchange. Forfeit by the rope." Ulric: "That's what you watch." It was the least he ever spent on that floor, and he tells no one it is his last.
- That night, at the desk: **FRAGMENT ACQUIRED (Compression-adjacent)**, verbatim. It is known this time, "the face… now standing in the doorway with their hat off". It was not taken. The half-second entry is closed with the §1 phrase. Session nine is laid alongside and does not match; its page is left alone. A **four-head** entry is written, *Compression-adjacent. Reydan.* (Function / Benefit / Cost / Integration), with "Found in me afterward… Not taken. Not called." Count: **four confirmed, one anomaly**.
- Lira through the wall, the same night, with a candle ("It was in you all week… when I was holding the spoon"). Brom at first light outside the carters' inn: "It's mine… Only you did it soft." "On the road… I'll bring my hands."

**59 — The Leavings.**
- Day 8 morning: **Brom's rounds**. The cookshop slate; a copper each to the newcomers "for all the times I'm not going to"; the dock partner's mark and hand. "I don't go round collecting goodbyes from people who'll have forgotten my face by the spring… That one I'll count again." **Keth at the pump**: he would still rather buy the rest than fight it; "Come and find me, and I'll buy you a supper." Kept open.
- Evening: **Vell's back room**. The copied record: about thirty leaves in her hand, every line and every *atypical*, stamped with her keeper's seal. The rule is broken and explained: "The records know you existed here." Brom's stamped results extract is "the twelfth"; Cael's is "the first of the other kind". **The cupboard reading is offered again**: "Next time you're through… I'm not young, so don't be too long about it." It is not held.
- Night: **Ansel buys the stew** ("We're even"). Reydan came to the cooper's doorway and said he knew now; the name no longer sticks. Lira gives the four girls her wrist cloths. Maud at the ropewalk: "Write when the middle's gone."
- Day 9 dawn: the lodgers' book (*Gone east. Paid. Good lodgers.*); the heavyset man's "Win?"; the parcels; the sister's plate given back for the next ones. Packing. The night before, he moved the closed half-second leaf behind the Compression entry, leaving **session nine alone on its leaf**.

**60 — The Road East.**
- **Reydan cutaway (market morning):** a wool wagon to the first ferry. Dace returns the lamp-oil keeper's account for him to carry home, and Reydan has never read it. Dace: "He didn't decline anything… interesting on purpose." Reydan decides again not to ask early: he will ask when the boy has had time. **The nod.**
- Cael: the market's acknowledgments (the pie, the chestnut man's hand, the paid and wiped slate, "Feed him", the betting man's *C. — gone east. No price.*). **Dace's sealed note**, from a regular who "said that wasn't the kind of thing he did". "Go and be somewhere else first. Then come back and tell me about it." The nod from Cael's side. *Find me later…* goes into the ledger of things owed: Vell's cupboard, Keth's supper, Reydan's question.
- The east arch. He **looks back once** and calls the roll of the district. The note is opened on the first rise, exact; "He didn't know the man's name. He never would." Lira: "That's the whole district in one sentence." **Lira's second turn**: she no longer needs Fenmark's letter ("If it came, I'd read it… But I'd not need it"). Cael thinks of the lamp; "You still want to be the best Wind alive." "Oh, yes… I've only stopped needing anybody at Fenmark to stand and watch me do it."
- The first rest: "You're doing the thing." The **closing entry**: four confirmed, all found, none taken; session nine alone; a record that's his; the things owed; "Two people who know all of it. Neither one of them has turned back." Then the Book 1 line on the inside of the old cover: *Still true. More parts. Same one thing.*
- **The roadside tests** (Brom kneeling, a hardened palm against the braced left forearm). First, inert. Second, a piece caught with nowhere to go, sent **into his left knee**. Four more reached too early and got nothing. The **seventh**, waited for and the road chosen first: **a quarter of a slow push**, sent home, with a band across the right shoulder and breastbone, two breaths, felt in the teeth. Once more in two further tries. *Exactly like Wind at the start.*
- Walking three abreast; single file for carts. The protected four lines. The book ends on the road, east, "did not look back again".
