## Continuity, story, and canon

### Findings requiring repair

1. **Cael's consent rule is contradicted by the next stages of training.** At the end of Chapter 32 he writes, *“A thing I can do to people without their knowing is a thing I have to ask before I do.”* In Chapter 33 he deliberately attends to the old man, the girl, and the smith one at a time to determine whether they are kindled, without asking. In Chapter 34 he establishes a nightly room sweep and reads late fighters without permission. The manuscript may intend a boundary between passive presence-sense and probing an individual, but it never states that distinction; the existing sentence is absolute. Because the supper explicitly names consent as the moral lesson, the immediate breach weakens Cael's integrity and the Reader Standard's requirement that wrong conduct be recognized as wrong.

2. **Cael's response to Dace's warning protects only some of the people Dace has warned him about.** In Chapter 31 Dace explains that a dated book of names and habits can become a coercive list, including for fighters already known to the circuit. Cael then converts full names to private marks only where the fighter is *not* in Vell's ledger. That leaves ledgered fighters—the Shield and Keth among them—linked to detailed weaknesses in a portable private book. Either every fighter needs a private mark, or the narration needs to explain why ledger membership changes the threat model. As written, Cael's solution does not fully answer Dace's story, though the scene presents it as his considered correction.

3. **Vell's guild chronology contains a small arithmetic contradiction.** In Chapter 36 she says she was Iron at nineteen and Bronze at twenty-one, that younger practitioners were brought to watch her, and *“I had a few years of that.”* She then says the disqualifying bout occurred when she was twenty-two. The Bronze/watchers interval is about one year, not a few. This conflicts internally and with the book map's intended early-twenties break, but it can be fixed in one phrase without moving any event.

## Repair brief

1. **Clarify the consent boundary of Iron-adjacent sensing.**
   - **Location:** Ch. 32, final Log rule; Ch. 33, market tests on the old man, girl, and smith; Ch. 34, nightly sweeps.
   - **Observed issue:** Cael writes an absolute ask-first rule, then reads strangers without asking and without recognizing a breach.
   - **Effect on the reader:** It makes a newly earned moral insight look forgotten and weakens trust in Cael's honesty, especially under the thirteen-year-old Reader Standard.
   - **Proposed scope:** A localized same-author repair defining what requires consent—most plausibly, targeted reading beyond public presence—and revising the Log line and first public test so that every later sweep follows the stated rule. Do not remove the training sequence or watcher detection.
   - **Strength to preserve:** The supper's warmth, Brom's *“Being noticed”* admission, and Cael's willingness to name his own wrong.

2. **Make the notebook-security response answer Dace's actual warning.**
   - **Location:** Ch. 31, from Dace's *“Good thing we don't share”* conversation through Cael's evening recoding of names.
   - **Observed issue:** Cael anonymizes only fighters absent from Vell's ledger, leaving ledgered fighters attached to detailed exploitable weaknesses without explaining why that is safe.
   - **Effect on the reader:** The solution feels incomplete and undercuts both Dace's example and Cael's reputation for following evidence to its practical consequence.
   - **Proposed scope:** One small logic repair: anonymize all fighter identities in the private book, or explicitly establish a different protection for ledgered names that prevents the notebook becoming the list Dace fears.
   - **Strength to preserve:** Dace's scouted-fighter story, the principle that records belong partly to the people in them, and Cael's choice not to destroy factual observations.

3. **Correct Vell's one-year/few-years chronology.**
   - **Location:** Ch. 36, Vell's guild history: *“Bronze at twenty-one”*, *“I had a few years of that”*, and *“I was twenty-two.”*
   - **Observed issue:** The stated ages allow roughly one year of Bronze standing before the disqualifying bout, not a few years.
   - **Effect on the reader:** A simple arithmetic snag interrupts an otherwise persuasive disclosure and creates avoidable doubt about the mapped timeline.
   - **Proposed scope:** One phrase only—change the duration wording, without moving her ages, the bout, or her early-twenties break from the guild.
   - **Strength to preserve:** Vell's restrained delivery and the revelation that she keeps the archive because official recognition once erased something she truly did.
