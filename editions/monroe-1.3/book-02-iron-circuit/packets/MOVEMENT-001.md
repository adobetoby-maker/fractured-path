## Where we enter

A year and some weeks after Cael walked into Ardenmere with a bag and Hesk's notebook. Book 1 closed on the Darrow Innes win, a
district that has decided to notice him, a flag on his file that Coss could not read, and a stranger in the market who let himself be
seen. Since then: he and Lira moved out of Torvin's into two adjoining rooms at the back of a practitioners' boarding house two
streets from the Ironyard; he turned fifteen; he has beaten eleven Copper-tier formals this year; he is Assessed-Copper in Vell's ledger
with *atypical movement pattern* nearly a fixture. Two fragments, Wind-adjacent and Pressure-adjacent, both partial; Book 1 also logged
them firing together once, never on purpose since. Lira knows everything and has read the log. The Compact's sweeps through the
district have been heavier for six months. Open the book inside a bout, not after it.

## What this movement is for

Make the reader feel the circuit as a legitimate world — brutal, fair in its own way — and teach the system hard. This is the densest
progression-teaching stretch of the book (target ≈83 progression-vocabulary hits per 10k words): tiers and ranks as lived facts, the
circuit's ladder and its calibration chains, Arbiter and declaration language, Cael's fragments with every cost itemized. Let the
reader understand enough to anticipate.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **Ulric on the page** (~2,000): four watched bouts, three columns (confirmed / probable / discarded), the shoulder-drop, one Wind
  burst in the fifth exchange with its costs shown — hip-drop, held half-breath, the half-body-width displacement, the locked landing
  beat, the hip's line of strain. The hit he took in the second exchange from watching hands instead of weight. The purse arithmetic.
- **The circuit as an economy and a law:** Vell's archive tour and the ladder; calibration by chain; corrections announced to faces;
  the no-kill rule; Dace's board as a second ledger; Stedd's reliability currency; the first-glance tell (chalk board vs. Vell's table).
  Keep Vell's line verbatim (BOOK_MAP §8 item 18).
- **New episode — a compliance sweep crosses the district on the page.** Two officers, the fixed route, vendors to exact change, children
  a street ahead, the Ironyard's main board wiped and rebuilt from Dace's memory within the hour. Cael watches from the middle distance
  and adds to his officials page. No Compact contact with Cael. It sets up *controlled findability* for Movement 2.
- **The coaching-interference dispute** and Vell's four-minute procedure; "The keepers talk." Vell mentions the six months of sweeps.
  "The oldest ones use different words for things" — the reading deferred.
- **The ten landing-beat trials with Lira** (~2,500): a learning fight with a visible correction. Five trials prove the lock does not
  shorten; trial ten spends the lock as a perfect read of one committed strike. Rule from today: never spend the burst close to anyone
  whose one hit he can't price.
- **The Power Log** named and formatted (what it is / source / conditions / deployment range / costs / open questions). Write the
  Pressure-adjacent entry with both faces (receiving/dispersal and on-rhythm delivery amplification) and both cost lists, per BOOK_MAP
  §11 a — every later use must be predictable from this page.
- **The seam:** Lira steers him into his right-rear quarter; the burst only initiates off the left hip because hers does, because her first
  instructor's did. Angle-denial footwork closes it by position. "Everything I ever borrow will come with fingerprints."
- **FRAGMENT UPDATE** (verbatim, BOOK_MAP §8 item 12) arrives mid-session; Lira gives him a moment without asking. "The fragments are not
  static. They develop. Or I'm developing my understanding of them."

Physical challenge: Ulric bout; the landing-beat trials; the seam drill. Lira cutaway allowance ≈1,500 words (e.g., watching him write
after Ulric, or the moment she watches the update land) — she may know only what BOOK_MAP §5 allows.

Development: Cael ≈4–5 growth beats per 10k; supporting beats for Lira (several), Vell (two or three), Dace (one or two), Stedd (one).
Humor: Lira's "Don't thank me. Buy me lunch."; the fruit vendor's "A boy who never limps isn't learning anything."

## What must remain true / withheld

- Two fragments at entry and exit; the update is a revision, not an acquisition. Fragment notices keep the LOCKED irregular format and
  are anchored to Cael's perception within two sentences; speakable aloud.
- Fragments arrive unbidden from people he has been close to; he must not try to take an ability on purpose or state a rule (Book 3).
- Vell says only that the oldest records "use different words." No [UNBOUND] yet. No proper reading.
- Stone floor, low ceilings, fourteen practitioner lamps (BOOK_MAP §11 c). Vell speaks of "thirty years" at the ledger; no precise volume counts (§11 b).
- Brom is in the city off-page by the end of this movement at most; he is not met, named or seen yet.
- Reader Standard: no profanity or oaths; violence with cost and no gore.

## What the author owns

Scene selection and chapter boundaries; the order of the archive tour, the sweep and the dispute; tactics inside Ulric and the trials;
conversation, humor and local texture (market, boarding house, the east bench). Whether Hesk's first letter of the book appears here.
How much of the officials page and the purse arithmetic is dramatized versus felt. Fights may run long when the problem changes; let the
trials breathe.

## Where we leave pressure

The fragments revise themselves on their own schedule — the first sign that what Cael carries is moving whether or not he watches it. He
has a format honest enough to catch the next change, a lock repriced as a tool he has not yet used in a real bout, and a seam guarded
rather than sealed. Lira, meanwhile, has been training harder and longer than her rating explains.
