## Where we enter

The morning of the Brom bout. Two weeks of watching; a working manual on a Path nobody publishes; a two-line plan in the Power Log
(*Wind: read-fed bursts only. Never reactive.* / *Pressure: locked. Do not open it. Not once.*); a hypothesis that the transition between
Iron Skin's modes leaves a window in the third exchange. Lira has made him fight without either fragment for two weeks.

## What this movement is for

The book's first central contest, lost well — and the friendship it starts. Then effort turning into capability: a third fragment arrives
after three mornings of being hit for science, and the man it came from teaches Cael how to hold it.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **The Brom bout (~7,000 including the walk-in and the floor):** main floor, the largest crowd Cael has fought before; the old yard-owner
  at the back as a visitor (§11 n); Vell keeping the ledger herself. Exchange one: the hypothesis dies on contact — the "lag" was the
  training post's wood. Two forbidden openings declined at a price; one reactive burst off a fall. Exchange two: speed meets a read
  modified against fast approaches; the redirect down through both legs; the Wind burst now buys a third of its distance at full price —
  closed. Exchange three: nothing left, fighting small, and the half-second when Brom's read sweeps him and catalogues nothing — no draw,
  no cost, no handle. One plain strike lands. The exchange runs three times as long. Exchange four: Brom declines to investigate and
  finishes the bout on the legs. Track position and injury throughout; the opponent learns too.
- **The floor exchange verbatim** (BOOK_MAP §8 item 11). Brom sits beside him, not over him. Vell: *atypical movement, third exchange.*
  The log: "Every ability has two ledgers: what it costs to use, and what it costs to carry without using." "Also: made a friend today."
- **Brom cutaway (~2,800)** at the author's choice of placement — the bout's third exchange from his side (his read finding nothing it
  knows how to be hard against), or the third sparring morning watching Cael stop. He may record the absence; he may not explain it.
- **The market wall:** Brom's equivalent information (the dinners that went quiet; three weeks; the corrective instructor; leaving that
  night); "I don't think they were bad people." Cael says [SHATTERED] plainly; Brom already knew. "Build toward the name and operate
  without it." "Show me the log sometime." / "Maybe." Terms: full read every session; say what you're testing; stop if either breaks.
  Lira hears it all that evening.
- **Three sparring mornings.** Two more terms (tell me immediately; don't sell my method). Cael asks Brom to stop being careful with him
  (Book 3 recalls this). The quarter-intensity amplification test that sits him on the floor ("Prediction was a map. Knowledge was the
  ground."). The defensive/offensive timing felt through the knuckles. On the third morning, mid-reset: the quiet, the settling outward
  over his skin — **FRAGMENT ACQUIRED (Iron-adjacent)**, verbatim (§8 item 1). He writes before he speaks. "You absorbed part of my Path."
  Brom reads **the whole log** in the alcove. "You're going to be something no one has a word for." / "I know." / "Good. That means you
  get to choose the word." "Three fragments in a year is fast."
- **The teaching day:** "You're commanding it." Listening, not speaking; the month against the estate wall; forty small failures; the
  signature walks in on its own for four breaths; the headache band; it will not run beside the other fragments; stillness only. "It's for
  *knowing*." The day-one ledger entry ("I own something I cannot use… Begin.").

Physical challenges: the Brom bout; the three sparring mornings; the amplification test; the teaching day's failures.

## What must remain true / withheld

- Brom wins, four exchanges. Cael's hypothesis was wrong. The third-exchange vanish is *not* a fragment and is never explained; Book 2
  later files it beside session nine as unexplained.
- The Iron-adjacent fragment arrives on the third sparring morning, unbidden, after close sustained exposure. Cael does not reach for it
  and does not conclude he can take abilities on purpose; Brom does not say he can (Book 3).
- The Power Log is shown to Brom whole; Lira already knows. Nobody else.
- Iron-adjacent at the end of this movement is barely usable: one strong signature, three paces, stillness, a few breaths, a headache.
- Stone floor, lamps, low ceiling. Brom's read began at his Kindling (§11 g): "four years" becomes two of the read, more of the body.
- Reader Standard: the bout's injuries hurt and carry over; no gore.

## What the author owns

The bout's exact choreography inside the binding shape; chapter boundaries (the bout may take a whole chapter and spill into the next);
the market setting and the order of Brom's story; the sparring mornings' tactics; where the Brom cutaway sits; humor in the alcove
(Brom's open amusement, Cael's "Again. Unamplified this time.").

## Where we leave pressure

Three fragments, one of them a capability Cael cannot yet use in any room with more than one person in it. A friend who reads pressure for
a living and still can't categorize him. Lira has not yet decided what she thinks of the man who beat Cael. The main circuit season is
about to begin.
