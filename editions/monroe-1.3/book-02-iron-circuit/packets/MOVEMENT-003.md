## Where we enter

Three weeks after a large stranger told Cael he was writing the wrong things and left. The notebook now records architecture. Lira is in
the middle of her deliberate losing season and getting visibly better. Havel's compliant report has gone up the chain with one marker in
it that nobody placed.

## What this movement is for

Brom arrives as a person before he arrives as an opponent — and as the first problem Cael's method cannot read. The first third of the
book closes here: the system is fully taught by the end of this movement, and the reader should be able to keep a character sheet.

## Where we leave pressure

A scheduled bout against a friend-in-waiting whose Path is a machine for returning force, with a hypothesis (a transition window in the
third exchange) built from two weeks of honest watching — and a plan that locks one of Cael's two fragments away by rule. The main floor
is posted. The district is betting against him at unflattering numbers.
