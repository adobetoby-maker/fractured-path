## Where we enter

Autumn reaches the Unranked District late. The main season doubles the Ironyard's schedule and Vell's dispute load. Cael owns a third
fragment he can use only sitting still in a quiet room. Brom is a fixture of the alcove; Lira is still holding him at polite distance for
beating Cael. Lira is provisional Iron-equivalent pending two confirming bouts. Havel's quarterly review of the Ardenmere file is due.

## What this movement is for

Ardenmere becomes home with three people in it — and underneath the home, two impossibilities surface that nobody can yet read: a
designation no living person placed, and an old word in a margin.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **The season's economics** (purses, vendors a half-step behind the schedule, Dace's predictions beating the betting odds); the tally of
  dispute types.
- **A clean mid-afternoon win of Cael's on the page** (~1,500) — then, three days later, **the bettor's dispute**: the same guild-adjacent
  practitioner as Movement 1, now written down and threatening connections. Cael's dated notes (the shoulder-drop "two entries, dated")
  cross-referenced with Vell's ledger; the claim evaporates. Cael opens the Iron-adjacent sense in a room of thirty that morning: total
  flood ("The fragment cannot yet count past one.").
- **Dace at the archive shelf:** "Good thing we don't share." The scouted fighter who came back careful. "Remember whose hands they're for."
- **New — Vell corrects a chain on the page** (~2,000): a benchmark fighter proves overrated; she corrects every line beneath him and
  tells each affected fighter to their face. (Neither Cael's nor Lira's rating moves.) It shows the ledger's law the way the archive tour
  only described it.
- **The three-person dinner:** Lira's cooking and smoke; Brom's pickled jar ("a trade"); "You two have made an actual home out of this."
  Cael opens the read on Brom across the table — the banked fire that never goes out; Brom feels it ("Your range is terrible."); "Are you
  two doing something invisible at my dinner table?" Then Brom to Lira: "You should be competing at Iron-equivalent." / "I like him." /
  "I thought you might." **New (E11):** in the weeks after, Lira and Brom build their own friendship — she drills him in angle-denial
  footwork; he reads her signature honestly when she asks whether she is really past Copper.
- **Lira's first confirming bout** (~2,000), a win. Vell records one of two.
- **The Iron-adjacent program** (range bought with stillness; a walking channel asking only present-or-absent; practitioner vs. not,
  active vs. passive; the false positive — a dozing fighter idling his Path; "the read reports presence, not intent"). Late in the movement, with Brom, he
  finds the **pulse**: not a held channel but a single sharp interrogation at close range, which can survive movement for an instant —
  static more often than signal. That is the version he carries into the Keth bout.
- **The watcher:** three sightings in three weeks — the Ironyard's back wall, the market, the edge of the practitioners' quarter; reserve
  held deliberately at even pressure ("a fire banked by someone still watching the coals"); not Havel's texture. Lira walks closer in the
  market; Brom doesn't recognize the technique. **New (E5):** Cael and Brom try to read the watcher back at length; the watcher leaves at
  the edge of range. Nothing learned but patience.
- **Havel cutaway (~3,800):** the quarterly review; *Suppression-Advisory Watch, Priority Level 4*; the manual's Section 12 (reserved for
  Gold-tier practitioners under active security review); two drafts of a query to Coss; the same-afternoon reply verbatim; the question
  filed where the instruction can't reach.
- **Coss cutaway (~3,300):** what worries him is that the question was asked at all; he answers flat; files the upward supplementary note
  (recommend review for appropriate disclosure level); receives no response for a month; closes the loop by not asking again; begins
  looking at other files and finds nothing.
- **The record window** (≤800 words; BOOK_MAP §9 limits exactly): the designation, no sign-off, *systemic protocol, origin: registry
  sub-layer*, the timestamp older than the file. Nothing more.
- **Vell's archives** (~5,000): the handling rules (basin, lamp at arm's length, outer corner, one volume open); her own decades, her
  predecessor's rounder hand, the unnumberable oldest section; the old circuit's vocabulary (witness lines of six or seven names, no
  classification column, capability recorded as observed); **the marginal note verbatim** (§8 item 3); Cael copies the first sentence
  small; Vell's guild story (a few years of standing, not fifteen — BOOK_MAP §11 b; her predecessor's shelf "thirty-odd" volumes, no
  precise counts); "The Compact does falsify things." / "I know." Twenty years of suspicion, no evidence. Cael tells nobody about
  the note. This is a handling-tour; the full reading stays owed (§11 o).

Seed for Movement 6: Cael rereads his accumulated Keth entries one quiet evening and realizes how complete a picture three months of

  Cut the source's "infrastructure far older than the Compact" and "logic nobody alive had written." No will, no maker, no intent.

- [UNBOUND] is copied and filed; Cael may feel it pull at his own empty classification column, but he must not conclude it names him, must
  not reason about breakage vs. freedom, and tells no one (Book 3 Ch19 depends on it). Vell does not know what the word means.
- Drop the source's Ch16 log inference that "something is keeping a record of me" (§11 h); he may note that the notices are terse and
  fielded, like a keeper's format, and stop there.

## Where we leave pressure

A home that runs on choosing each other. A file that says something impossible and a Compact that has decided not to ask. A word in Cael's
smallest handwriting that he cannot yet afford to say aloud. And Keth — the circuit's Iron-equivalent standard, whom Cael has been watching
for months without quite admitting why.
