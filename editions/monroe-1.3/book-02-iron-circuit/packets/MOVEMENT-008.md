## Where we enter

The bout's evening. The Ironyard packed past its doors — Dace's count, street included, about six hundred. Extra lamps (not torches; stone
floor; low ceiling). Vell at the ledger table. Lira and Brom at the west rail. The well-dressed woman at the back with her small book open.
Reydan stripping off an outer layer and handing it to no one. Cael carries three integrated fragments, one anomaly, and a plan to break
three reads.

## What this movement is for

Every learned capability tested under changed conditions, and an answer arriving that Cael did not build. Then the offer that turns a win
into a door — and leaving a place on purpose, carrying its honest record.
