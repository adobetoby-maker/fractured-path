## Where we enter

Cael is Iron-equivalent and readable; he has started varying patterns and knows what it costs. Lira is provisional after a loss she chose
how to carry. The district's evening economy has learned his name. A story about an unclassified boy beating ranked opponents has been
travelling city to city and growing.

## What this movement is for

An opponent arrives who is everything the method cannot handle: formally trained for twelve years, no file to watch, and famous for never
fighting the same way twice. Preparation becomes a three-person discipline — and in the middle of it something happens that none of them
can explain.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **Reydan cutaway (~4,500), replacing "Cael pieced it together":** the standing-room fee; two ordinary bouts watched for footwork between
  exchanges, failure as informative as success; academy from eleven; invitationals restructured to keep him out of brackets; wasted trips
  to small-pond champions; the version of the story that reached him (a Bronze R1 beaten by a boy with no Path); "I want to fight the
  unranked one." / "He's Iron-equivalent now." / "I know. I want to fight him anyway." … "Tell him I'm not here to embarrass anyone… I'd

## What must remain true / withheld

  description, never a diagnosis. No hypothesis says "what all Paths were."
- The pre-bout count is three integrated fragments and one anomaly — Compression-adjacent does not exist yet.
- No one theorizes that Cael can take abilities by watching or contact (Book 3).
- Reydan is twenty-two, Iron-tier Rank 8, burst-compression Pressure Path.
- The scout does not approach before the bout. The watcher, if seen, stays unidentified.

## What the author owns

How the two weeks are paced and which days are dramatized; where the Reydan cutaway sits and whether a short second piece of it precedes
the bout; Ansel's round and his want; humor under exhaustion; chapter boundaries. The author may let the district's arithmetic of the fight
(odds, credit, the vendor's "feeling well these days") run as texture.

## Where we leave pressure

The night before a fight Cael believes he can actually win — not survive, win — with less of a file than he has ever carried into a bout,
a read that gives him timing but maybe not direction, three redirects' worth of shoulder, and one unrepeatable half-second he cannot
summon. Across the floor, a man who has never needed to hurry.
