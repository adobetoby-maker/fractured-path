## Where we enter

It is between the second and third meets. Halcenvane stands second on the circuit with a season of tidy ratings. The variance program has just been designed. Brom is unbeaten. Lira wonders, aloud, about the bill. The records-broker who probed Halcenvane's records office fourteen months ago is still an open line in the delegation's ledger.

## What this movement is for

The market reads Cael at his own speed, and the oldest open wound in the found family turns out to be small. This is the last third-of-the-book stretch for heavy teaching. The dossier, the band and the variance are progression vocabulary seen from outside. The book's two great opponents arrive on paper, Zerin and Marek, with numbers that should not be possible.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **The second market's three coats:** academy scouts (Auremont's two), handicappers, and the compilers who buy. Karis: "It's the clients who get to be nobody." The grey-wool woman, like a lighthouse, logs him logging her.
- **The board-man (Lira's errand):** no book on the demonstration bouts; the margin market closed inside a day; "He's the most classified fighter on this circuit." The table laughs, and Cael files it as cover.
- **Brom's dossier,** returned by the southern coach "like a dropped glove." Page three is the old seam, repaired fourteen months ago. The semifinal shows the funnel ("Brom is standing at the narrow end charging admission"). The final is fought honestly by a boy without a map: "You fought me. Not the paper."
- **Seln cutaway (~3,000):** the mill town's service side. The dossier is acquired off the page ("How the three evenings were spent, no record anywhere would ever hold" stays true). He reads it twice by one candle and finds the compiler's word *managed*. He matches the tasking fingerprint to the records-office broker, returns the copy through its removes, and closes the file in the only ledger he trusts.
- **He tells the table administratively** ("Most threats are commerce wearing a cloak.") and gives Karis the confirming absence. Karis: "Accepted." Cael: "Good." Seln looks at him one second too long. The log names the two professionals of the same strange trade.
- **Karis's information audit** for the whole company: nothing tactical within thirty feet of a rail, letters through Bracken's pouch, "Decent and instrumental at once." Ephram's rehearsed entry was sold within a day.
- **Auremont's junior scout charts his resets,** the first observer in three years to work a seam he has not surveyed. "Note the sensation. It's been a while."
- **The null report** (verbatim): "True. Complete in form. Empty."
- **The quarry town at the head of the lake,** ten days into the high country. Seln's sentence arrives on schedule (the halls are cold), and Rooke rewrites the warm-up doctrine. The company keeps a silence about Fenmark for five days.
- **The coat-rack:** "Huh. It's small." Lira vs Fenmark's second entry (~2,000): warmth as the first exchange's weapon; the doctrine's third-form gap taken from inside and beneath; the exit she gives him. The drill book carries her name.
- **Brom's funnel** collects twice ("Do your own next season"). **Karis's two interviews with the panel clerk** come back as eleven pages. **Ephram's semifinal loss (~1,300)** to the heat-shadow, itemized aloud like freight.
- **Cael vs the grappler foreman (~2,800):** the designed slow first exchange meets a real grappler. One hand gets on him. Compression stays banked at contact. The shoulder-seam is wrenched and he scrambles; then he wins the long way. "Paid full price, first installment." The shoulder takes four days.
- **Hesk's letter** ("the fault's in the workshop, never the instrument"; "Your grandmother proved a version of it once with a door"; "Mind the difference between the ones measuring you and the ones reading you"). Lira gets the middle paragraph.
- **The back steps:** "The moment was three years of work, and I already had it." The equipment mistress's nod: "Institution's bankrupt; the person gets paid." **Zerin's sheet,** read twice: "She's faster than me *right now*." Rooke: "It's a bracket of ceilings." **Marek's page:** "The dangerous direction."

Physical challenge: the grappler bout; Lira's Fenmark bout; Brom's funnel bouts. Cutaway: Seln ≈3,000. **The first third of the book closes at the end of this movement** (≈99,000 words). After this the teaching density steps down.
