## Where we enter

The wagons roll south two days to the wool town, five weeks after convocation. The card says *scheduled per demonstration-exhibition provisions*. Lira's first bracket bout as a formal Iron is the next morning's opening slot. Cael rides the tailboard out of a habit that the printed program has made obsolete.

## What this movement is for

The doctrine works, and works too well. Two meets show the found family winning in brackets with their names on them while Cael wins in "a category invented before the categories," with ratings he chooses. The movement ends on the first pole, written in confidence and wrong. Keep teaching density high: the reader should watch the instrument run (slates, struck extremes, eleven minutes from bout to board) and then read how it works.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **Recognitions at the wool town:** the clerk's niece's paid transcript; the validator's word "adjacent to it," given away free with her initials.
- **Lira's debut** against the Stone Path on the sheep-market slope: three exchanges. "Nobody gets to be surprised by me anymore. I have to actually be this good." Log the bill of four bursts, one over rate.
- **Brom on the page (~2,000):** the frightened fifteen-year-old won kindly in one exchange, then the buried fair-day Copper R4. This is the first public funnel and pricing; "Good bracket."
- **Karis's fieldwork notebook** ("Institutions are like fighters. The charter is the stance. The habits are the tells.") and Ephram watched for the work: "Pressure-testing."
- **Cael vs the quarryman (~2,800)** on the third floor against the old market wall: herding refused four times, the wall turned around by increments, one contact. "Story undersold you … I know managed effort when I'm the one being managed." Then **the Bronze (~1,200)**, solved more carefully than the man ever knows. Margin note: he wants an honest answer and calibrates what the instrument sees, and both entries are true.
- **Second of six.** Rooke: "you fought exactly as well as you intended to."
- **The protest ruling** (verbatim) at the waystation. Karis: "The category is going to hold you exactly as long as nobody makes you." Lira's two days of half work.
- **The mill town.** The examiner's manual studied across two evenings; Cael charts four bouts' slates first; **page thirty-one** ("You can lean it"); "one blind spot, and you live in it"; "Note the tense we both used."
- **Bracket days, on the page:** Lira vs the guild champion (the pillar corner banked two days; "Your floor has furniture"); **Karis vs Lira (~1,800)**, "a three-exchange family argument" in which Karis loses and collects data; **Ephram's first regional final (~1,600)**.
- **Cael vs the yard-master (~3,200):** a siege by paid-for inches, the floor down to two-thirds, the crane-beam's light as the asset he stopped seeing, and the fifth exchange bought deliberately as the doctrine's tax. "You fight like an auditor." The two academy entries; the *curiosity* Bronze answered completely and truthfully about the public suite: "the mask has stopped being a thing I hold up and started being a register I speak in."
- **Seln cutaway (~1,200):** he reads six ratings across two towns and four tiers, with a spread inside four points against the bracket fighters' thirty or forty. Then, at the document table: "Variance is cheap. Buy some." Rooke's metronome remark. The engineered-scatter program is designed, costed and offends him, and he inventories the flinch.
- **The stove night** ("Anyone else notice we're rich?" / "The difference between now and Fenmark is solvency.").
- **The assay evaluation,** the most boring ninety minutes of the meet: Gault's travel case and its handle. "Two instruments, one year, both managed." "Used-to is a rating. It just doesn't post."
- **The first pole,** at a coaching inn on the road home, verbatim (BOOK_MAP §10 item 21).

## What must remain true / withheld

- The public suite only, at documented rates. Compression is never shown. Wind runs to its documented working rate and no further in public.
- The ratings land in "a tidy band just above the middle of Iron."
- This mill-town session is the term's semester evaluation (BOOK_MAP §12 C8). It is routine and renewed.
- No meta references (C10). No "four years" (C3).
- Seln's cutaway infers *management*, never mechanism. No sentence gives his reason for helping.

## What the author owns

How much of each bout to stage and which to compress; the meet towns' texture (sheep-pen footings, the mill hall's racks and loading doors); the order of the mill-town days; conversation and humor at the inn tables; whether Rooke reviews on the steps or in the yard; the design details of the variance program.

## Where we leave pressure

Six bouts, six wins, and a band so tidy that a statistician and a coach both caught it. Cael has written "I can beat anyone at my tier … Not this season's problem," and the book knows he is wrong. The variance program begins next meet. Somewhere a market is already pricing him.
