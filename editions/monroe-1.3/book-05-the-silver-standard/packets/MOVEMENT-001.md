## Where we enter

It is the last night of a twelve-week summer recess at Halcenvane, on the bluff above Ostrand. Book 4 closed on a wall above the river: six things that aren't a Path, a stamp that says he proved it, and a teaching assistant sixty feet along the wall saying nothing, "which — from him — was agreement." Since then Cael has trained sixty-one sessions and the Wind ceiling held at six. Hesk has written nine letters. The watchers kept their rotation. Seln sat at a summer counter all recess. Karis spent ten weeks building the argument around a clause she banked with one word. Cael is sixteen and says so. Open in the log, at the window, then go to convocation.

## What this movement is for

Put Cael on a continental roster by the plain operation of other people's honest text, and teach the instrument the whole book is about. This is the book's densest teaching stretch, at a target of about 83 progression-vocabulary hits per 10k words. Teach tier brackets seeded by paper, the demonstration provision, the exhibition category, the registry's validation, and the Silver Standard's architecture: execution, control and effect; five judges with the high and low struck; scaled to a Silver Rank 5 par. Teach Cael's six fragments, each with its standing cost. The reader should finish able to guess what a rating will say before a panel posts it.
