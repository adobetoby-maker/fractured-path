## Where we enter

The delegation is in its first week on the seventeen-day road to Norhold, two wagons running downhill with the spring melt. Rooke, Gault, Seln and the reserves travel; Withrow and Bracken will catch up. Cael charts the road as an education. The draw has not posted. Nobody at Halcenvane has seen Daeva closer than a woodcut.

## What this movement is for

Make the reader arrive. First the road, then the city, the mark sealed in public, the brackets beginning, and the one empty chair. The tournament's scale should be felt as physics: eight thousand seats, fourteen delegations, the champion's gate. So should its fairness, which Umber verifies in front of everyone. Daeva is held at a hundred metres.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **The road's days:** the coastal column whose coach studies only Cael (third day), the silver-on-grey on a waystation flagstaff (ninth), and the road sorting itself (fourteenth: "The city starts here").
- **New learning fight on the road (~2,200):** at a waystation yard Lira asks for the corner-game against the one reader who can watch her build. She fails twice, then finds the version where the corner is priced before he can name it. Rooke watches and says one word. She carries the tax-early lesson into Zerin and still loses.
- **Withrow at the twelfth waystation,** enjoying herself: "The tournament will measure the five of you. The continent will measure all of us." Log: her thesis is that the paper is wrong about people.
- **Norhold arrives all at once** (the wagons stop by involuntary consent). Ephram: "That's not a building." Karis: "The prospectus was counting people." Lira on the liars being inaccurate.
- **The north gate; fourteen delegations; the banner over the champion's gate.** "This city is the system with the roof off." The watchers dissolve into general surveillance: being watched by everyone is the nearest thing to privacy.
- **Fame at continental scale:** the boy with the likeness; the six editions bought methodically; THE FIVE FROM THE BLUFF puts Seln inside the group; "Inaccurate. The office's ledger is larger." (the season's first confirmed joke). Ephram's protected "pre-sold" line.
- **Auremont's arrival,** forty riders arranged as information. Cael declines the read: "I decline to review the publishing."
- **The orientation and the ring's sockets;** "cheaper than deciding." **Umber cutaway (~2,000)** on the eve of the opening: the master tables in their case, the last cycle's seals, his clerks, and his faith in a working sentence. Plant the man, not the ruling.
- **Vastin cutaway (~1,000):** at his desk the observation files show the Concourse row's seating, and seat twelve is held unplated under his rank. He did not request it. He notes that, and does not permit the thought its weight. (This sets up Seln's later "Somebody announced it for him.")
- **The draw at dusk,** read by torchlight in company: Zerin on Lira's semifinal line; Marek first seed ("Somebody in the seeding office reads past the paper"); Ephram's "Correct tournament"; Karis's stillness at IVENNE ("She knew it while it was forming"); and the blank lines. Close the chapter on the protected line.
- **The opening procession:** eleventh of fourteen, last in the file, unbracketed. The crowd's specific sound. **Daeva at a hundred metres;** the first-read log verbatim. **Umber locks the Standard;** the attestation verbatim; "They cheer the banner. They *trust* the seal."
- **Opening rounds:** Lira vs the Ash screener (~1,800), whose screens tax the sense she leans on. Brom's round-two five-exchange bout with the hill-country mispricing (~1,800). Karis's three exchanges; the separate pools confirmed on the page. Ephram vs the coastal second entry (~1,200).
- **Two Silver filings** (*doctrinal interest*; *the crowd*); Karis on the inverted economics ("People fight *you* for meaning"); Rooke calls it "a *survey*"; Seln: "Scheduling is opinion."
- **Karis reads the trial rules:** *Squads shall be drawn from the academy's roster.* *Again.* "Twice is starting to look like a door somebody built on purpose. A note for winter."
- **The city's tournament** with Brom: "And when the theater and the mechanism are the same thing?"
- **The Compact row:** Ilsev, files squared; Havel, second seat; seat twelve unplated, unfilled and defended. **Havel cutaway (~500):** he reads the seating order and the unplated chair's held instruction, and does not write it down. "Blank pages are the ones that get written last."
