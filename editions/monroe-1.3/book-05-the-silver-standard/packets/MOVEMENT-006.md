## What this movement is for

**Each companion fights separately, before the companions fight together.** The bible's three arc tests run in their locked order, each a featured fight with full room. Cael watches from the rail, and his charting is the delegation's best scouting instrument. This is the book's middle-third heart. Give the companions their largest development beats, keep Cael's growth steady through what he learns watching them, and let the system surface only at turning points.
