## Where we enter

The seventieth morning, at the plank. The calf is a leg on the flat and says nine uphill. Brom's wrist is closed and must not pull. Lira's ankle is taped and stiff. Karis's palms are two pink lines each. Fifty-two in hand. Teague's crew is out on two hold slips.

## What this movement is for

Ground, not teeth. The first true freedom is a paid catalogue of a mountain; the mountain throws them anyway; and the sixth chair walks up a lip as a professional to a patient she cannot read. Turns and promises:

- **The Oxhollow slip** (seventy, on the map, paid at Oxhollow) sorts itself to the top, and he counts why: it pays; nobody's finger is on it; it is *ground*. Pike: take a cart ("You want to tell a ford it holds a cart, you'd better have had one on it."). Seln asks about shale-backs quietly at the plank's end, and Cael does not go to listen. The smith's cart is six against its return. The boxes go because there is nowhere to leave them.
- **The fan and the bench, given room (≈7,000):** a cart hauled on a rope; the shelf pitched like a roof; the gorge that gives every sound back late. Lira runs in hundreds; Karis's cord, width and pitch, stones dropped and counted; Brom says a cart on scree "floats" and walks against the strap; Seln is always already where they will sleep. Cael is paid by the line for the first time in his life. **Holding still applies:** he uses nothing on the mountain.
- **The herd and Karis's day** as a full learning encounter: spacing; the line moved without an animal seen moving; a rock passing between two of them (a step, not flight); the plate going and the herd stepping uphill *as one, no first animal*. Laid beside the ford page: same sense, opposite answer. "Derived. Not tested." "On a slope, flight is a landslide with legs." "Write it in yours, too. I'd like it in two hands."
- **The narrowings** and the eighth's clean break. Brom takes the cart across it, and the tail-board doesn't bang because nobody lets it. The ledge in sight. "I'll run it at first light."
- **The ledge and the stampede (≈4,500, terrain fight):** the ledge goes under Lira; her own Wind, full, crosses the gorge; thirty heads come up; the shelf cannot hold them at a run, and they come down the face in a front. "There. Thirty seconds. Lee side." "Cart's gone." **Karis's lattice line** across the face is a declaration the size of a road. The herd splits round the fin at eleven paces; she sits down at the eighth second, emptied. Brom goes back for two boxes (the wrist goes under the cloth). Cael goes after the third. Compression and Anchor *want*, and he prices both and holds, because the split stands on Karis's line and one more loud thing turns it. He takes the shoulder raw: three ribs, left side, low; the calf opened on the shale. The cart floats and goes in with its shafts up. Lira's leg broken under a plate, the boot gone. ## What must remain true / withheld

- **No fragment used** on the mountain. Compression and Anchor want, and he holds. Deployment since the Line stays **one**.
- Oryn knows nothing of fragments; no deep reading is run. She records nothing and remembers. Her Tide in full: contact only; about three serious mendings a day, fed and slept (two on a road); cannot heal herself; "cannot mend what I can't read."
- The herd's flight is **derived**, not tested. The herd's dead are "heard, not sworn."
- **The ankle is not mended.** The leg is.
- Nobody mentions quiet ground. Oryn's other five holds are not named, here or in her window.
- Teague learns nothing he couldn't read off a yard.
