## Where we enter

The switchback shelf above a valley with no name: the second day past the Line. Five people, a cart and a horse, three boxes and Seln's case under one canvas, two letters held for a courier who may not exist. Cael is eighteen, with nine held fragments, one anomaly, and a habit of counting watchers that has nothing left to count. "Nothing anywhere was deciding what he was, except him."

## What this movement is for

Deliver Arc 3's promise whole before anything complicates it: a country with roads and no paper, an institution (the board) that prices outcome and never tier, and a discipline (the hold) that turns four years of not-using into the only competence that pays. Teach the system hard. Turns and promises:

- **The skip goes on the page, not into a summary log.** The log-voice can open the movement, but scenes carry it. Day 4, the ford: the westbound courier on a mule takes the two letters and does not ask what they are, and he writes down that she did not. Day 6, behind three carts: the boy's hand knocked down ("Not on the road."); "You're paper, aren't you" … "Then you know how to keep quiet. Keep it." The etiquette is learned as observed, not explained: walk quiet, carry steel, no fire you don't need, don't declare. Claim, evidence, ruling: hold.
- **C1 (default unless the owner rules otherwise):** in Lowmarch's first week the horse and cart are sold. A horse is fodder and a sign that says *paper*; the scrub ruts past Lowmarch take mules. The sale and what is left of the Book 6 purse become a sealed reserve that Brom's rule keeps them from living on. The log's "in hand" counts only what the board pays. Give it one good scene and an argument with a joke in it, then let it go.
- **Lowmarch, day 9, and the board read like the Cinder House board.** Fourteen slips, not one pricing the crew. Pike: sixties, one arm, "Copper, Force, thirty years on the roads." "Walk, carry, climb, fight." / "In that order?" / "Depends on the day." A heading opened with five names and five columns: Job, Hirer, Witness, Outcome, Price. No tier column. He reads it upside down and looks for the missing column "the way you look for a stair in the dark, with a foot."
- **Three contracts as short episodes.** The ferry escort (day 14; six crossings; two marks; standing where they can be seen, a job he has done before "at a higher price and for worse people"). The fence-line at the steading (days 22–24; Brom cuts six hours; Karis is asked not to light, does not ask why, and writes down the not-asking; "Cheap"). The wash (day 33; rope; Lira on the far bank, Brom on the near; three wheels; "You're not paper" / "I am"). The ferry broker may carry the hook-and-bar mark, as a plant only. Nothing about him is implied.
- **The hold as a learning arc.** Lira trying runs that end with nothing (a burst is a shout); Brom carrying a load and not hardening; Karis keeping one point she will not light; Cael cataloguing the nine as things *held*, each with its rent: Shadow drifting at dusk (twice, on a road with nobody on it), Anchor wanting every doorway since the Ostrand road, Storm sitting heavy. **No fragment is used in this movement.** The log reads "Deployment since the Line: none" through day 49.
- **The Thornwater road (days 41–43), ≈4,000:** the carter at the fire ("Two carts went quiet on it last month"); the stretch at dusk after a wheel cost an hour; positions; the valley's noise; **Lira's hand up at a hole in the noise**; Seln without turning, "Don't declare anything." / "Walk. ## Where we leave pressure

Day 49: thirteenth of thirteen, with eleven lines between them and Teague. "I'm free. First time." Something out here hunts Paths, and nobody has given them the reason. Teague has read them and been read. The ford clearance, forty marks, sits on the plank with HOLD and his name beside it, and his crew is going out on the autumn road for a week.
