## Where we enter

The switchback shelf above a valley with no name: the second day past the Line, the second camp beyond it and the first fire. Five people, a cart and a brown gelding with one white foot, three numbered boxes and Seln's locked case under one canvas; Hesk's satchel holds the current log, full old volume, observation notebook, and two no-return-address letters held for a westbound courier. Cael is eighteen, with nine held fragments, one uncounted anomaly, no deployment since the Line, no open injury, and a fading left-forearm bruise one week from gone. “Nothing anywhere was deciding what he was, except him.” [B6-reconciled 2026-10-11: audit A1, D1–D4, N1–N4, N7; coordinator O1, O2]

## What this movement is for

Deliver Arc 3's promise whole before anything complicates it: a country with roads and no paper, an institution (the board) that prices outcome and never tier, and a discipline (the hold) that turns the discipline of not using into the only competence that pays. [B6-reconciled 2026-10-11: audit A3] Teach the system hard. Turns and promises:

- **The skip goes on the page, not into a summary log.** The log-voice can open the movement, but scenes carry it. Day 4, the ford: the westbound courier on a mule takes the two letters and does not ask what they are, and he writes down that she did not. Day 6, behind three carts: the boy's hand knocked down ("Not on the road."); "You're paper, aren't you" … "Then you know how to keep quiet. Keep it." The etiquette is learned as observed, not explained: walk quiet, carry steel, no fire you don't need, don't declare. Claim, evidence, ruling: hold.
