## Where we enter

Oxhollow's bank at dusk on the 84th: a hundred and sixteen in hand, and six owed. Ribs taped, the calf bound, Lira's leg new and her right boot gone. Oryn is walking north; Teague's crew has crossed south. Nine days to Thornwater.

## What this movement is for
