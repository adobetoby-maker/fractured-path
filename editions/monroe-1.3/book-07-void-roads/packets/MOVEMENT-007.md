## What must remain true / withheld

- **Shadow is used once in this movement**, unseen and never attributable. That is its second and last use in the book.
- **The rim saw a practitioner walk out declaring, and saw nothing work inside.** Teague saw the same and asks nothing.
- Faceless: no seals, no names, no office. The specialist is not the Ostrand-road woman: the same craft, a different weight in the laying.
- The case is untouched. The index and the stroke-and-bar are not decoded. Nobody theorizes.
- Oryn joins as a stop on her route, never as "sixth," never "join." The Stair slip's three hundred is taken and never paid.
- Teague's crew is unnamed, including in Teague's window. The healer of no Path does not appear until M8.
