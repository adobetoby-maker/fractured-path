### Findings

1. **Measurement continuity — minor but real.** Chapter 5 establishes the stillhound at forty-five yards and Cael counts **110 strides** from the white stone to the gate. Chapter 6 then calls the stone “a hundred and ten yards” away at line 9, preserves Karis’s explicit `110 of C.’s strides` at line 109, gives Seln **104 of his paces** at line 123, and has Seln’s internal report call the distance **110 yards** at line 137. The distinctions between stride, pace, and yard matter unusually much in a book built around exact measurement. The physical fact is stable; its unit is not.
2. **Audible referent — line defect.** Chapter 7 line 321 reads, “He had heard their four said, or heard of them.” On the page, the next sentences eventually disclose that “four” means the crew’s four ledger lines. In audio, “their four” initially sounds like four people and is not clean on one pass.

The “five roads” in Chapter 4 and “eight roads” in Chapter 6 can be read as five Lowmarch departures plus three Thornwater departures rather than eight unique physical roads. That is defensible, but if the distance passage is opened for repair, changing “roads” to “approaches” or otherwise making the counting basis explicit would prevent a second numerical hesitation.

### Method and uncertainty

`editions/monroe-1.3/tools/formula_metrics.py` strips Markdown H1s and scene markers, removes emphasis marks, and excludes fenced system notices from sentence/readability calculations while retaining their words in total count. A paragraph is one non-empty repository line. Sentences split at `.?!` followed by whitespace and an opening quote/capital or at paragraph end; common abbreviations and ellipses are protected. Syllables use a vowel-group heuristic with silent-*e* and *-le* adjustments. Proper names and system terms receive the same estimate and can bias Flesch results. Flesch uses the standard formulas recorded in the script docstring.

Written paragraph and marked-scene results are not equivalent to the source research’s pause-derived ASR segments; comparison is directional. The very high Reading Ease also understates conceptual difficulty because the movement expresses sophisticated institutional ideas in short, mostly monosyllabic words.

## Repair brief

1. **Location:** Chapter 5 gate count and Chapter 6 lines 9, 109, 123, and 137. **Observed issue:** the same gate-to-stone distance alternates among 110 Cael strides, 104 Seln paces, and 110 yards without a stated conversion. **Effect on the reader:** a measurement-centered mystery appears numerically inconsistent, and an audiobook listener must stop to decide which unit is canon. **Proposed scope:** reconcile only these distance references (and, while open, clarify whether “eight roads” means eight surveyed approaches); preserve all protected speech and the forty-five-yard following distance. **Strength to preserve:** the stillhound sequence’s exact, procedural suspense and Karis/Seln’s different measuring minds.

2. **Location:** Chapter 7 line 321, Teague’s cutaway (“He had heard their four said…”). **Observed issue:** “their four” has no immediate audible noun and initially suggests four people rather than four ledger lines. **Effect on the reader:** the mutual-reading climax briefly loses one-pass clarity. **Proposed scope:** replace or expand this single sentence so “four lines” is explicit; do not alter the order or content of Teague’s assessment. **Strength to preserve:** Teague’s terse counting voice and the careful reveal that he has heard enough to respect restraint.

3. **Location:** unprotected reflective/expository narration across Chapters 1–7, especially the road/board explanations and the two cutaways. **Observed issue:** sentence SD 11.62 versus ~26 and Flesch Reading Ease 92.9 versus 72.3 show narrower, easier surface prose than the selected formula, although every primary working range passes. **Effect on the reader:** the movement is exceptionally clear but has less contrast between compact beats and sustained thought than the formula requests. **Proposed scope:** a light same-author pass joining only closely related mid-length statements into clear compound/complex sentences and choosing slightly more exact vocabulary, followed by one metric recheck; leave dialogue, protected wording, scene breaks, and successful suspense untouched. Do not force the SD decimal if mean or ≥40-word share leaves its accepted range. **Strength to preserve:** the clean thirteen-year-old readability, short landing beats, and plainspoken road-country voice.
