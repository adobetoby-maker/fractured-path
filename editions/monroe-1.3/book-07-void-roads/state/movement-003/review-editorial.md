## Unscored reader response

Interest caught immediately on the seventy-mark paper and became personal when Cael sorted the job to the top because it was *ground*: work built from the very habits that institutions had treated as proof he could do nothing else. It rose again when the horn plate revealed the shale-backs, peaked through the ledge collapse and stampede, and changed kind rather than falling away when Oryn asked, “Who's worst?”

Interest slipped only a little during the repeated hundred-by-hundred and narrowing procedure in Chapters 16–18. The detail is tactile and ultimately load-bearing, but several iterations teach the same cart-and-shelf fact after the reader already understands it. The larger clarity slip comes late in Chapter 22, where Oryn's window reaches the ninety-second day and the next section returns without warning to Teague on the eighty-fourth.

Cael remains the person whose change matters most: he accepts observation as paid work, gives Brom authority over a load, trusts Seln's scratches over his own eyes, and repeatedly refuses useful fragments because an uncheckable answer would make the map dishonest or the stampede worse. Oryn is the movement's most compelling new person. Her worst-first ethic, route arithmetic, inability to heal herself, and appetite to understand without guessing make her feel necessary rather than convenient.

I expect the promised meeting at Thornwater in nine days and the deep reading when Cael is whole. I would continue.

## Continuity, canon, and story audit

### Calendar, money, objects, and physical state

**Pass.** The movement begins on day 70 and reaches Oxhollow on day 84. The intermediate anchors hold: the cart is paid for on day 71; mapping occupies days 73–77; the ledge falls on day 78; Oryn arrives on day 80; the lip is crossed on day 81; the road and descent fill the remaining days. Oryn's window then advances separately through day 92.

The board purse is correct: 52 in hand at entry, 6 paid for the smith's cart, 46 carried onto the mountain, and 70 received at Oxhollow, leaving 116 in hand with a further 6 owed because the cart did not return. Earned total 132 is also correct.

The three boxes survive; the reserve remains sealed in the third; Seln's locked case stays on his strap and is neither opened nor lost. The cart goes into the gorge. No new named object or person violates the naming boundary.

Injuries carry causally and without silent healing: Cael's calf begins at nine uphill strides, improves on level ground, then reopens on day 78; three left ribs break and are taped rather than healed. Lira's old ankle remains stiff and is never mended; her tibia is the injury Oryn sets and mends. Brom's forearm reopens when he pulls the boxes and is mended on day 80. Karis empties herself after eight seconds and recovers only enough to write briefly by Oxhollow. Seln remains unmarked.

### Power limits, knowledge, and reserved disclosures

**Pass.** Cael uses no fragment on the mountain. Wind, Iron, Compression, and Anchor offer help, and he refuses them. His deployment count therefore remains one, inherited from the ford. Shadow-adjacent remains sealed and unused. Native Path use by Lira, Karis, and Oryn is never miscounted as his deployment.

The shale-back conclusion stays correctly bounded: same or related discharge sense, opposite flight response, explicitly “Derived. Not tested.” The day-78 event uses that result without turning the day-75 note into knowledge it did not yet possess. The surviving count is “heard, not sworn” until Cael later counts twenty-five.

Oryn learns no integration mechanism and runs no deep reading. Her surface readings, contact limit, daily reserve, road reserve, inability to heal herself, and rule that she cannot mend what she cannot read are all taught before they become load-bearing. Teague learns only what the yard, keeper, bodies, and lost cart disclose.

No one says “quiet ground.” No forbidden theory names the anomaly, the Quieting's source, the Architect, primordial architecture, or `[UNBOUND]`. The movement does not disclose Karis's later hypothesis or make a premature connection between Cael and ancient sites.

### Cause, consequence, and relationship movement

**Pass.** The learning chain is complete: Pike's warning leads to controlled observations; the noon rock separates sound and impact from fright; the slipping shelf reveals simultaneous herd response; Karis records a derived rule; Lira's involuntary declaration validates it at catastrophic scale; Karis turns the response into a split around previously catalogued shelter. Nothing decisive arrives unexplained.

The movement also pays its relationship promises. Cael does not take Seln's report at the plank, later trusts his marked stones, gives Brom authority over the cart load, accepts Karis's second hand as a check rather than an endorsement, and does not soften Lira's responsibility into a comforting falsehood. Oryn's practice turns “worst first” into the same outcome-over-rank ethic the board has been teaching.

### Located issue: Chapter 22 temporal reset

At `chapter-22.md:137–233`, Oryn's cutaway moves from day 84 through the morning of day 92. At `chapter-22.md:237`, “Teague had counted his three before the healer's mule was out of hearing” silently returns to their day-84 meeting, and `chapter-22.md:299` returns to Cael later on that same day. The causal link is valid, but the chronology is not announced. A fluent adult can reconstruct it from Oryn and Teague meeting at `chapter-22.md:159–179`; an audio listener or thirteen-year-old can reasonably hear the Teague section as day 92.

The same passage contains a smaller ambiguity at `chapter-22.md:293`: “It would be three weeks, then, from whenever the mountain had done it.” The intended meaning is the total rib/tape interval, not that three weeks have already passed, but the sentence sits inside the unmarked rewind and can reinforce the wrong date.

## Reader Standard
