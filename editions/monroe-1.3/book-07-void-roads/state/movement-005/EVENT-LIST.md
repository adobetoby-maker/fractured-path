## Changes made while drafting

- **The crossbow is fetched, not carried in.** Chapter 30 (closed) leaves it lashed on the mule west of the stone, and counts "a hedging bill and two knives" at the den. So Oryn names it at the far lip, Seln goes for it (south about at forty yards, then outside the lane and up it) and is back at Cael's count of two hundred and seventy; Brom spans it by hand because the spanning hook is in a pannier. Flagged for a ruling in the author report.
- **Bolts, corrected:** six at noon. After the rim: one on the floor, one on the string, four in the sleeve. After the stone: one on the floor, one in the hound, three in the sleeve; a third goes on the string for the night watch and is never shot. Morning of the 97th: three + one off the string + one cut out = five. (The chapter 33 line above that says "four bolts in the sleeve and one on the string" is superseded.)
- **Cael's place in the lane:** four strides behind Brom's box and ten from Karis (not ten behind Brom), so that the Shadow use is ten strides. He passes on the **south** side, by Brom's left shoulder and Lira's left hand; Oryn is on Lira's right, a head lower, with Lira's weight on her shoulder.
- **The first two hounds at the lip** come at Brom where the rim is a kerb a hand high (the south-east), not under the overhang.
- **Ember's point** stays lit until Brom's string slaps at the stone; he shoots by it.
- **The state at the stone** is said by Cael in two parts: the count (hounds, bolts, sheets, who is hurt) and one sentence of his own to Oryn: what went up the hill with a light was himself, on purpose, and he has no more to say of it that night. She answers by asking whether his calf is worse than the arm.
- **Blade's bill is shown through Oryn:** Lira's knife will not cut a blanket ("Who's been cutting stone with this?"); she asks for no answer.
- **Karis binds Cael's calf** on the night of the 96th with the sling's leavings (Oryn calls it a good knot and the wrong cloth). Her written lines are mine: three lines of state and "I am asking nothing tonight. Entered."
- **Chapter 33 has five scenes; chapter 34 five** (the map and the contract run as one scene at the stake). **Seln's window** is the first two scenes of chapter 34.
- **The mendings of the 97th are done at noon**, by Oryn's choice, since it is the hour at which no hound has been seen abroad. The mule draws the dead hound off; Brom may not, with new fingers.
- **Lira's window is in two parts** (chapter 35, scenes 2 and 4): the night of the 97th, and the walk of the 98th. Oryn's eight crossings (Cael) stand between them, in their place in time. The order's test comes at the end of the first mile: Karis falls toward her left, and she takes her on the wrong shoulder.
- **Added, chapter 35:** a flint fire at the spring on the night of the 98th (Seln makes Brom's bread to dictation and it does not burn; Karis enters a finding; her first attempt at a word); the mile to the fork on the 99th, where Cael lets himself look at what the morning will be; Oryn walks Thornwater's bench with two fingers and puts five people off until after noon; Lira climbs the loft ladder with one arm.
- **The loft:** Seln strikes Cael's second reason himself ("I shall not be a reason"). Karis says one word aloud, "Wrong.", and enters the rest against herself. Cael names it wrong and says that he will do it; no hands are called for. The leaf is his third under the Book 6 rule (written before, at full weight); the first rule, that he is not to make one come, is said and is kept (she proposed the reading at Oxhollow).
- **The reading** is counted in breaths, twelve to the minute: sixty at five minutes, two hundred and forty at twenty. Her report falls at two hundred and sixteen to two hundred and twenty-eight; "Eleven years" after it; the reach and the notice inside the twentieth minute.
- **Not done:** no scene at the stake on the night of the 96th; no reading of anybody by the tenth; nothing of the seven after the wall.
