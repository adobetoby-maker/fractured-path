## Unscored reader response

Interest catches immediately when Karis's straightedge turns the earlier sites into a direction, then rises sharply at the Drowned Hall reveal. The strongest sustained sequence is the Hall: the floor's rule is taught before danger, Brom's instinct creates the trap, Oryn's rescue changes the target, and Cael's Compression/Anchor answer follows from evidence rather than convenience. The line from those events to “Four make an argument,” the fourth-site cordon, and Pike's empty column makes the whole movement feel like culmination rather than an isolated encounter.

Interest slips slightly during the consecutive method-heavy passages: Cael's river-and-stone self-read in Chapter 55, Karis's copying and alignment proof in Chapter 57, and the retrospective Seln/Oryn windows in Chapters 59–60. Each passage earns a later emotional or plot payoff, so the issue is density, not dispensability. The reverse chronology is consistently dated, but a thirteen-year-old may need an extra beat to reorient when a chapter steps back several days.

Oryn matters most here. “So are you,” her turn north, her refusal to take back the bow, and her four-line letter turn a temporary “stop” into durable belonging without pretending that she has forgiven Cael. Seln giving Karis the field book and Cael refusing to read Teague are equally strong, quiet character payments.

I expect the next book to bring Oryn and Teague back after the thaw, follow the bearing past the fourth site, and test what the recovered kit and alignment mean without prematurely solving the still place. I would continue. The two visible placeholders must be filled first because they break the fictional surface and the audiobook.

This is a verified physical-state contradiction, not merely plan variance.

## Repair brief

1. **Location:** Chapter 54:323 and Chapter 58:31. **Observed issue:** two literal `[[TIER-B…]]` coordinator markers remain where protected dialogue must appear. **Effect on the reader:** the fiction and audiobook break outright at the Hall's cost/payoff and at Oryn's departure; the protected-wording gate cannot pass. **Proposed scope:** coordinator inserts the exact approved Tier-B units only, then checks the immediate lead-in/out for grammar and reads both passages aloud; do not paraphrase or invent the missing wording. **Strength to preserve:** the restrained Hall aftermath and Oryn's unsentimental route arithmetic.

2. **Location:** Chapter 54:253–261, checked against Chapter 58:19 and Chapter 60:145. **Observed issue:** Lira's slung left arm “came up… to steady” the bow despite the binding no-hold order and later claims that it held nothing. **Effect on the reader:** it weakens injury continuity and makes the later medical accounting factually false. **Proposed scope:** restage only the brace so the coping, right shoulder, right knee, and/or wall stabilize the stock while the left arm stays inert inside the sling; retain both shots, Seln's spanning, and the action's present length. **Strength to preserve:** the earned payoff of Lira's one-handed shooting lessons and the fight's clear shared labor.
