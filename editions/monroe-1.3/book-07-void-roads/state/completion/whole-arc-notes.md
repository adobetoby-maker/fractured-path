Reader: Codex, independent fresh-context whole-arc seat. Date: 2026-10-11.

Scope: Book 7 manuscript chapters 1–60 in order. These are working notes for the completion report, not manuscript revisions.

## Binding pre-read notes

- Reader Standard: fluent thirteen-year-old; clean language, moral clarity, consequential non-lurid violence, one-pass comprehension, audio-first referents.
- Book 7 is season-blind and uses feet/yards/paces/cords, not metric units. Generic `season` and `month` are allowed.
