So the cord's length and Karis's stride are never stated; only the six-and-a-half-foot offset is, which agrees with a cord of about sixty-five feet. If the owner wants the cord stated, it is one sentence in chapter 27.
4. **"Three blank circles" against chapter 12.** Chapter 12 says that only at the bend does a road walk round a blank. I kept that true and added two blanks that no road goes round (one in hatching, one in water), found on a second reading and marked *guess*. They sit, loosely, where the Stair and the Hall will be; the hide is said not to take a straightedge, so nothing about an alignment is spent. If the plan wants the other two circles drawn later, cut one speech of Karis's (ch26:139–143).
5. **The trials' order** is Karis, Brom, Seln, Lira, Oryn ("smallest first", since a burst is the loud thing), not the packet's listing order.
6. **Additions to the trials:** Brom lifts the box inside ("Same."); Lira takes off the boot and asks along the line, northward; Seln's count of three. Lira's "I feel thirteen" (the year before her Kindling) is in chapter 30.
7. **When the private tests fall:** after Oryn's crossings, in a quarter of an hour when the five are each occupied, a cord north of the stone at Brom's first turf. He prices the risk first (the hour; the quietest thing he owns; the ford's burst drew three). The Iron read is reopened once on the floor, "the same thing… done now for work, and he did not count it a third"; he writes only *No seam.*
8. **Why he does not tell them** is mine: Lira's face after the step; and Oryn, who knows nothing of what he is, standing there ("that was not his alone to tell. It was a real reason. He distrusted it for coming so readily to hand."). He also takes it that the others suppose what he supposed until the stride: that a thing taken out of a Path stops where Paths stop. No one says so aloud.
9. **The day-96 log has no deployment line**, for the first time since the shelf: "It would not have been true, and he did not yet know what would be." The logs of the 89th, 90th and 95th end on "Deployment since the Line: one."
10. **The Ring's sheets are made on the 96th** (ten and a half), because nobody is inside the Ring again and Movement 5's packet does not stage them. Karis does not rub the band on the lip.
11. **Cael overruns his own limit** by a quarter of an hour for the last two stones, and says so. It is the reason that six people are at the den at sunset.
12. **Seln at the den:** "eight, or nine", all out to the east that morning, none back. Movement 5 has nine.
13. **Karis's window** shows five rows and "a space at the foot of the leaf"; it says that she is frightened "by how well it agrees" and that she lifted her pencil from a sentence with *as though* in it. It gives no likeness, no who, no why.
14. **Teague's window** has him read "HOLD" over five names without grudging it, and decide that "a feeling is not a figure". He is a silent witness on the 91st and does not nod.
15. **The two HOLD slips are unresolved** at the movement's end. The ledger's board track gives the five no line for either, so the next packet should say that the fortnight ran out and Teague had them, or leave them be.
16. **Seln and the stake's number:** he will not guess what thirty-one counts. Cael sets the stake beside the query in Ephram's letter and the posting as a third point: "He could rule the line now… What he could not do was say which way along it he was looking." Nobody says "Compact".
17. **Vell's "session":** Cael says only "I know what she's speaking of. I don't know what she means by saying it now… It's hers… I only keep it."
18. **Oryn "has been round it thirty times"** makes the Fallow road part of her ordinary round east of Thornwater. Her window in chapter 22 has the round take her "out by the far road"; I read that as the east road.
19. **Money:** 116 → 114 (the boot) → 108 (the smith). Nothing else is spent; Thornwater lends six blankets, to come back; food is not itemised.
20. **Length:** 37,119 against about 38,000; the three markers will add a little.
21. **Secondary share** is 6.8% (plan 7%).
22. **"Fallow"** is explained only as the holds' word for ground that is resting. Nothing is said of its ever having been worked.

## State at movement end (for the ledger)

**Calendar.** Sunset on the **ninety-sixth day past the Line**, at the eastern lip of the Fallow Ring's floor. No season, month or English weekday name is on the page ("the cold term"; "frost"; "before the snow"; "fortnight"). Days on the page: 84 (dusk), 85, 86, 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 92, 93, 94, 95, 96; and, in Teague's window, the evening of the 89th. Weather: frost every morning; dry roads; a north wind on the Fallow. The next movement opens where this one stops.

**Where everyone is.** Six people at the den under the eastern lip, ten cords and half a floor inside the line: Cael, Lira, Brom (with the middle box on his back), Karis, Seln (on one knee) and Oryn. The lane trodden by the cord runs from the floor's western lip two hundred yards and more down to the grey stone. Oryn's mule is hobbled on the western side of the stone with her panniers, six of Thornwater's blankets, the carter's left boot and the long shape in its oiled cover still lashed on the pack. No hound has been seen. The sun is half under the hills.

**Cael.** Eighteen. **Nine confirmed fragments and the uncounted anomaly.** Logged deployment since the Line: one (the ford); the day-96 entry carries no deployment line. **On the 96th, unwitnessed:** the Iron read opened a pace short of the line and carried across it; a foot's worth of Wind evasion twenty strides inside; the Iron read once more on the floor. Shadow is sealed and untouched; it lifted at dusk on the 95th at the turn of the ruts and on the 96th inside the line, and was refused both times. Anchor asked at the stone and at the stake. Ribs: taped, eighteen days from the break, three to go. Calf: bound in Oryn's linen, closing by itself. He has told nobody that anything answered him inside, and has given Karis "It was dark before… It's dark now." for her sixth row. Hesk's satchel holds the log, the old volume, the notebook, Hesk's letter, Pike's lines on bark, and now Ephram's letter and Vell's. He has not answered any of the three.

**Lira.** Wind; Iron, Rank One. No burst since the 78th; on the 96th she asked for a whole one inside the line and took one step of a yard and a half. Right tibia mended on the 80th: walking; not to run until about the 110th. **Right ankle not mended**, taped by herself; the carter's right boot over a torn shirt. Long knife drawn at the last line.

**Brom.** Iron Skin; Copper. Called the Skin aloud inside the line; none. Left wrist sound; the cuff came off at the middle of the 87th and is inside his shirt with Pike's dead slip. He has carried the middle box from Thornwater. He has the hedging bill.

**Karis.** Ember; Iron, Rank Three. Refilled: one point on a wick on the 93rd, "three parts full"; nothing since (roads). Inside she can see every place and set none. Holds: the perimeter table (sixty-two stations, one offset); the six-row table of the trials, with the sixth ruled on his statement; ten rubbings in the box and an eleventh, half done, in her coat; the carter's map with three pencilled rings, two marked *guess*; the registry's sheet; the long cord and the short one; the brass; forty-odd chips; the leaf with her nine crossings. She has said "A circle is a made shape." once, and nobody took it up.

**Seln.** Shadow; Bronze. Nothing to lift inside. Unmarked. He has read the posting as an office's paper, written by somebody who learned to write inside the Line; the stake as five years in the weather and driven at the far end of a measure; the eastern runs; the den. **The case is on its strap, on him, shut.**

**Oryn.** Tide; Iron, Rank Four. At Thornwater on the 93rd; one mending there (the carter's hand) and two more that evening. She gave the five her days until the 99th of her own choice, for two reasons. She has taken a surface reading of Brom's wrist on the 94th and at the stone on the 96th; **inside the line she reads nothing and has read nobody.** She has crossed with Brom (twice together, once alone with his arm held across) and with the six. She knows nothing of fragments and has no theory. She cannot mend until she is west of the stone.

**Teague.** At Lowmarch on the 91st with his three. He read the Fallow posting twice on the 89th and put it back; he has no reason and has given none. He watched the five take it. The two holds' slips are his if the five are not down inside the fortnight.

**Objects and money.** The middle box: chips, lamp, paper, ten rubbings, the two maps, the brass, the short cord. Two boxes in Thornwater's loft, the reserve sealed in the third, unopened. The Oxhollow cart has gone home with grain. Board purse: **a hundred and thirty-two earned; a hundred and eight in hand.** Owed: one rope, sixty yards, four-strand, to the ferryman, with no price. A hundred marks lie in Pike's box against the broker's mark on the Fallow paper. Cael's coat has no sleeves.

**Pike's book and the wall.** **Seven lines**; the seventh is the protected traverse line with *less six, the cart.* under it. **Tenth; Teague nine up.** The Fallow posting carries five strokes and is in Hesk's book. Two slips with HOLD and the five names are on the oak.

**Who knows what.** The mechanism: the five, and nobody else. All six know what quiet ground does to a live Arbiter, to the stride, and that a healer's hands stop there. The party knows that the registry's iron stands on the line, numbered thirty-one, some five years old; that no instruments are at the site; that hounds den under the floor. **Nobody but Cael knows that anything answers inside.** Pike, Teague, the clerk and the holds know only that five people took a paper.

**Open threads.** Where eight or nine hounds are, at sunset. A lane two hundred yards long and one way out that anybody has walked. A healer who cannot mend east of a stone. A captain's quarter of an hour. What he has not told them, and why. A posting written inside the Line for a thing that is not there. Thirty-one. Two blanks on a calfskin marked *guess*. "A circle is a made shape." A rope, two holds, three unanswered letters, and no roof.
