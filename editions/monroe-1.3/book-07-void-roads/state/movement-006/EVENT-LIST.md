## Fixed things I set before drafting

- **The telling** is a retelling of what the five settled in the edition's Book 6: his own Arbiter woke at his Kindling; the station's plate lit eleven seconds late; the word that came is what a form writes when it cannot be filled in, set where an older name used to be written (the older name is NOT said); the light went out and has been dark since. At the line nothing in him changed. Why the fragments answer inside: nobody knows, and he says so. Sources by Path: three she has had her hands in (Lira's Wind, Brom's Iron, Karis's Ember), and six by Path only (Pressure, Compression, Storm, Anchor, Blade, Shadow). Of the Shadow he says its Path, that it is kept shut for its source's sake, that he used it once on the hill and counted her eyes first. She asks for no names.
- **Her cost** (what he cannot pay beforehand): she refuses the price he wrote for himself (to be sent away); she keeps the answer she would have given at the door, and he will never have it; the size of the rest is hers to set when she knows what it took. He is not forgiven; he does not argue; he says once that it was wrong, and she tells him to say it again when it has cost him something.
- **Whether it touched her:** she felt nothing go; near the end something kept pace beside her, and then it was in him. She cannot read herself. She will know by her hands at the bench. In her window they are whole: two mendings as priced. What was taken is the asking.
- **Her conditions, all hers:** on himself first, before anyone; she is the one he tells (the table, the 100th). Ask, every time (the yard, the 101st). The debt's terms (the well, the 110th): three readings on three different days, then all of it at once; nobody read on the road to find out whether he can.
- **The listening** (sensation only): his own current at the edge of every thought, with his own uneven gait; the nine come up out of it singly and are told apart by sound. Wind, dry grass leaning; Pressure, a bellows filling; the read, a millstone through a floor; Compression, silent, a rope under load; Ember, a wick ticking; Shadow, a place where the others drop out; Storm, the lowest, a sea turning over; Anchor, one held note; Blade, a wet finger on a glass. The tenth is the hearing. Nights: the 100th, no sleep; the 101st, four hours; the 102nd, idle.
- **Brom's forge story:** his father kept a forge in a shed past the gardens; Brom slept there as a boy when the house was loud; the first nights he heard every tick of it and the third night he slept: one does not stop hearing a forge, one stops listening, and the noise that is new will have one on one's feet.
- **The Wind economy** (learning encounter): Storm resettles at the well; the ground comes half a beat late under a counted foot. Rooke's four-count walked between well and trough, a foot's lean on the fourth. The listening shows the waste: Wind has always begun on the third count. Lira, from the coping: "You've always been early." Correction: ask when the ground arrives, on four-and. Eleven leans: one stagger, four wrong (two late, two early), six right. A lean at half the spend.
- **The first self-reading:** on his back in the loft, two fingers on his own wrist and a palm on the breastbone. Written above everything: ten flows and no banks, a limit of the reading. Then a place near the middle that none of the ten runs through: it is not a current, not the tenth, not one of the nine; everything is nearer there; the ten go round it. Three things he can count: where; that nothing in it moves; that all ten go round it and none through. **It is not named.** He tells nobody what he found; he tells Lira and the six only that there is a thing and that it is the healer's to hear first.
- **The yard bow** (the hold's): an old stirrup crossbow kept under the gate-walk in a goatskin; a belt hook; eight blunt bolts fletched with goose feather; it throws left. The mark: a straw sack on a stake outside the west gate, shot from the gate-walk's rail at noon; forty paces, then sixty.
- **The four wheres** (the circle of six): the Fallow Ring, walked; the round blank a day south of Lowmarch, marked *guess*; the round blank far to the south-east among the water-strokes, marked *guess*; and Seln's second round on a sheet he cannot place.
- **Word to Pike:** the Thornwater carter went down light at first light on the 100th with four lines of Karis's and the hold's mark; he is back at dusk on the 102nd with Pike's bark. The broker's clerk had his copy for that day's boat.
- **Karis's rank:** the packet lists the five as "Iron R1, Copper, no ranking, Bronze, Iron R4". The edition's Karis is Iron, Rank Three, retained. I write her as Iron, third rank, and flag it.
