Interest slipped somewhat during the consecutive method explanations from the “one basket” discussion through the naming, looms, strata and stopped hypothesis (chapters 41–43). I expect the next movement to turn the four “wheres” into a dangerous second-site expedition, force Cael's new rule to survive pressure, and bring Oryn back into contact with the group on her own terms. I would continue. The movement pays off the tenth fragment, Cael's nineteenth birthday and the naming of the Quieting while opening a larger geographical and institutional problem.

### Reader Standard

The movement passes the thirteen-year-old Reader Standard in content and moral framing. There is no profanity, obscenity, sexual content or innuendo. Violence is recalled through injury and consequence rather than gore. Most importantly, Cael's act against Oryn's consent is named as wrong by Oryn, Cael and the companions; confession does not purchase forgiveness, and Oryn controls the conditions and timing of any future reading. Courage, loyalty, restraint, honest uncertainty and care for injured people all carry real costs.

The conceptual material is denser than the readability score suggests, but it is usually grounded in an apple, a loom, a stone, a hand or a bow. A fluent thirteen-year-old should be able to follow it. The chief comprehension risks are the missing lines and the repeated fine distinctions among observation, finding, hypothesis and fear.
