The following narration repeats that the panel will receive growth “in five things that were already on paper.” This conflicts with supplied project evidence:

- `STATE_LEDGER.md`, entry state, “Public record of capability”: the hearing transcript carries Wind and the Iron read, plus Ember in one thin exhibit; “Nothing else.”
- `STATE_LEDGER.md`, after Movement 2: “Pressure withheld at the plate. Compression untested on a floor that answers (ringed). Ember off the floor by agreement with Karis.”

- Chapter 12 itself establishes the governing doctrine exactly: the public record holds Wind and Iron; the transcript says nothing of Pressure or Compression; Ember is too thin to show without becoming news; “Two capabilities are on the public record.”

This is a canon contradiction, not merely plan drift. It also creates a forward structural problem because the Book Map currently expects the Movement 8 evaluation to test five capabilities plus a flat Pressure trial. A local synonym change cannot repair it. The coordinator must choose whether the earlier baseline/record is revised or the later evaluation plan is rebuilt around what the record lawfully contains. Until then, Chapter 43's emotional conclusion—that hiding and honesty finally align—rests on a false premise.
