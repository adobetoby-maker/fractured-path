The final Seln cutaway then changes the meaning of the whole movement. I expect the next movement to become a contest between Cael's habit of noticing people and a watcher trained to occupy the missing place in attention. I would continue.

## Continuity, canon, and story findings

### Findings requiring repair

1. **Chapter 8's internal calendar cannot contain the narrated training span.** The chapter opens on the twenty-second morning (line 3). Ephram then takes three sessions to find the seam, Cael watches “the fortnight that came after” (line 153), and the narration reaches a tenth training day (line 177), yet Brom explains the result on the thirtieth evening (line 185). Only eight calendar days separate the stated dates. This is a verified manuscript contradiction, not a plan preference. Repair the date or the stated duration, then check the joins into the thirty-third-day Lattice visit and forty-eighth-day baseline.

2. **Chapter 9's practice rule contradicts the bout shown and filed.** Line 47 says bouts consist of three exchanges. Lira's bout then contains four scored exchanges and ends 3–1 (lines 55–77). The cleanest repair is to state the actual winning rule—apparently first to three clean touches—without changing the well-developed four-exchange action.

3. **Chapter 10's cost arithmetic names the wrong percentage.** Four bursts leaving the hip where three did on stone (lines 103–115) means the per-burst after-cost is about 25% lower, or that the same bodily bill buys about one-third more work. “A third off, session for session” conflates those two comparisons. Use one denominator consistently. Preserve the binding outcome: four free bursts on sprung timber and an unchanged landing beat.

## Repair brief

1. **Location:** Chapter 8, lines 3 and 153–185. **Observed issue:** a three-session discovery plus the following fortnight and a narrated tenth day cannot fit between the twenty-second morning and thirtieth evening. **Effect on the reader:** the dated academy chronology stops being trustworthy, which is especially damaging in a book built on counts, records, and measured intervals. **Proposed scope:** one local calendar repair—change the concluding day, the opening day, or the duration—followed by a join check against the thirty-third-day Lattice visit and forty-eighth-day baseline. **Strength to preserve:** the full false-feed learning sequence and Brom's recognition that his body is being taught a lie.

2. **Location:** Chapter 9, lines 47–77. **Observed issue:** the stated three-exchange practice rule conflicts with the four exchanges and filed 3–1 score. **Effect on the reader:** the first formal ladder bout appears to break a rule introduced only moments earlier, blurring whether Lira won by tactical adaptation or narrative convenience. **Proposed scope:** a single rule-line correction to the actual victory condition; do not shorten or rescore the bout. **Strength to preserve:** all four adaptations, especially the opponent's correct third-exchange adjustment and Lira's answer to it.

3. **Location:** Chapter 10, lines 103–115. **Observed issue:** four bursts costing what three used to is a 25% reduction per burst or a one-third increase in work for the same bill, not “a third off” on the same denominator. **Effect on the reader:** Cael's supposedly exact self-measurement contains avoidable arithmetic ambiguity, weakening the numerical authority of the later baseline. **Proposed scope:** revise only the comparison phrase so denominator and percentage agree; retain four free bursts and the unshortened landing beat. **Strength to preserve:** the larger discovery that a recorded bodily limit may partly belong to the room in which it was measured.
