## 8. Major fights and set pieces: planning allowances (private staging the author owns)

- **The cut (M3).** A hundred strides of road cut to a cart's width, with scree on both sides and a frozen runnel a stride wide down the middle. A sink lies fifty metres below on the north side, iced and in shadow; there are eight hounds. Positions: Brom on stone at the south edge with a rock; Vastin a stride behind him; Lira on the south scree; Seln on the north scree, unseen; Cael twenty strides west, past the sink; Karis, Oryn and the mule at the east end. The decisions: the wall goes up and everything turns to the loudest thing; Brom and Lira work its south end silently; Cael bursts **away**, then goes still on the scree as the lock beat lands; the pack returns to the wall and breaks on it; the heap; the runnel. The opponent learns too: the den has learned the road. Costs to carry: Brom's foot; Vastin's knee (a month); Cael's burst bill.
- **The alley (M5).** Twenty-two steps in five flights, a stride and a half wide, walled three men high with no doors.
