## 3. New entry point for the densely tracked scene

### Chapter 12, scene 3 (`chapter-12.md:101–153`)

Enter through Cael losing track of Seln rather than through Seln becoming still. Let Karis finish the copy and ask her practical question about the bar; only afterward have Cael discover that Seln has moved to the window or doorway. Give Seln his minimal line first, then let Cael infer that the date—not the bar—moved him. End on a concrete choice by Cael, such as leaving the date uncopied in his own log, rather than on a catalogue of other unsolved things.

That order would be: Karis copies → the room resumes a small practical action → Cael notices Seln is elsewhere → Seln gives the withholding line → Cael identifies the date as the trigger → Cael chooses not to pursue or record it. It breaks the source chain of normal stillness → abnormal stillness → silent sum → unreadable look → Cael's ignorance → mental filing while preserving every required fact.
