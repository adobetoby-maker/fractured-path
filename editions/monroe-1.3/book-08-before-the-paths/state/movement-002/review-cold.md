# Movement 002 review — simulated cold read

This is a **fresh-context simulated cold read**, not a real audience measurement. I read only the six supplied manuscript chapters, Chapters 7–12, in order. I had no canon packet or drafting history. The two reader perspectives below are editorial lenses, not demographic research or a sales forecast.

The supplied movement appears complete as a six-chapter unit, although it intentionally ends one morning before Cael's promised ruling.

## Unscored reader response

My interest first caught on the concrete danger of the thawed river, then caught decisively at the Chapter 7 board scene when Seln explains that the apparently mild gate can freeze the crew's standing and send Cael back to a frame. The phrase "To a frame" gives the institutional threat a human body. From there, Lira's decision to use both hands, her bout with Cael, and the first ferry crossing form a clean rising line. The ferry failure is the movement's strongest suspense sequence: every solution grows from an already visible skill, injury, tool, or local fact. Vastin making himself into an anchor without any declared power is an especially satisfying entrance.

Interest slipped in two places. The opening pages require the reader to absorb ferry mechanics, the day count, several names, and the delayed crossing before the crew's personal stakes surface. Later, across Chapters 10–11, the distinctions among office, seat, seal, instrument, restriction, function, layer, and archive arrive in a long run of exact dialogue. The scene remains intelligent and emotionally alive, but a cold reader can lose the current decision while following the institutional vocabulary.

Lira matters most to me in this movement. Her left hand is simultaneously injury, shame, tool, tactic, and proof that she is still one person rather than "two sorts of girl." Vastin becomes nearly as important within a single crossing because his habits survive the loss of his office, and because the story lets his empty hands hurt more than a speech about lost status would.

I expect the next event to be Cael asking for hands at the plank and ruling on Treswick. I also expect Oryn to ask, rather than assume, whether she may examine Vastin's knee; Seln's recognition of the technician's date and Oryn's repeatedly swallowed sentence feel like the next two disclosures waiting behind that decision.

I would continue without hesitation. The movement pays off the boat, Lira's recovery work, Cael's practice, Seln's count, and Vastin's earlier moral choice while opening a specific next road rather than merely enlarging the mystery.

## Manuscript-only continuity and disclosure check

- **Canon:** No canon evidence was supplied, so there is no basis for declaring a canon violation. I found no apparent internal contradiction across these six chapters. Any agreement with an unseen plan is likewise not assessable.
- **Physical state:** Lira's left arm remains slow, weak, and weather-sore after the bout; her two-handed knot in Chapter 9 pays off practice without erasing cost. Brom consistently protects or compensates for his injured side. Vastin's bad knee, damaged hands, and incompletely opening left hand worsen plausibly through the crossing, meeting, vigil, and collapse. Cael's near-field visual blur after an extended read is carried into his recognition of Vastin and resolves gradually.

Oryn's reserve is described as finite and she refuses to spend it where ordinary care will work. Lira's gain is physical adaptation, not unexplained restoration. No decisive problem is solved by an unplanted ability.
- **Knowledge boundaries:** Vastin knows the five through the case and the misrouted broker's paper, not through unexplained access. Karis refuses to infer beyond the documents. Cael recognizes Vastin first by voice and stance while his sight is blurred, which the earlier encounters support. Seln's response to the technician's date signals private knowledge but does not distribute it to the others.
- **Reserved disclosures:** With no canon packet, reservations cannot be verified. On the page, the manuscript deliberately withholds what Seln recognizes in the date, what Oryn repeatedly stops herself from saying, the blacked-out correlation, the meaning of the Architect title, and what the Treswick archive contains. Those withholdings feel controlled. The bracketed production note in Chapter 12, however, is not a narrative withholding; it is unfinished manuscript text and prevents assessment of the exact date that triggers Seln.

## Repair brief

1. **Location:** Chapter 12, technician's return, immediately after “Deep layer. Sequence initiated.” **Observed issue:** A bracketed `TIER-B` coordinator instruction remains in the manuscript where the protected Compact date should be. **Effect on the reader:** It breaks immersion at the most sensitive reveal and makes Seln's recognition impossible to evaluate from the page. **Proposed scope:** Replace only the placeholder with the approved protected date text, then recheck Seln's reaction and all date references for legibility and consistency; do not expand the explanation. **Strength to preserve:** The black brush-bar, Karis's “not read” notation, and Seln's silent recognition create excellent restrained suspense.

2. **Location:** Chapters 10–11, from Vastin's “I hold no office” through the explanations of the deep layer, Treswick archive, pending restriction, and what can cross the Line. **Observed issue:** Too many adjacent institutional distinctions must be retained before their immediate consequence is restated. **Effect on the reader:** A fluent younger reader—and some adult readers—may understand every sentence yet temporarily lose which facts change the crew's choice and which merely establish provenance. **Proposed scope:** Make a surgical clarity pass: add or sharpen at most two brief consequence anchors at the dialogue turns, and clarify any ambiguous referent; retain the evidence chain and do not compress the inquiry into summary. **Strength to preserve:** Each speaker's method of knowing is distinct, and the procedural exactness is a source of character, tension, and originality.

3. **Location:** Chapter 7, opening launch through Seln's forty-first notch and the first explicit statement of what may be waiting across the river. **Observed issue:** The movement opens with a dense cluster of ferry procedure, thaw chronology, camp growth, names, and counting before the crew's personal danger is named. **Effect on the reader:** A cold reader can admire the setting while remaining uncertain which pressure should organize attention. **Proposed scope:** Use a light orientation pass only—bring Cael's pending ruling or his attention to the far bank into sharper focus a little earlier, or trim a small amount of duplicate timing information. **Strength to preserve:** The practical ferry detail, the ferryman's dry voice, and the river's physical threat establish the material world that makes Chapter 9's rescue pay off.
