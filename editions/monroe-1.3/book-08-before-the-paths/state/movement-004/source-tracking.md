### Beat chains to break

- **26:55–81:** The required fact is only that Lira's ladder does not pull. The source tells it as Cael recalling her long history of being drawn upward → watching for the familiar forward shift of her weight → seeing that the shift does not come → watching her turn from the tiers to the box and the dark below. The manuscript moves the scene to the parapet and gives Lira new analysis, but preserves the same history → expected body cue → absent pull → box/descent chain. The protected log at line 161 is not part of this finding. Break the inheritance by opening on Lira's spoken price (“a hill with a tax on it”), letting her shoulder the box before Cael understands, and placing any mention of her old forward-lean or registry paper only after she has entered the cut.

- **26:183–223:** The source opens the yard door and produces the clerk with his writing instrument, then has him scan the loaded party, hesitate over the bodily irregularity that might make him ask, refrain, return to the form, write *four*, and admit them. The manuscript changes the clerk, the person who catches his eye, and his reason for silence, but splits that same door/opening → book and pen → scan of hands and loads → almost-question → refusal to ask → written count → admission sequence across short paragraphs. The protected “Four hands” exchange at line 217 is not part of this finding. Break it by having the clerk already seated at the hatch with the green book open, entering the matter before he looks at anyone; let him make the count first, then notice Vastin only after the line is blotted, when asking can no longer control entry.
