Cael matters most. His fear is not merely that the station will expose him, but that the one person capable of looking correctly might look up from the evidence and see him; that is an intimate and specific threat. Lira becomes nearly as important through action rather than declaration: she sits outside the hedge, covers the window, walks between Cael and the square, and finally looks from the top without being claimed by it. Vastin supplies the movement’s strongest turn, moving from the man who classified others to a nameless “hand” who deliberately does not ask.

I would continue. The close—“The tiers closed, and the dark opened”—pays off the movement’s descent through social and physical layers while opening a concrete mystery.
