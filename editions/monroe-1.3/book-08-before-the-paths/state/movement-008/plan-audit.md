# Plan audit — Movement 8, “The Sunken Span”

## Verdict: REPAIR — bounded line repair

The movement is structurally sound: the seven-day leg, the four fight exchanges, the no-death win, the Span inspection, the blanket work, the three cutaways, and the handoff to the pass are all present in the required order. It is not ratifiable as printed because ten protected-text markers remain, the seep adds a forbidden thirty-third deployment, the 305th pages perform and display date arithmetic the edition expressly withholds, Karis's cutaway joins the leaf and the lamps finding in her interior, one annual-visit inference contradicts closed Movement 7, Lira remembers a plank reaction that the closed chapter cut, Oryn is not given the thirteenth as the packet requires, and the protected S19 log has a word-level italics error. These are local repairs; no chapter or fight restaging is required.

After the fixes below, the movement ends with thirteen fragments, deployment **thirty-two**, purse **123**, twelve people and the mule under the shale step on the night of the 305th, Oryn five days past her cairn, and Teague and Vastin still untold of the thirteenth.
