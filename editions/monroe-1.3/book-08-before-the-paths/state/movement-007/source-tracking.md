### 44:133 — the ledger becomes right-way-up

The required fact is only that Cael reads Pike's line right-way-up, but the manuscript retains the source's fuller chain in the same order: Pike writes the three-part entry; Cael reads it from the keeper's side; he sets that orientation against a lifetime of reading across the plank with the writing turned away; then he realizes that the change happened inside Treswick without a conscious decision. Moving the realization into Cael's physical position and adding Pike's exchange vary the surface, but do not break the inherited write → correctly oriented read → old habit → unchosen change sequence.

To break it locally, let Cael read the line before he notices which side of the bench he occupies. Pike can then make him stay for a practical reason—blotting, the empty columns, or the season leaf—and the orientation realization can arrive later, after another subject has intervened. Alternatively, retain the immediate realization but remove the retrospective lifetime comparison, leaving the change to register only as an unfamiliar bodily ease.

### 46:181 — ordered release from the deep reading

The source ends the reading by taking Oryn's contact away in a deliberate order: the reading hand leaves, the last contact withdraws slowly, she resumes ordinary breath and sits before speaking, while Cael waits for release. The manuscript keeps that micro-sequence: the breastbone hand lifts first, the supporting hand comes out from under his neck by degrees, Oryn stands only long enough to find the keg, then sits and recovers before giving the finding. The breath count, Lira's water, and the changed hand positions are new; the bodily exit and its order are not.

To break it locally, enter after contact has already ended, with Lira putting the cup into Oryn's hands or with Oryn giving the finding before she sits. Her exhaustion can become the aftershock of saying it rather than the bridge between touch and speech. Either order preserves the depth and cost of the reading without replaying the source's withdrawal → seat → statement progression.
