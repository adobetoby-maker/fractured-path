Days on the page: 294 (the Deepstacks; the hatch; the yard), 295 (the rope-walk; the outer gate; the bridge; the posts; the rise; the fork-stone; the spring), 296 (the cut's head; the cut; the cairn; the halting-ground), 297 (the western landing; the ferry; Lowmarch at dusk; Teague's window; the long room), 298 (the meadow; the back room; the south road; the fork above the mill; Millrace; the readings; the tellings; the deep reading; the cart-shed), 299 (the ford). No season, month or weekday name; no figure for the technician's date; no date printed for Pike's season line; no count of years since the Kindling.

**Where everyone is.** Twelve and a mule, in file, leaving the notch's head by the ruts that do not go back to Lowmarch: Seln ahead; the tall man; Teague, with Cael at his left shoulder a stride back; Lira; the young one; Karis, the two healers and the brown mule in the middle; Brom; Vastin a stride behind him; the bow last. Cleon is under Treswick with the leaf, the chronicle and the board. Pike is at Lowmarch.

**Cael.** Nineteen. **Twelve fragments. Deployment thirty-two.** He was a lamp for ten days and has entered it. He did not look back at the crown from the bridge; the promise is kept and done, and Oryn has not been told in so many words that it was kept (he told her the two things he had written that he owed). He has told Oryn: the two self-readings of the 281st and why; the eleventh; the twelfth. He has not told her, or anybody: **the two words (*still*; *set*)**; the warning; the date. He holds Oryn's finding word for word and has not written it. He has written that one thing came under her hands, unasked, and four reasons for not writing what (the fourth "not mine to write"). He has told Lira that something came and that he has no name for it. He walks at Teague's left by the field's ruling and has said aloud that the field is Teague's. He lent the dividers twice and did not open them (the ruling of the 168th stands, a hundred and thirty days old on the 298th). He has told Vastin that what is kept from him is "six people's". The eleventh's debt stands. Body: sound; the eyes paid on the night of the 298th.

**Lira.** Wind; Iron, Rank One. No burst. **The arm is "an arm"** (day thirty-three out of the wool on the 298th; marker 13 holds the exchange). She taught the four hands to Teague's four and corrects the young one. She offered the leaf to Oryn (marker 16) and was declined. She saw Cael go stiff on the plank and has agreed to wait. She has read the 298th's noon log. Her registry paper is not mentioned.

**Brom.** Iron Skin; Copper. No grey. **Left shoulder mended by Oryn** on the 298th; **right re-tied by the older healer** in two yards; both arms come up to his shoulders on the 299th. He paid the rope debt and gave Pike the thumbed slip. **The purse: a hundred and twenty-seven.** Oryn's bowl is back with her.

**Karis.** Ember; Iron, Rank Three; no point struck. She has copied the leaf's back (her notebook) and holds Cleon's drawn copy (a roll of card in brown tape). She has the fourth line: the mark, over those who are kindled and what is set; three. She has the grade as three marks in three ruled boxes, the first empty; the four running numbers; a fifth place of the two-bar mark, older than two hundred and six, with no figure; the office's run from the fourth place to it. She has not said the office's compass point or its figure in lengths aloud. Five lines of the list are spelled and unturned; she has said none of its numbers aloud. Left palm: stitches drawn, in Oryn's linen; she writes and draws with it; she is not to lean on it. The hide has a clean sheet pinned to its eastern edge, with a prick at the divider's point and a pencil line ruled through and a hand's breadth past it.

**Seln.** Shadow; Bronze. Out front. He has seen a horseman who behaves as a watcher would, and a riderless horse in the alders; he puts it no higher. He stood again at the spring's basin with his back to the slab. The case is on him, shut; not mentioned.

**Vastin.** No office; no age stated. **Right knee: day twenty of thirty on the 299th**; Oryn's linen and two new laths; the slat is off; the month stands as counted. He stood on the leg once, with the stick up, at the line in the cut (the 296th). He rode the dray's tail-board for three days at a copper a day, and the salt cart on the 298th at Teague's ruling; he walks every hill. He kept the tally of the fifty-two sheets and signed it V. He sat before the six at the halting-ground and laid no copper. He took Oryn's "Stand there." without a clause and stood an hour. He has said "Noted." once (45:165; marker 15 may hold a second). He told Oryn of the wall of the eleventh day himself. He is on Teague's count of ten as one of "your six"; that is a count for a contract; he has not asked to join the six and nobody has spoken of it. He has been offered a seat at the shoulder hold's fire and has not answered. **He does not know** the mechanism, the eleventh, the twelfth, the two words, the date, or what is under the bar; he knows that he is not told, and that it is "six people's". He stopped the sentence about the mark twice more, without a word.

**Oryn.** Tide; Iron, Rank Four, provisional. At Millrace on the 298th, thinner; nobody has died under her hands. She has read Brom's shoulders, Lira's arm, Karis's hand and Vastin's knee, each asked first; and Cael, asked first, for forty minutes, to her own floor. **Her finding is given** (46:197). She knows of twelve, and whose the eleventh and twelfth were (the twelfth's source as "a man under the registry"); she knows what the twelfth reads and that it has not been turned inward by Cael's will; she felt "something turn over, out at the edge" and thought nothing of it. **She has not heard the leaf.** She has not been shown the grade on the page. She walks east with the mule in the middle of the file, on her own terms (marker 11), and has sat in the sixth place (marker 12).

**Teague and his crew.** The term is taken as a contract: ten on the count (Cael, Lira, Brom, Karis, Seln, Vastin; Teague, the tall man, the bow, the young one); shares by the count; the field his; the reading Karis's. His wrist was used once, on a cart, clicked, and is wrapped in linen under the leather. He has not said "Noted." The tall man, the bow and the older healer have said nothing to the six. **What his four have been told:** that an office's survey was read at Treswick and gave a bearing (the long room); that the old man's wall has a month on it and is not stood before the month is out (Cael); the four numbers on the hide and "three days past the iron" (Teague, at the door; he came for the distance and did not look at the marks). **Not told:** the leaf or any word of it; the list; the mechanism; any fragment; the date; who Vastin was. Nobody in his crew is named.

**Pike's book and the cloth.** **Thirteen lines:** the twelfth is the clearance (*twenty; paid*); the thirteenth is *Treswick. Survey read. Bearing.* with its last two columns empty. **The season line is dated** (a day in its ruled space; no figure). Eleventh on the cloth.

**Cleon.** Rune, Bronze. He turned the skin, drew its back, gave Karis his copy, took his lamp back, and said his two lines at the iron. He did not look at Cael. He is not above ground.

**The faction.** One horseman, unidentified, a mile behind on the 296th; a riderless horse on the western bank on the 297th. Nothing east of the river in these chapters.

**Objects and money.** **130 → 128** (the 295th: sixty yards of four-strand, two marks) **→ 127** (the 298th: meal and a flitch at the mill, one mark). Coppers, which the closed text keeps apart from the purse: Vastin's three to the dray's carter, his own; the ferry's fare waived; the hearth at Lowmarch and the salt carter's seat not charged on the page. The reserve sealed in the third box. **The rope debt is paid.** The handcart went back swept. On the mule: three boxes (the fifty-two sheets in their order, with the tally), the flat, a sack of the mill's meal, Oryn's pannier and canvas. With Karis: the hide with its pinned sheet, the Ternhall brass, her notebook, Cleon's roll, the sheet of three boxes, Vastin's copied page. With Cael: Hesk's book, the dividers shut in it, the fair copy of the leaf. With Vastin: the tarred kit case (stake thirty-two was taken out on the 298th and laid on the table; its return to the case is not stated). The skin leaf, the chronicle and the black oak board are under Treswick.

**Who knows what.** The mechanism: six, as before; Cleon knows how one fragment came. The twelfth: the six and Cleon, **Oryn now among them**. The eleventh: Cael, Lira (the log), **and Oryn**; not Vastin. The warning: Cael and Cleon; Cael has written that a fourth reason exists and is not his to write. **The two words: Cael alone.** That something came on the plank: Cael, Lira; Oryn only as "one of yours, in its sleep". The leaf: unchanged (not Oryn, Teague or Pike). The list's fourth line and the grade: the six and Vastin; Teague in part. The date: Cael and Seln, each alone.

**Open threads.** Sixteen markers. Two findings that do not agree, and one of them in nobody's book. A warning that has begun to lean. A box ruled for a mark that no sheet carries. A fifth place with no figure for its age and two signs unread in its corner. Five lines of a list spelled and not turned. A compass point and a measure that Karis has not said aloud. A line ruled past a point. A debt to a man who walks a stride behind Brom. A seat at a hold's fire, offered and not answered. A horse in the alders. A wrist in linen. Oryn's road, which runs with theirs for a day and a half.

## Owner flags

1. **Sixteen markers** (table above). Three carry choices that change a scene's sense and want a ruling before the joins are smoothed: 43:71 and 43:87 (who speaks at the line in the cut); 44:195 (who says that the wall is still the wall); 45:379 (who owns the four lines that close the knee's reading and the chapter). **One unprotected overlap run stands** inside marker 13 (45:329), being the packet's own line.
2. **Units placed on inference, not on a pinned speaker:** 45:243 / 45:247 ("Eleven off the ridge." to the woman with the net; "I know." to Oryn, on Book 7 chapter 47's form); 45:263 ("Stand there." to Oryn); 45:343 ("Who sewed this." to Oryn). Are these as the plan has them?
3. **"He" is Teague (43:35–43).** The factor says that the man first on the one-armed man's cloth brought the salt up and foretold the answer. Is that the plan's "he"? If it is Pike or another, 43:39–43 and Teague's window at 44:13–27 (where he hears of the wall from the factor) both need re-staging, and "a wall's count" loses the reason I have given it.
4. **The factor's offer (43:25).** A standing wage for the five and a seat at the hold's fire for Vastin, which he does not answer. Does the seat sit too near Book 9's question of where Vastin belongs? It can be cut to the wage alone without touching the protected line.
5. **How Vastin gets east (42:31–63; 45:189–193).** He rides the rope-walk's dray for three days at his own copper, and the salt cart to the fork above the mill on Teague's ruling; he walks every hill, the cut, and the notch. The boxes stay on backs (§13). The packet's "the road and the cut walked again on a bad knee" is met at the rise and the cut only. Is a ridden road acceptable?
6. **Money.** The rope at two marks (42:21–23) and the mill's meal at one (45:283) are my sums, chosen to land the calendar's 127 on the 298th; the ferry's fare is waived (43:239); the hearth at Lowmarch on the 297th is not paid for on the page. Should that night cost anything?
7. **What the list says (41:57–59; 41:125–135; 45:29).** Nine lines; the first four are the face's four things; the fourth puts one word, the mark, over "those who are kindled" and "what is set", with the number three. That is the whole of the list's content on the page, and the word *set* is the one this edition uncovered in Movement 6. Is that more, or other, than the plan's "Cleon's list puts the kindling mark and the sheet mark in one line"?
8. **Ages "by first sheet" (45:77–109).** I have made them running series numbers at the foot of each abstract (61, 94, 138, 206), a sheet a year, with Vastin giving the form and nothing of who drew the first. It follows on the page that an office has had a yearly sheet of the fenced place for two hundred and six years, and that the six rows were only what had been held back (Seln, 45:99). Does that say "they knew" earlier than the plan means it said (the crest, the 303rd)?
9. **The fifth place on the 298th (45:57–63; 45:101–109).** One group, six sheets; a stroke and two bars; no figure in its corner; "older than two hundred and six" and no more. Karis says that she saw its square at the spring and kept silent. Is that an acceptable account of Movement 3's silence, and is the withheld figure right for the 305th's "documented four hundred years back"?
10. **The office's distance and the dividers (45:113–151; 46:321–335).** A run on the two-bar sheets from the fourth place: a compass point and a measure in lengths, neither printed; "three days past the iron, as we walk, a little less". Hesk's dividers are opened for the first time on a distance by Karis, on Cael's loan, at Lowmarch, and again at the ford. Movement 8's "by the office's distance" and the Span-to-court days should be checked against "three days… a little less".
11. **Stake thirty-two** is taken from the kit case and laid on the table (45:35–39). It has still never been driven.
12. **The horseman (43:117–135; 43:157–165; 43:245).** One distant rider on the western bank and a riderless horse at the landing; nothing identified. If no watcher is wanted before the Span's, the three passages lift out, and the second half of the 296th's "Known" (43:193) goes with them.
13. **Teague's window (44:3–97).** He does not know who Vastin was and declines to ask; he reads slips and ledgers; "the boy" is the young one in his head; the young one "has a Path", unnamed. The bow speaks to Teague (not to the six). Is anything here more than Teague may know?
14. **Pike (44:115; 44:153–157).** The twelfth line's wording is mine. "Dated" is rendered as a day in the ruled space by his own book's reckoning, with nothing printed. "A hundred and sixty-eight days" counts from first snow on the 129th.
15. **The four heads after marker 3 (44:175–187)** are in my words, as Teague's. When the protected term is restored they may double it; they can be cut back to Brom's question and Cael's answer.
16. **Force seen (44:225–255).** A root of the ash for the ground behind him; "a ton and more of oak and salt"; Brom's memory of a Force at a quarry. Cael's log of the line and its cost is in my words and keeps clear of the thirteenth's.
17. **The file (44:283–291)** fixes a march order that Movement 8 inherits, with Cael at Teague's left a stride back (as that packet has him at the Span).
18. **Millrace's outcomes (45:305–317; 45:349; 45:377).** Brom's left shoulder mended and both arms to his shoulders by morning; the stitches drawn on the eighth day; the slat replaced by two laths and "the month stood as it had been counted". The book's end has Karis's hand "bound" and scarring, and Vastin on day twenty-nine: both still hold.
19. **Oryn is told the eleventh (46:35)** as well as the twelfth, since she would have found it under her hands. She is asked nothing about Vastin's debt.
20. **Oryn's answer on the inward turn (46:59–63)** forbids it only while her hands are in him, and leaves the road to him. Movement 9's log has "Cleon said don't" and no word of hers; is her leave here too wide, or too narrow?
21. **Oryn half-notices (46:171–175), and Lira sees (46:247–259).** "Something turned over… one of yours, in its sleep"; and "You went stiff." The two words are told to nobody, and the page says so; but two people now know that something happened. Is that inside "told to no one" and "keeps the change out of his face"? Either beat can be cut alone.
22. **The reasons are written in the book (46:267–279)** without the thing itself, and the fourth is "not mine to write". Lira reads that book. If the entry should be kept out of the book altogether (as pole two was), it can become a thing that he sets in order in his head.
23. **"Twelve. And a mule." is Seln's (46:297),** by my choice; the plan gives the count and no speaker.
24. **"Known"** is entered under the 295th, 296th and 297th (42:289; 43:193; 44:209). The 298th has the fixed log and no such line.
25. **Cael to Vastin, "It's six people's. That's why." (42:157).** He confirms that something is withheld and whose leave it wants. Is that more than Vastin should have before the debt is paid?
26. **The promise.** Cael does not tell Oryn in so many words that he kept it (he tells her what he had written that he owed). If the plan wants it said at the mill, one line in 46.1 will carry it.
