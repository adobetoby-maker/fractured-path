## Reader Standard

The underlying narrative passes the content standard for a fluent thirteen-year-old:

- no profanity, obscenity, sexual content, or innuendo;
- violence and injury have fear, cost, recovery time, and consequence without gore;
- honesty, consent, restraint, duty, care, and keeping terms are dramatized at personal cost rather than preached;
- cruelty is not rewarded, and power is never treated as consequence-free;
- humor is warm and stops when pain enters the room.

The grade-level formulas do not by themselves prove accessibility. The sentence-level language is very easy by Flesch measures, but the grade solution asks the reader to hold several documents, marks, ages, sites, and inferences at once. The scene succeeds because Karis distinguishes observation from meaning and because concrete objects remain on the table. The inline notes destroy that support at exactly the moment the inference needs to be stated. They are therefore also a Gate 27 and audio-first failure, not merely a typesetting blemish.

Outside the blocks, referents and dialogue are generally clear by ear. Cleon/Vastin are differentiated through location and task in chapter 41; the movement's many unnamed roles remain identifiable through repeated physical or occupational tags. No name collision creates a material audio problem here.
