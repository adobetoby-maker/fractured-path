## Decisions made before drafting

1. **Deployment stays at thirty-two.** Nothing is opened outward. The twelfth is acquired; what it shows him in the minute of its coming (the fifth row; the board) is the acquisition, not a use. Afterward it *leans* at made things and he learns not to let it look; two glances that he catches too late (a table's joints, a hinge) are logged "not counted", as trials have been all book. It is never turned on a person, on the station, on the leaf, or on himself.
2. **How the twelfth behaves (mine).** The other eleven ask a question. This one does not ask; it leans, as a head turns toward a thing. What it gives is single plain words of the notice's own kind: *bears*, *borne*, *nothing*, *sits*, *goes*.
