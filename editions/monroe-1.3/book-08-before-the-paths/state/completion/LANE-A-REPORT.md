## 5. How the protected text was verified

1. **Baseline diff.** Every chapter was diffed against `pre-lanes/` with `diff` (never git). The 58 hunks are in `laneA/all-hunks.diff`; I read each one again at word level after the last edit.
2. **Protected-line list.** `laneA/protected_lines.py` lists every manuscript line that shares a run of six or more words with BOOK_MAP §10, matches a line of `protected-patterns.txt`, or is an italic/document line (145 lines in ch1–30). After the pass the same 145 lines are present with identical text.
3. **Hunk check.** `laneA/check_hunks.py` tests each hunk against that list. No hunk is on a protected line. Two hunks are within one paragraph of one, and both are narration only: ch4:261 (the paragraph after a log line) and ch21:231 (the paragraph before the protected log). See §7.
4. **Whole-chapter invariants.** For each of the thirty chapters, the full sequence of quoted speech, the full sequence of italic lines and the full sequence of digits are identical before and after. So no spoken word, no log, letter, slip or document line and no figure changed anywhere, protected or not.
5. **Beside-exchange rule.** Short protected lines that an n-gram cannot catch ("Four hands," / "Four." / "Right.", "Off floors,", "Noted", "I looked at it.") were handled by hand while reading: I changed nothing in or next to those exchanges, including their attributions. Six narration tags inside ordinary (unprotected) dialogue paragraphs were changed (ch13:201, ch14:47, ch18:157, ch20:279, ch23:309, ch28:135); the speech in each is untouched.
