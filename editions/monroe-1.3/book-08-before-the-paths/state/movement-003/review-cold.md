# Movement 003 — simulated cold read

This is a **fresh-context simulated cold read**, not a real audience measurement. I read only the compiled manuscript material supplied for this review: seven files, chapters 13–19, in order. No editorial canon evidence was supplied, so the continuity section below distinguishes internal consistency from verified canon. The movement appears complete as a movement: it closes the clearance job, changes the travelling party, and lands at the threshold of Treswick while deliberately leaving the archive as the next movement's event.

## Unscored reader response

Interest caught in chapter 13 when Karis and Brom each claim the border-road clearance with “Ours.” The choice solves Cael's immediate problem—how to move toward the unnamed town without pretending he has made a larger vow—and gives the journey a practical price, danger, and social procedure. Vastin following without invitation adds a second, quieter hook.

The strongest run is chapters 14–16. The reconnaissance makes the cut comprehensible, the wrong plan grows from evidence the crew genuinely has, and the reversal—“They did not go any longer to where a declaration was made. They went to where it ended”—is both fair and alarming. Vastin's Iron Wall, its tactical uselessness until Cael changes the pack's reason, Lira's recovering hand, and the consequences afterward all pay off rather than merely decorate the fight.

Interest slipped in the middle of chapter 17, during the extended analysis of the survey forms. The discoveries are good, but marks, columns, hands, calendars, sites, sheets, the hide, the stake, and the unreadable language accumulate faster than a cold reader can rank them. The visible `[[TIER-B: ...]]` coordinator notes in chapters 16, 17, and 19 create sharper breaks: they momentarily replace story with production instructions and sometimes remove the speaker from emotionally important exchanges.

Vastin matters most to me in this movement. His movement from office-bound authority toward consent, instruction, fallibility, and membership is specific and costly. Cael remains the center because his restraint links every strand: he refuses a premature vow, refuses an unearned verb between the deep-layer paper and his own date, and finally chooses to touch the page. Karis is the person whose need most clearly drives the next destination.

I expect the next major event to be entry into Treswick and an attempt to reach Cleon and the archive without Cael approaching the station, with Vastin's habits threatening to expose the group at the gate. I would continue. The movement pays off its local job completely while opening the archive question, the fifth site, Cael's matching date, and the social danger of the town.

## Internal continuity and boundary check

### Canon

No canon packet or editorial context was supplied. I therefore found **no verified canon violation** and cannot certify canon adherence. The manuscript is internally consistent on the evidence available, apart from unresolved production placeholders discussed below.

### Physical state

- Lira's recovery is carried visibly: she votes with a shaking left arm in chapter 13, says she can stand on scree “with two arms” in chapter 14, holds the hound slowly in chapter 15, is priced at fifteen days in chapter 19, and carries the case in her left hand afterward.
- Brom's bitten foot receives a ten-day estimate in chapter 16; at the next evening Oryn correctly calls it nine days. His older shoulder binding remains protected, and he shifts the heaviest box to his left side in chapter 19.
- Vastin's knee is given thirty days in chapter 16. On day 280 Oryn calls it the first day with twenty-nine after it; on day 281 she says “Twenty-eight.” He uses the stick, does not kneel or run, and is prevented from lifting a box. This count is coherent.
- Oryn's expenditure after treating Vastin leaves her grey and counting to fifty; the later decision not to read Cael while tired respects that depletion.

Confidence: high for internal continuity; no external canon comparison was possible.

### Power limits

- Stillhounds track Path discharge rather than ordinary sound, adapt to repeated declarations on the ice, ignore quiet bodies, and lose the signal when standing in water. These rules are planted before each decisive use.
- Iron Wall is fixed to Vastin's feet, disappears if he shifts, drains him severely, and is tactically bypassable. Its strength does not erase its positional weakness or physical cost.
- Cael's Ember produces reflected pain in the old palm weal; Wind moves him ten strides but locks him for one count. He wins the turn by choosing the destination and trusting Vastin's Wall, not by ignoring the lock.
- Cael does not automatically acquire Iron Wall despite proximity to the declaration. The text notes the absence rather than silently changing the apparent acquisition rule.

No internal power-limit contradiction was found. Confidence: high.

### Knowledge boundaries

The movement is unusually disciplined here. Karis stops herself before turning a felt comparison into a claim; Seln separates the five absences on the forms from speculation; Cael refuses to put a verb between the deep-layer return and his own classification date; and Vastin catches himself classifying restricted material under authority he no longer possesses. The distinction between a fact, an inference, and an institutional habit remains legible.

One clarity pressure remains in chapter 17: the reader must simultaneously track five sites, four known rings, a fifth class mark, two date systems, an unreadable script, a surviving field book, abstracts, and a censored deep-layer return. These do not contradict one another, but their relative importance is not always refreshed at the moment of discovery. Confidence: high.

1. **Location:** Chapters 16, 17, and 19, every `[[TIER-B: ...]]` block. **Observed issue:** Six coordinator placeholders remain in the manuscript, including missing protected wording and/or speaker attribution. **Effect on the reader:** Immersion breaks at climactic emotional and expository beats; some exchanges cannot be understood or read aloud as finished dialogue. **Proposed scope:** Resolve only the specified protected lines and tags, then remove the brackets and instructions; verify the surrounding paragraph joins without otherwise rewriting the scenes. **Strength to preserve:** The understated, character-specific dialogue and the restraint around belonging, authority, and promises.
