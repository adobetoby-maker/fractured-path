### Beat chains to break

- **17:89:** The source places Karis's finger near documentary evidence and immediately emphasizes that it is above, not touching, the sheet. The manuscript moves the gesture to the ruled remark but keeps the same near-evidence → explicit non-contact sequence and much of the sentence frame. Break it by letting the pencil or a pebble locate the remark, by having her touch and then withdraw, or by putting the interpretation before any physical gesture.

- **18:139:** The source names the knowledge as the first thing Cael has possessed without writing down, then lets him discover that he can carry it. The manuscript keeps unprecedented unrecorded knowledge → discovery of capacity/ease in the same order, changing possession/carrying into knowing/not entering and fear at its ease. Break it by opening on his fear at the blank line, returning the book first, and only later contrasting this choice with his lifelong habit of entry.

- **19:33:** The source declines to identify the city, says the particular city does not matter, and collapses all cities into one healer's experience. The manuscript keeps does-not-matter-which → all-one-city in the same words and order, adding “different rivers.” Break it by entering through the patient's tier column or the untiered woman's bed, then reveal only afterward that Oryn refuses to name the city because the system, not the place, is the point.

No other passage met the threshold. The slip, road doctrine, cut terrain and positions, Iron Wall mechanics, burst/lock/heap sequence, costs, spring readings, short row and placed sheet, Kindling-date sum, Seln's lesson, fork terms, patient pricing, departure, and first view of Treswick all follow required plan events.
