## Decisions made before drafting

1. **West is a job.** Cael rules on a slip and says so: he can price a cut, and he will not vow a town. The boxes and the papers come because nothing of theirs is left in a store. Nobody votes on the town.
2. **The rope is not bought.** The broker's cordage is three-strand. Brom will not pay a debt in a rope that was not asked for. The purse stays at 118 until the factor pays; the debt stands.
3. **Deployment moves to thirty-two** on the 279th: one Ember point at the mark (the one who is meant to be loud), and one Wind burst, away. Nothing else is opened. No eleventh comes.
4. **The technician's date is never printed.** The office that keeps the stations opens its year on a different day from the seat's registry, so a station's "year and day" must be turned before it can be laid beside a registry date; Vastin gives the difference while reading the abstracts for procedure, and the page does not give the number. Cael turns it and comes out on the date that has stood at the head of his own file on every desk since his Kindling. No span is computed. He does not write it.
5. **The short count has a cause.** Seln has drawn the technician's sheet out of the second row to do the turn in daylight; Karis then hands that row to Cael to be counted a second time. It comes out one under her figure. No figures for the rows are printed.
6. **Markers, not guesses**, wherever the plan quotes a line in part or leaves its speaker unpinned (listed at the foot).
7. **Vastin does not sit** in front of the crew after the cut; every instruction he is given he qualifies before he keeps it.
8. **The shoulder hold's factor has no name and no pronoun on the page.**

## Ground for the cut (one picture)

The border road climbs west. Below the shoulder hold it goes through a spur by a cut a hundred strides long and a cart wide, rising to the west. The south side is a bank of scree thirty feet high, with the hold's sheep-walk along its lip. The north side is a low bank; beyond it the ground falls fifty-five yards of scree to a sink, a round hollow where water goes into the hill, iced and in shadow. A seep comes out at the cut's head and runs down the middle of the road in a runnel a stride wide, which freezes every night and does not thaw in that shade. Three carts lie broken at the cut's foot, where things that slide on the ice arrive.

Stations: Karis, Oryn and the mule at the east mouth (the foot). The line, sixty strides up, abreast of the sink: Brom on a flat stone at the road's south edge with a rock; Vastin a stride behind him; Lira on the south scree above; Seln over the north bank, unseen. Cael's mark is twenty strides west of the line.

## Chapter 13 — Ours (day 278) · Cael ≈4,850

1. **The plank (first light).** The page still untouched on its oilcloth; Karis packs it. Pike pins the slip that came over in yesterday's bag: *Clearance. The cut below the shoulder hold, on the border road, two days west.* Twenty. Vastin sits on the settle at the door end of the eave; he is on no sheet. Everybody reads which road it is. **"Ours" (Karis). "Ours" (Brom).** Karis has never claimed a job before.
2. **Hands.** Cael gives the ruling that he promised, in daylight: he is ruling on a cut and says why. Lira: she saw the end of that move the night before. Five hands. Pike will not write a clearance taken without a healer named. Oryn, from the wash-house door: the mill has waited a cold term; she will go round by the top. **Pike writes a sixth line for the healer**, the first column he has ever had for her. Vastin pays the keeper for the loft and gets up.
3. **The landing.** Teague's salt goes down the planks on backs. "Which way?" / the cut, and what is past it; left with Pike. Teague looks at a pair of hands again. The tall man says nothing. The ferry takes people and led beasts; the mule and three boxes cross. Vastin pays his own copper and stands at the stern strap without touching it.
4. **The rope; the road.** On the western landing the broker turns back the sheet: three-strand. Brom puts the purse away. The border road; the order of the column; the hold comes down; Vastin last, where nobody has told him to walk. **The drill begins:** why nobody declares; the five findings; hands for words. His first mistake: he answers a hand aloud.
5. **Cold camp.** A carters' halting-ground with dead rings. The signals drilled at dusk; he stands in the middle of the road like a gatepost; he asks what a person does when one of the others is bitten, and says honestly that he cannot promise to watch it. Cael: "Then you'll stand behind Brom." Lira asks what he is on a plate; he says only that it is a Path for standing. Shadow at dusk, held. A seventh bowl; a copper left on a stone. Log.

## Chapter 14 — Carters Declare (day 279, morning to mid-afternoon) · Cael ≈4,850

1. **A grey thing (dawn).** The copper has frost on it. A stillhound keeps its distance beside the road for half a mile. Seln's hand; six people and a mule become posts; Vastin does it right, at the ditch, and is told so by nobody. What he learned: it did not look at him.
2. **The shoulder hold (noon).** The foot-track; the gate; the factor's terms. Three carts since the frost, a carter and two mules. The runnel. **"Carters declare, on the ice."** Twenty at the gate when the factor has walked a mule up it and down. A healer is fed.
3. **The lip.** Seln, Lira and Cael on their bellies on the south lip for two hours. The whole ground laid out. Five grey backs round the sink. The runs over the north bank. Three wrecks, all at the foot. Lira reads the scree; Cael reads the ice with his eyes and remembers a river.
4. **The plan.** Karis's five findings turned into stations. One of them will be loud, once, at a mark, and then leave the mark; the pack will come to an empty place and stand; three killed by silent hands, and the rest go. Cael takes the loud part: it is his to pay. Vastin's station is a stride behind Brom. He repeats his reservation. Oryn at the foot with her linen on the mule; Karis with the clock.
5. **Into the cut.** The ice underfoot; the stations taken; twenty strides alone. Ember, one point, on a twist of tow on the ice. The palm. He walks back down. Over the north bank they come: eight, and running, and they do not stop at the mark.

## Chapter 15 — The Loudest Thing (day 279, the fight) · Cael ≈4,900

1. **The wall.** The den has learned where a thing that declares on this ice ends up. Eight hounds come down the runnel at the line, and past the line is the foot of the cut. The crew are posts. Vastin steps into the road and brings the stick down. **Iron Wall, first shown:** intention, the act, what is seen, what it does (eight at a run, held), two feet from Cael's face.
2. **Rooted.** What it costs at once: his feet cannot leave the line, and he is the only sound in the cut. The pack learns the plane in two breaths and goes for the banks at its south end. Brom, outside it, with a rock and no grey: one. Lira on the scree with two arms, taking the ground from under them. A hound tries Brom's boot.
3. **The knee.** One comes over the end and takes the old man by the bad leg; he does not lift a foot. Lira comes down on it two-handed: the slow hand holds and the quick one strikes. Cael, two feet off, with nine loud things and a knife, sees what is wrong with a wall: the only reason to cross it is standing at its end.
4. **Away.** He bursts away, across the road and up the north bank behind the plane, and is a post for one count behind another man's wall. The pack turns back off the scree and comes down onto the ice at the new sound, with the plane across its road. It breaks there. The heap. **Seln in the heap.** The runnel's ice goes under them, and two stand in running water with nothing reaching them. Steel, and Brom's rock.
5. **Three.** Three get clear and go over the north bank and down, onto the sink's ice, which a week of thaw has rotted; it lets them through. Vastin lets go of the road. He does not sit. Cael waits for an eleventh thing and nothing comes. The count: five; three; a foot; a knee.

1. **The knee.** Oryn comes up the cut with her sleeves up. **She asks, and he says yes.** She binds it standing, and takes two fingers on his wrist. **"A month."** He walks back what he can. Brom's foot: "It's a boot."
2. **Twenty.** The factor walks a mule up the cut and down. [[the *clear because it's empty* line]]. Twenty on the gate's bar; a thumb on the slip. A hundred and thirty-eight. The Path is named: Iron Wall.

## Chapter 17 — Three Readers (day 280, the spring) · Cael ≈4,850

1. **Daylight.** Six rows under pebbles. Karis counts every row and writes the figures. Five groups of signs in every row: "Five." Four she can lay against the hide by their measures. Oryn will not come to the slab.
2. **Pattern (Karis).** Five columns: the day, the station, the measure, the instruments, the anomaly. Six years and no measure has moved by a stride. Three clerks' hands and one word. The instruments read true for every surveyor. One remark in six years: **"Behaves as device. Cannot be."** She begins a sentence about lamps and stops it with her rule.
3. **Omission (Seln) and procedure (Vastin).** No name, no cause, no column for why; after the year of the remark the form is printed without a space for remarks. Vastin: what an abstract is; the stations' year and the seat's, and the difference between them. **Reflex one: a directive. Reflex two: a classification.**
4. **The grade.** The box at the head of every sheet. The same stroke and bar on all four, and the fourth is twice the size and has the same. The fifth group's box is not filled as theirs is. [[*Two copies of one ignorance.*]] Nobody says who, or why.
5. **A sheet short.** Karis wants a second hand on each count. Cael has the second row. It comes out one under her figure. The cord in his stomach. A hand sets a sheet on the slab before him.

## Chapter 18 — A Year and a Day (day 280, the spring) · Seln ≈1,850 · Cael ≈3,000

1. **Seln (cutaway).** The night of the 277th from inside: a date under a pencil, and a file that he had copied the head of. The turn done in his head. This morning he draws the sheet out of the second row to do it properly, against the abstracts' own day column. It comes out as it did.
2. **Seln (cutaway).** What he will not do: say it; write it; guess. What it costs to give a thing without saying it. The case on his chest is not thought about. The boy counts a row short and goes white. He sets the sheet down, and goes to the water so as not to watch.
3. **The sum.** Cael reads the date for the first time. He turns it. He does it twice. He knows the day. He does not put a verb to it. His hand goes to Hesk's book and comes away. He lays the sheet back at the foot of the second row. "I've read it."
4. **"Draft nothing."** Vastin's third reflex: **"This has to go up."** Seln's first lesson to anybody, verbatim. Vastin looks, and says what is there.
5. **The close.** Karis ties the tapes. The protected exchange; Cleon is named for the first time; a day and a half.

## Chapter 19 — Different Verbs (day 280 evening; day 281) · Cael ≈4,900

1. **The fork-stone (evening).** A drovers' fold with a hearth-corner, where a fire has walls. **"I'm not an archive person."** The city she left; the sheet with a column for the patient's tier.
2. **The term.** **"Leaving and routing are different verbs. I'm doing the second."** Five holds, and down onto the mill from above. **"Three weeks"** (Seln). The sentence that she swallowed three times: the station. He writes it in the log and reads it back.
3. **Prices.** Each of them, and none lower than the rest: an arm of fifteen days; a foot and a knot that is not hers; a small woman who forgets to eat; a strap worn on one shoulder; a knee on the first of thirty days. Cael last, unread: [[*the one thing I'm walking up that road owing*]]. **"Bring me whatever walks out of that archive with you. All of it."**
4. **First light.** The boxes come off the mule and go onto three backs. [[*Not the steps … Not the square.*]] She goes north and does not look back. He turns at a quarter-mile.
5. **The rise.** A bluff, three walls in tiers, a pale steady light on the crown that he does not look at. He asks Karis for the oilcloth and lays his hand on the page. Five people and a marshal.
