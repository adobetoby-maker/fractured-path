# Editorial review — Movement 009, Chapters 54–60

**Outcome:** The movement's story, continuity, power accounting, calendar, and physical logic are sound. The pass is fully developed rather than compressed, its death changes the people who survive it, and the court delivers an earned progression revelation without crossing the book's disclosure ceiling. The text is **not release-ready**, however: ten literal `[[TIER-B: ...]]` editorial placeholders remain in the manuscript. They remove protected lines and whole protected exchanges at major turns, interrupt one-pass reading, are being counted as prose by the supplied metric run, and appear to be the cause of the one mandatory source-overlap finding.

## Unscored reader response

Interest caught immediately when Seln returned wet to the ankle and reduced the pass to the quiet ruling, “It is kept.” The revealed stone rigs turn a landscape description into a concrete trap, and the plan for a slanted wall makes the solution feel built from prior learning rather than granted by the plot. Interest peaked from the fifth volley through Cael's use of Force: the young one's decision is prepared, spatially intelligible, irreversible, and followed by enough grief that it does not read as a disposable combat death.

Interest slipped in two places. The first is artificial and decisive: each `[[TIER-B: ...]]` block replaces story with production notes, most damagingly at the end of Chapter 55, throughout the council and close of Chapter 58, and during both departures in Chapter 60. The second is organic and smaller: Chapter 58's circumference procedure, from “At the seventy-ninth length” through the division by three and a seventh, asks the reader to hold several measurements after two reflective chapters. The arithmetic pays off in the scale and perfection of the court, but this is the point at which a younger reader is most likely to skim.

Cael matters most because his restraint is now as consequential as his capability: he uses Force on stone rather than a person, stops the Rune reading at shape rather than meaning, crosses the cut alone, and refuses the next interpretive step even after “He fit.” The emotional fulcrum of the pass is Teague's unnamed young practitioner, whose whole-head nod in Chapter 54 and one-stride choice in Chapter 55 turn the wall doctrine into cost. I next expect Cael to tell Lira what the centre felt like and the westbound six to confront Vastin's unspoken place among them. I would continue.

The supplied run covers Chapters 54–60: seven chapters and 37,547 prose words. I used the supplied results without re-estimating them. The method is `editions/monroe-1.3/tools/formula_metrics.py` as documented in its module docstring:

- headings and exact scene-break lines are stripped; fenced system notices are excluded from sentence/readability calculations;
- each non-empty prose line is treated as a written paragraph;
- sentences split at `[.!?]` followed by whitespace and an opening quote/capital, with common abbreviations and ellipses protected; em-dash interruptions do not end a sentence;
- syllables use a vowel-group heuristic with silent-e and `-le` adjustments, treating proper nouns like other words;
