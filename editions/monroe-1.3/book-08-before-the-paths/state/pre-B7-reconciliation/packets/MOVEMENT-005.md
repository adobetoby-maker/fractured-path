## Where we enter

It is the 284th day, under Treswick. The lamp goes into the middle gallery. A light is already on at its end, and a man is already reading.

## What this movement is for

This is the archive's first week. Karis has found the room she has wanted all her life. A marshal learns his name is a blade with no handle. A craftsman shows how he reads a made thing's bones. Brom is offered the life his family wanted and picks up a box. Seln looks at the door his old trade would have used and decides nothing. Then the faction reaches inside the walls. A man with nothing behind him holds a line for people the Compact would restrict, and Cael comes away with the eleventh. We cross the first third into the middle, and progression language surfaces at the turning points: Rune explained, Iron Wall paid, the notice.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **The Deepstacks (284th).** The galleries, the shelving, the smell of a place where nothing has been decided. Cleon reads to the end of his page first. The sheets are laid out 11/29/12. Karis: "Older than [UNBOUND]." Cleon: "I know that hand." "Being believed … It's the end of being able to be wrong." "Give me a week." Cael finds himself, for the first time, unfiled.

"I'll think on it." He picks up the box on the bound shoulder, a debt Cael enters. On the stair: "You think Karis is right?" … "Good. I'd hate to have turned down a real job for a wrong hunch."

## What must remain true / withheld

- Cleon's Path is shown from outside only. The *inside* (the lean) and the acquisition belong to Movement 6. **No twelfth here.**
- The eleventh is acquired in the alley's quiet middle minute, after Iron Wall has been witnessed in use: the cut first, then this full minute. It is **not deployed and not told** to Vastin.
- Karis does not read the leaf yet. The bundle is on Cleon's shelf, a thing with a lean and no language.
- The faction expected a declaration and saw a wall and a rock in an alley. **It sees nothing of quiet ground.**
- No healer works inside the gate. Oryn is north.
- Vastin does not know the mechanism and his age is not stated. "Forty years" is his rounding.

## What the author owns

The galleries' geography. The order of the chamber days, and which small discoveries Karis makes on which day. Brom's yard and the broker's words. How the alley's minute feels from two feet below a plane. Where the humour sits: the lamp, the chairs with and without cushions, Brom's "Cart was heavier".

## Where we leave pressure

The faction knows where they sleep, and they know the crew knows it. A man who held a wall for them has asked for it to be entered, and Cael has entered more than the man knows. Karis has three days left of Cleon's week. A board leans, and a boy holding a lamp has started to notice which way.
