## Where we enter

It is the 291st day. After the alley the working party goes by the carts' street in daylight, with the boxes on a borrowed handcart. Karis's left hand is bound in a strip of Brom's shirt. Cael has eleven fragments and the eleventh has nothing to do. The clerk has not asked three things.

## What this movement is for

This movement holds the book's reveal and the arc's second pole. The twelfth arrives at a word and carries a warning. A parcel brings the second half of a competence, and the largest instrument on the continent does a wrong thing once, for him. Then the leaf is read aloud **once**, and that night Cael takes it on his feet: not excluded but *prevented*, and he happened anyway. **This is a deliberately quiet movement, with one set piece.** The reason is that the reveal must land as a discovery on paper by a researcher, and the fire must hold. The physical pressure is the tail chase and the faction's presence in the streets.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **The reader (291st).** Cleon shows the Path from inside: "A record sits … An instruction *leans* … This is a stair." Karis: "The strata lean too." Then **the acquisition at "there"**. The fifth row leans, and so does the board, and Cael does not turn the twelfth on anything else. He tells Lira first on the stair ("Twelfth. His. Lean."), then goes back down and tells Cleon at the bell, in the order he has it. "You did what." "That's not in the classification system. That's not *supposed* to be possible. That's —" / "No. I'll not say that one." The cup of water, and **the warning, verbatim**. "He went back to the carving." Vastin and Cael carry the case up the stair together. **The notice on the stair, verbatim**, with *He looked away. Kept.* The eyes are paid on the last flight. **"Cael did not tell anyone. He remembered."**
- **Rune practice (~2,000, folded across the days).** The twelfth leans at every made thing: the inn table's joints, the bridge's arches, the dividers' hinge. He learns not to look, as he learned not to look at the light. It is never used on a person.
- **The tail (291st–292nd night; ~2,500 set piece).** Seln and Cael follow a hood through the outer district. The hood goes through a tier gate Cael cannot legally cross, and he stops at the line. No faces, none taken, nothing learned but the faction's patience.
- **Dividers (292nd).** The parcel and **Hesk's note, verbatim**. The meal: four marks, two cups kept, and Seln's slip read and burned ("Closed"), leaving 130. Vastin and the cups: "Out here you count who's owed a seat." This is **the first inventory written inside walls** (§12 C10). **The light, not steady for a count of one:** "an instrument turning toward him." It is the fourth entry on the page. He does not cross. The promise holds at its first cost.
- **The leaf (293rd morning).** Describe the object before the reading: horn-coloured, point-written, the scraped name with its first upright stroke. Karis reads it **aloud once** (§9; §10 Tier A 1), then paraphrases it once: "the Architect" is a title, about four hundred years ago, and the purpose is the un-bound. "Older. Made. Us." Cael catalogues the room, not himself. **The chronicle** is marked: "Marked." / "Marked." Vastin reaches ("the mark that is given at the kindling —") and Karis answers with **two pages** and the ruler rule. The copy, with the gap drawn.
- **The yard fire (293rd evening).** Seln is told on the street ("Made." "For a kind."). Cleon comes up, the only time. Cael gives **Lira the page to read first**; nobody explains it to her. "So the whole time they weren't afraid you'd break something. They were afraid you'd *exist*." Brom prices it. Seln will keep the cache "a while longer". Karis says two pages, then sleeps on a bucket. Brom to Cleon: "Good." Vastin: "aimed … At a category. Which turned out to be a person. Who's sitting across the fire." Lira: "You signed the mark at kindlings." / "I would have." **Pole two**, taken on his feet and not catalogued, verbatim. "I want to stand in the middle of it." Cleon leaves: "Tomorrow the rest of the leaf. The list of parts."

**Physical challenge:** the tail (set piece). The eyes' cost on the stair.

## What must remain true / withheld

- **The reveal is spoken once.** "Four hundred years" appears only in the record's voice, in Karis's single paraphrase, and in Cael's pole-two sentence. "The Architect" is a title, never an agent.
- **There is no line between the leaf and the sigils**, anywhere, in any head. Never use *terminal*, *circuit* or *infrastructure*.
- The chronicle's hole stays a hole. Book 11's plant is marked, not read.
- The twelfth is not turned on Cael's own still place in this movement. It does not turn unbidden until Millrace (Movement 7).
- The light is deniable. Nothing hints at agency. Nineteen, and **no birthday**. "Eight days inside" is counted from the 284th.
- Cleon looks away from the boy on purpose and keeps that, and says no more than the leaf says. Karis does not theorize. The record's fear is reported, never endorsed.

## What the author owns

The chamber's light on the leaf, and the length of the silence before Karis reads. The texture of the meal. The route of the tail. How Lira reads the page. The pacing of the fire's voices within the fixed order: Lira first, then the circle, then Vastin, then Cael.

## Where we leave pressure

"Now what." There is a bearing, a set of marks nobody can read yet, and a site at the end of the line that has been quiet since they built the machine. He wants to stand in the middle of it. Tomorrow comes the list of parts, then the road east.
