## Where we enter

It is the 294th day. The list of parts takes all day, and Cleon's finger is on the last line at the bell. Twelve fragments. Karis's hand is bound and she writes with it anyway. Vastin is on day 15 of a month. There is still no mule.

## What this movement is for

The book turns east, on the same road read backwards. Two crews join by contract and not by friendship. Cael watches the Force line in a meadow and does not yet know where it will matter. The grade is cracked, and the sites run oldest-last into the deep edge. At Millrace the healer reads him as deep as she goes. Meanwhile, and against the card, the second instrument turns inward by itself. We are in the last third now: progression surfaces at turning points, and the grade's vocabulary is counted separately (§7.2).

Consequential turns and promises:

## What must remain true / withheld

- **Force is seen, not acquired.** The thirteenth comes at the Span's barricade.
- The Rune finding is **told to no one**. Oryn's finding is hers, whole. The two instruments' disagreement stays unresolved.
- Oryn does **not** hear the leaf. Cleon is not above ground; his goodbye is at the iron door.
- The grade is observation only, with no who and no why. No first-tier site appears. "Four hundred years" occurs only in the fixed 298th log.
- "Noted" order: Vastin's first is here. Teague does not say it yet.
- The wall's count is the crew's six, Karis included, plus Teague's four. The healers are not on it.

## What the author owns

How the road east reads in reverse. The plank room's crowd. The meadow's details. Teague's crew at the table. How the grade's numbers come off the sheets. Millrace's lean-to, light and noise. How long the deep reading takes on the page.

## Where we leave pressure

Twelve walk east along a straightedge's line, two of them on no wall's count. A healer's route and a researcher's archive ride on one mule. A boy carries two findings that do not agree and a warning that has started to lean. Five days out is a fence the Compact built, and somebody is still watching it.
