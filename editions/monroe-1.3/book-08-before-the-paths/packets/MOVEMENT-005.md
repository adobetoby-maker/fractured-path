## Where we enter

It is the 284th day, under Treswick. The lamp goes into the middle gallery. A light is already on at its end, and a man is already reading.

## What this movement is for

This is the archive's first week. Karis has found the room she has wanted all her life. A marshal learns his name is a blade with no handle. A craftsman shows how he reads a made thing's bones. Brom is offered the life his family wanted and picks up a box. Seln looks at the door his old trade would have used and decides nothing. Then the faction reaches inside the walls. A man with nothing behind him holds a line for people the Compact would restrict, and Cael comes away with the eleventh. We cross the first third into the middle, and progression language surfaces at the turning points: Rune explained, Iron Wall paid, the notice.
