## Where we enter

It is dawn on the 299th, at Millrace ford. There are twelve and a mule. Teague's wrist is wrapped. Vastin is on day 20. Karis is writing with the bound hand. Oryn's road runs east with them for a day and a half and then it does not.

## What this movement is for

The leg goes on the page as the place where two crews learn each other, and where the young one becomes someone a reader will grieve. Then the fourth site: a watch-post, a barricade, a captain's line, and a width that wins because the people inside the line have stood there before, with nothing gained by it. The thirteenth arrives. The next night every finding goes onto one blanket, the lamps finding is said aloud and filed on its own page, and a marshal finds a crack he does not pry.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **The leg (299th–303rd; ~9,000).**
  - The Stair's bowl: Karis spends an hour at the rim checking a number she already has.
  - **The cairn at noon on the 300th:** Oryn looks once, straight, and walks on east.
  - The Hall's marsh, where Cael read himself twice in the autumn.
  - **Two dry camps, and water at the seep (~3,000 set piece).** Shale-backs stand on the slope by the only water. Teague's field reading leads. The young one moves too fast and the tall woman stops him with a hand, wordlessly.
  - "Known" is entered under each date. The crest at dusk on the 303rd.
- **The crest.** The watch-post: hut, fire, kit under a hide, two timbers, seven hoods, two crossbows, one low practitioner. "They knew." Seln: "That iron's been in the ground years. Not one winter." Teague: "Drawn?" Cael draws it. "Nobody declares. Nobody dies. They're seven people on a posting. Noted?" / Teague's **first "Noted."**
- **The Span fight (304th; ~4,500, developed; BOOK_MAP §8).**
  1. The watch has the first exchange. Brom takes bolts on Iron.
  2. **Teague goes through the middle.** Cael is at his left, a stride back, the whole exchange. **The thirteenth.** The notice renders verbatim. The recoil goes to the wrist, then the elbow: "Through."
  3. The watch reforms. **"Nothing was gained inside" is entered first.** "By count." Five go over stake thirty-four (Cael, then Lira, Karis, the young one, Vastin) and four stay on the shelf. They walk. The pinch. Eleven seconds. "You'll die in there." / "We've been in three times." The watch goes east one at a time and is not pursued.
  4. The broad man goes over the sink's edge at the fourth second, and Teague does not turn.
  
  **Win sentence, verbatim.** Oryn sets and knits the leg and is grey for a quarter-hour. The healers nod. Teague's arm: "That's a fact about a line." / "Noted" / "Everybody says that now." Brom's week of colours.
- **The Span itself (noon).** An arch on two piers over the sink. Carvings on the piers' inner faces, copied. No steps: the arch is walked as a road rises. The bearing runs "Through". **Nothing happens on the crown.** The thirteenth listens and the ribs know the cost. **The log, verbatim.**
- **The shale camp (305th).** The blanket and its contents. The rows lean along the bearing. The point is fixed with a thumbnail circle.
  - **The lamps finding, verbatim.** Vastin: "If a man designed the apparatus, then the sigils —" / the ruler rule. Two pages. Vastin's third "Noted." **Cael does not add his own third page.**
  - **The directive and the objection, verbatim.** "That's a crack." The seals are unnamed. "The Compact … Yes. Part of it."
  - The broad man's "Aye". The healers' third exchange: one woman's hands in the other's work.
  - **Force, dry:** Cael lines his frame on the shale without firing. The thirteenth listens and the ribs warn him.
  - Seln on the pass: "I looked at it." **The close, verbatim.**

## What must remain true / withheld

- **No wall drill with Teague's crew.** The doctrine "behind it" is told to them only on the pass morning.
- The Compact knows the crew walks out declaring. **It never sees what works inside.** The watch sees bodies stand inside the line.
- Teague is **not told** Cael took his Force. The Force is not used, only lined dry. "First use" belongs to the pass.
- The two pages stay separate in every head. Never use *terminal*, *circuit* or *infrastructure*. "Recent administration" means **the Compact**. Nobody reaches for "someone else".
- The cairn is on the 300th. Count Oryn's days from it (§12 C3). No cutaway at the court.
- The tall woman and the older healer still do not speak to the six. The broad man's first word to them is "Aye", on the 305th.

## What the author owns

The leg's country, camps and weather. How the seep is solved. The watch's behaviour in detail. The crown's view. The blanket's order of work. The humour of two men on sticks at one fire who never mention it.

## Where we leave pressure

A notch in the hills to the east, low and square, where the bearing runs. Seln did not like it and will look again from nearer. Past it, by the office's distance, the oldest site waits. Cael wants it more than anything since he was fourteen, and he has told Karis why. He was right. He does not yet know how right.
