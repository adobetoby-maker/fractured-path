## Where we enter

It is dawn on the 299th, at Millrace ford. There are twelve and a mule. Teague's wrist is wrapped. Vastin is on day 20. Karis is writing with the bound hand. Oryn's road runs east with them for a day and a half and then it does not.

## What this movement is for

The leg goes on the page as the place where two crews learn each other, and where the young one becomes someone a reader will grieve. Then the fourth site: a watch-post, a barricade, a captain's line, and a width that wins because the people inside the line have stood there before, with nothing gained by it. The thirteenth arrives. The next night every finding goes onto one blanket, the lamps finding is said aloud and filed on its own page, and a marshal finds a crack he does not pry.

## Where we leave pressure

A notch in the hills to the east, low and square, where the bearing runs. Seln did not like it and will look again from nearer. Past it, by the office's distance, the oldest site waits. Cael wants it more than anything since he was fourteen, and he has told Karis why. He was right. He does not yet know how right.
