## Where we enter

It is the 281st day, on the border road's last stretch. The city grows by the wayposts: nine miles, six, three. There are six of them and no mule. The boxes are on Brom's good shoulder, on Lira and on Cael, and the kit's case is on Vastin, who picked it up at the fork-stone unasked. Vastin has walked beside Cael since the rise.

## What this movement is for

This is the book's one partly quiet movement. The reason: its tension is legal and bodily, a [SHATTERED] body entering a Compact city whose top holds a station. It does not need a fight. It holds a learning encounter in which an instrument fails, a confession, the city shown as a machine, and the irony that the only legal door into the oldest record of what Cael is admits him as a number. Progression teaching stays dense in the first third: the ten as currents, the Path-tiered city (passes, tier gates, the smith's two forges), and the provision's columns.

Consequential turns and promises:
- **The reading inward (281st, the six-mile post).** First he works out the line between "stop touching it" and "do not read yourself". Then come the fourth and fifth readings. The ten are named as currents. The still place is read *around*, not touched. **Finding: the reading feels current; the still place has none; "a cord on a stone."** He needs an instrument for what does not move. He writes down later that he is afraid of that.
- **Cael and Vastin on the road's edge.** Vastin sits, for the first time since the cut. "I read your file …" (the span anchored, not numbered: §12 C2). "You were the instrument." / "I was the error." **The confession, as narrowed:** "I declined to read you a second time. You know that. What you do not know is why." In return Cael gives him the instrument's nothing, the thing nobody else holds. It is not friendship. It is ground. Then **the log, verbatim** ("color" stands).
- **Treswick (282nd).** The grey bridge is older than the walls. The outer gate counts heads and asks for nothing. The outer district works as a mechanism. The mule at the trough. The inn with two doors: 136. Each of them looks at the city in their own way. Vastin: "I have papers I will not show. I want that said once." Brom: "They shoe by the tier." Cael walks the district's edge. **The road to the registry crosses the square**, and the promise holds. **Seln's report:** the quarry, the provision, hands entered by count, the clerk, the exposure. "Four hands." / "I've two." "Entered by count. That's the door." **The close, verbatim.**
- **Cleon at noon (283rd).** "Off floors." / "Fifty-two sheets." His terms. He stops a step late.
- **The provision (284th).** They go up by count under Cleon's pass, which the keepers count with a pen. The square is avoided by Seln's route. Four hands: Cael, Brom, Lira and Vastin, who says "Yes" before anyone can ask. Seln is not on the count. The clerk does not ask, and writes "four". **Lira's ladder does not pull.** The iron door. "The tiers closed, and the dark opened."

**Physical load:** a six-mile approach, three tiers and two gates climbed with boxes and a bad knee, and the walk round the district's edge. One small physical beat is the author's option.
**Cutaways (~4,000):** Oryn, ~2,500, on her north circuit: Oxhollow's gate, a man with a wrist set wrong, a birth, the unread ribs on her mind. Vastin, ~1,500, in the outer district inn ("the first honest district"). He knows nothing of the mechanism and does no date arithmetic.
**Development:** about 15 Cael beats. Supporting: Vastin 2–3, Lira 2, Seln 1–2, Karis 1–2, Brom 1, Oryn 2, Cleon 1.

## What must remain true / withheld

- Lira's paper is **registry paper, Iron first rank, formal** (§12 C9), and stays unshown.
- Cleon's Path and tier come through Seln's report ("Rune Path. Bronze.": ruling 17). Cleon takes no notice of Cael. Cleon's Path is **not shown** in this movement. Bones come in Movement 5.

## What the author owns

Treswick's texture: brokers' tally boards, the rope-walk, the ferry landing, the smith's two forges and the coloured coats at the gates. Which way Cael walks the district's edge. Whether the optional physical beat happens and what it is. How the counter talk about strata goes, heard through the yard door.

## Where we leave pressure

Cael is through an iron door older than the walls, as a hand carrying a box, into a dark that had been there before any of it was. Above him a whole city reads bodies. Below him nothing reads anything. He has a question he has never let anyone hold, and he is about to watch a stranger whose craft is reading how things are built.
