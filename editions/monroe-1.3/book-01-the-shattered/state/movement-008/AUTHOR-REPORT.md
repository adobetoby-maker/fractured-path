## Discoveries and deviations (with reasons)

1. **Length: 33,300 words against ~28,000 (+19%).** Nothing was padded, and nothing was summarized to save length. The overrun comes from three places. Dessa II needed four developed exchanges. The quiet week holds five supporting-cast beats the brief asks for (Unclassified, apricots, errand boys, Sella and Torvin, the old man), plus Lira on trust and the Pressure practice. Coss needed both his cutaway and his table scene. **Flag for the coordinator**: the book total moves accordingly.
2. **Dessa II runs ~3,300 words against the 1,500–2,000 allowance.** It is one scene. The decisive fourth exchange and the recovery before it are expanded, as the task asks, and each exchange changes the problem: the false clock, the trap sprung, the nod found, the decision made on the mark. Outcome: **won**, fourth, called. The cost is the hip and the eye, carried through the quiet week. Dessa learns too ("Next time I'll watch your mark").
3. **The answer's logic is Cael's own objection turned round.** Section four's "cannot be assessed by ordinary means" is the very thing he argued in the autumn, and the definitions in section one keep him out of the category. This makes the find before dawn a recognition, and makes Ilsev's counter-procedure feel earned rather than arbitrary.
4. **Coss learns of the ring from Ilsev's report**, because Cael answered her about his arm and declined to name Vell. This satisfies BOOK_MAP §5 ("by M8 that the boy fights in the circuit") without anybody naming the keeper.
5. **Coss comes back on his own days, in his own coat, at his own fare.** This is the "person rather than title" decision made physical. The pressure on him comes from a section head's "view" that "several people hold". Coss knows only departures from procedure and draws no conclusion. He never sees a flag himself; the senior flag stays for M9.
6. **The quiet week is enforced by injury and by Lira's ruling** ("A week. Nothing."). Pressure practice comes after it, alone, facing the trough, which keeps it "only ever used alone and in fragments".
7. **The old man's lesson pays his ch 8 invitation** ("Come back when you've got coin for yard time"). It is a learning encounter with a body in it: pushing, a finger, getting up from the ground. No Path is shown and nothing of his past is asked. The correction Cael can attempt again is getting up in pieces, which he uses on the wall with Lira the next evening.
8. **Hesk's "being seen" letter is written on the page in M8 and arrives in M9.** The protected line first appears in the letter itself, as BOOK_MAP requires. Hesk's answer comes from the timepiece: a thing that is talked about gets brought to a bench and opened, while quiet things go out with the ashes. Hesk thinks around the old file and stops.
9. **The source seam is fixed.** Nothing in the quiet week quotes "someone else wants me gone" before Lira and Coss produce it in ch 53.
10. **Sella is the boots woman.** She has been in the house since ch 8; this movement gives her the canon name.

## Source reuse

The source chapters (18–20) were read once for events and never opened while drafting (see AUTHORSHIP.md for the coordinator method note). After drafting, the author re-read its own retold scenes for source-shaped sentences and recomposed about twenty-five places where a beat followed a source sentence's order. Examples:
- Ilsev's "where did you learn to argue" became a question about whom to be grateful to, answered with Hesk's rule.
- Ilsev's "you've handled every part" farewell became "Mind the stair."
