## 1. Reader response (unscored)

**Where interest caught.** Immediately: a market a boy knows by the hour "handed a cup filled to the brim and told not to spill it", and the pin seen before its shape can be read, because he has seen that shape in wax. The shadowing holds by geometry (lanterns as a ruler, the egg woman's blanket, sixty counted in a bricked doorway, a cat with great dignity). Torvin's "Define often." The old man's "Wonder what changed," and the walk down the lane to the answer that is him. In ch 22 the letter lag lands emotionally for the first time (Hesk writing "two losses" to a boy who is 0 and 4 and has just come up in somebody else's coat). The information seller's three sentences are the best new episode in the movement: local, exact, and chilling because the man asked about letters home and never once about the yards. Ch 24's two watched bouts are small contests in themselves, and the late back foot arriving as a cold certainty is a genuine progression beat. Ch 25 is the peak: four exchanges, four different problems. Ch 26's honest sort is an act (the torn page unfolded and its sentence finished), which is what the coordinator asked for. Ch 27 ends on a stranger Cael watches for two breaths and does not write down.

**Where it slipped.** Ch 23's Vell cutaway is strong in its parts but long in the middle: the "who next" catalogue (Baro, Brenna, the Fenrow Ember, Petra) and the paragraph re-explaining the ledger custom already given in Movement 3. Ch 24 is the slowest stretch for a young reader (two watched bouts, the weights, Doss, a week of reports) though each piece pays later. Ch 26's write-up runs interior for a long time before the torn page gives it a body. Two lines made me stop for the wrong reason: Cael's "Six bouts, one win" and the letter's "It was the sixth bout" (he has fought five; see §3).

**Which person matters.** Lira, by a distance, for what she does not say on the step. Then Dessa, who becomes a person through four pages and then asks Vell a question nobody expected. Vell's private "Atypical" and her notes to a successor she has never let herself choose. Doss, in a page and a half, carries the book's cost ("I was in that book… Then I wasn't").

**Which event I expect next.** The thin case going up the fish steps to the post. Hesk's reply, due tomorrow or the day after. Who Vell puts across from him "Sunday week."

**Would I continue.** Yes. The movement pays its own promises (the tell, the win, the sort) and plants the next one in plain sight.
