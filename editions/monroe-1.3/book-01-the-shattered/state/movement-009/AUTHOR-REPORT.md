## Discoveries and deviations (with reasons)

1. **Length 33,969 against ~37,000 (−8%).** The aftermath chapters (59, 60) came in short; nothing was summarized to save words and nothing was padded to reach the budget. The bout itself is ~5,400 words across ch 57–58 (allowance 5,000–6,000).
2. **The partnership mistake has a Vell edge.** Vell's third condition is the knife: she asks "Has the girl been told?" knowing the answer; Cael kept the condition only to the letter. Lira names that at once ("*Told*. She knew you").
3. **"You're more like him" cuts both ways**: Hesk's years of deciding alone what Cael could carry, and Hesk's own refusal to be put in a drawer. "Half of it" kindly.
4. **Lira's term is born in her cutaway** (packet option), from the academy coal stairs; paid after the bout with "Then be one."
5. **The crossing comes from evidence the reader watches accumulate**: Feryn's three accounts, Doss, the Stone woman's bucket, the week-two Bronze, the washer-bill drawing, the floor over the river in ex 1, the slow sixth in ex 2, the market gate in ex 3. The ruling line stays blank until the bout.
6. **Feryn's loss-lesson is used in ex 3** (hold the strike; be where the opponent's exchange ends) — and Darrow uses the same idea back in ex 4, which is why the Wind is not enough.
7. **The unexplained use does not resolve the decisive problem alone**: it buys half a second and returns force only along a road Cael had proved and loaded nine times; the tenth is his choice, thrown as the Pressure's cost arrives *during* the exchange (the coordinator's "costs at the wrong moment").
8. **The old man gives the nod instead of his number** — an inversion of his habit.
9. **Vell says his name at the rope before the bout** (the yard had come), and writes it after; the board had it first, in Red Cap's chalk.
10. **Hesk's "properly" is paid by a letter written before the result**, so his pride is in the telling, not the win.
11. **Coss hears the result through the district return**, which also explains how the section head heard of the bout; the senior flag's date (the day after) is noticed and explicitly not concluded from.
12. **The tollhouse half-mark footprint is left untouched.**
13. **Lira's healer reference** uses no place name (Merchant Row is Denvash).
