## Discoveries and deviations (with reasons)

1. **Chapter boundaries.** Five days, seven chapters. Arrival night (7); the district day (8); Lira (9); the Hesk cutaway, then the Cinder House morning (10); the watched bouts and the vouch (11); the terms and the bout (12); the aftermath, the Vell cutaway and the walk (13). 2. **The road notes' gate** (ch 7). M1 Ch5 had "Ardenmere's gate opens at dawn and closes at full dark", against "no gate". I reconciled them in story: the note meant the Ranked core's gate across the river. Cael has traced a true figure into the wrong place, a second-generation version of the bracket lesson.
3. **The cart, written down** (ch 9). Lira's question brings the road back through a body detail (his hand goes to the strap Yeni mended over the hub scuff). He writes the cart that night as bare fact, with no arrow and no word, and does not count it. Flag: M1 said "unwritten until Lira's question" — now written, uncounted.
4. **Vouch cost found out** (chs 11–12). He asks Torvin, then Yeni, then Lira herself. The brief only says he "resolves"; I let him follow through, because finding out the price before he spends someone else's name is the movement's moral beat. It also gives "last four exchanges" a reason beyond curiosity.
5. **Cael's name kept out of Vell's ledger** (chs 12–13). BOOK_MAP §1 has the ledger list him by name "for the first time" after Darrow. So Vell does not write his name. She writes "unrated debut (14; nothing to register; vouched, L.)", and her cutaway states the custom: a name goes in "when the yard had a reason to need it". This plants the ending.
6. **"Nothing to register"** is what Cael tells both Vell and Renn (Vell's cutaway is canon-bound to that phrase). Renn's "I don't have one" became "Nothing to register."
7. **Lira and Renn's figure.** She refuses to describe the figure before the bout ("knowing didn't help me") and explains it on the walk. This keeps her post-bout speech honest, so it does not read as if she had withheld a warning.
8. **Marrow.** I added an over/under at one exchange, and Vell's "the book" is ambiguous on purpose (her ledger or Marrow's). Marrow lost on the over and did not lay it off. Lira's "Who won?" / "Marrow." about her own Renn bout.
