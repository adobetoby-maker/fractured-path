1. **ch13 74 — "three people had watched him move tonight."** Sixty people were on the rope.
   The intent (the three who could read it) is clear from the list that follows, but the literal
   claim snags. Fix F1.
2. **ch13 78 — "until the last of the crowd had gone."** The next paragraph then has the pie
   boy and Marrow leaving *after* the crowd, and she opens the ledger only after Marrow. The
   pre-repair "until the yard was empty" was changed to avoid "yard… yard" after the signpost
   was added, and the logic slipped. Fix F2.

   House morning is Wednesday, one week after the certification Wednesday), but ch10 121, in the

   definite article invites a reader to take them as the same day and put Cael's morning before
   Hesk's Tuesday. "the next morning" is true on both POV clocks and removes the collision. F5.
4. **ch13 238 — pains sentence (optional).** "ribs and shoulder and the stinging forearm, so
   that every breath was a small negotiation" hangs the breath on all three; only the ribs do
   that. F6 ties it to the ribs. Minor.
5. **ch12 212–214 (no fix).** "the yard was full." followed by "an ordinary sort of night:
   sixty people" — the pre-repair mediating sentence is gone. Reads as "full, for a middling
   night"; left alone.
