## Discoveries and deviations (with reasons)

1. **Hesk is told the five.** The promise was "Lira first, before Hesk". Once Lira had heard it, the letter followed the same night, and the untold count fell to zero. The source's Ch16 letter to Hesk ("Told Lira everything… the fragment") supports it, and it pays the long "count of untold things" motif. Hesk's reply stays inside his reserved history. **Flag for the coordinator** (deviation 12 above).
2. **Lira's cutaway is the reception of the telling itself**, from "Some of it's yours" on. She tells her row of three and her eleven drops in return. That closes her M3 secret and makes the knowing partnership two-way.
3. **The opponent learns twice.** After the second exchange Feryn hides the build under stillness and talk. In the fourth he does not release at all; he puts his hand where the boy's go-round will take him (Doss's "once too often"). Cael's reading improves every exchange: none, halfway, at the decision, at the decision again. It is still outrun.
4. **Vell's call is set up in her cutaway**, not narrated during the fourth exchange. She decides beforehand: the first exchange after working weight that can only hurt him. Cael's "Good call" pays it.
5. **The "[SHATTERED]" answer is given at Vell's table**, so that Vell now knows the word (BOOK_MAP §5). Corvane was changed to ask "What are you?", so that nobody in Ardenmere except Lira and Coss knew it before Feryn.
6. **The Feryn bout is a Monday-noon exhibition** in sleet, off the card with a gate. This explains a "forty-one" crowd after Renn and Corbin drew larger ones.
7. **No second Vell scene after the bout.** It was planned and dropped to keep Vell short.
8. **Corvane is a Pressure practitioner at Copper**, so the lesson has a body in it. Her air touching him for an afternoon with no notice becomes part of the contact arithmetic in ch 47. This stays hedged.
9. **Dessa at the board** plants M8's Dessa II ("a talk owing… Not this month") without choosing its outcome.
