## Where we enter

About five weeks in. Four unrated losses, each longer; the log thickening; Lira knows the word and has stayed. A slow, hazy market morning.

## What this movement is for

The Compact's shadow becomes real, and patience pays for the first time.

Consequential turns:
- **The sweep.** A man with the balance-and-hand pin walking an unhurried route; Cael shadows his geometry without changing pace; doubles back through three streets (a tense non-combat physical sequence). Torvin's "four in eleven years"; the yard-owner's "Wonder what changed"; Lira's arithmetic ("the district's one new variable"). **Book 6 plant:** the reader should feel that a single quiet sweep is disproportionate. Optional new episode: Cael buys one answer from the information seller about what the man asked (local knowledge only).
- **Letter to Hesk** with the lag shown. It must **not** quote "being seen is not the same as being safe" — that line is coined in Hesk's later letter (fix the source seam).
- **Two weeks watching Dessa** from the third row: four pages becoming a person — the private count, the left side when tired, never looking at the crowd. Lira: "Leave room for the version of her you haven't seen."
- **Dessa I** (2,500): spending versus banking; the late back-foot tell; the built third exchange (his, earned); her advance in the fourth; the pivot he did not decide. "No book on you." "Rematch?" "No. Not yet." Vell's unread second line and **"It's a compliment. Don't spend it."**
- **The step.** The second instance, logged honestly — and now he sorts the Renn stone into the count. "Two instances is a pattern…" Lira offers "Borrowed." — a word set down, not insisted on. His promise: when he knows, she is first, before Hesk.
- **Cutaway — Vell** (~2.5k): choosing Dessa for him; what "atypical" means in her book; the private note "Recommend the boy be matched against increasingly credible opposition."
- Doss's nod across Torvin's table; Torvin's near-speech; Renn's theatrical claim around the district.

## What must remain true / withheld

- Nobody identifies who requested the sweep; Cael's only conclusion is "what changed: me".
- "Borrowed" stays a guess about a feeling; no mechanism; no notice yet.
- Protected: "It's a compliment. Don't spend it."
- Remove the source's omniscient aside about standard procedure — the procedure's departure will be Coss's to notice in Movement 5.

## What the author owns

The shape of the shadowing; how the information seller is used (or not); Dessa's bout choreography; how Lira and Cael spend the evening after; how the log entry is quoted.

## Where we leave pressure

A first win, a promise made on a step, and — unseen by Cael — a Compact field agent arriving in Ardenmere with a file that is too thin.
