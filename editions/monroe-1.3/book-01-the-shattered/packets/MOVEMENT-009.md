## Where we enter

Two days after Coss's offer. "I'm not leaving." The logic on the page: quiet is what makes him easy to erase.

## What this movement is for

The climax and the handoff. Visibility as friction; a partnership mistake made and repaired; accumulation beats power; something in him does what he cannot describe; the mystery widens instead of closing.

Consequential turns:
- **The Wager.** Vell, ledger open: "I want a marquee bout." Her three conditions — she has the final say on the opponent; no special terms; **Lira is told before anyone signs**. Feryn on the low wall: "Convince me." He names **Darrow Innes**, Bronze R1, Iron Path, eight years professional. Feryn's reason (he clawed to Bronze alone).
- **Lira's anger**, earned: "You arranged this without asking me first." The real apology; "I'd rather be worried and informed than calm and useless."; "This forgiveness doesn't renew automatically."; "You're more like him than you probably realize." **Cutaway — Lira** (~2.5k): that night, deciding to stay in fully, and the term she has set for herself ("During, I coach. After, I get to be a person." may be born here).
- **Two weeks**: precision that accumulates on a padded post; the district's Iron Path veterans; Feryn on futility as the real weapon; Hesk's letter arrives (protected line); the steps: "It doesn't change what you are. It changes who'd have to explain themselves…"; **Coss's second visit**, less composed — "I can't out-quiet that."; the daughter about Cael's age.
- **The Bout** (two chapters, 5,000–6,000): the yard two hours early; ~300; the slates (11→13→15, 13 at the close); Feryn's coat; the claim/evidence/ruling of the **crossing** — the half-beat Darrow's frame needs to reset after redistributing a load, low left, rib to hip; four exchanges; same place every time; Darrow noticing; the Wind evasion seen by everyone in the third; the fourth — the angle and the absorption together, force sent back up its own road, the rest of the strike landing; the tenth strike into a crossing carrying more load than it ever has; "Begin." … "It goes in the book… as it happened." Darrow rises alone. "What are you?" / "I don't fully know yet." Lira's fear arriving after. The concurrent notice; *They're not separate things I switch between…* **Cutaway — Vell** (~2k): the call and the entry, never during the decisive exchange.
- **After**: the ledger by name; the baker, the errand boys, the one-degree nod; Hesk's "I'm proud of you" letter; **Cutaway — Coss** (~2.5k): three days after, the senior-level flag he cannot read; he closes the file without a note. **The market stranger**, two days after the fight; not chased. Lira: you do not have to carry every piece alone. The final log lines.
