## Where we enter

The morning after Renn. Shoulder and ribs bruised, a forearm scrape he cannot account for. Lira has vouched; Vell has written more than a result.

## What this movement is for

Losing usefully, and the first shared weight. This closes the book's first third: keep the progression vocabulary dense (ratings, Paths, ranks, the Arbiter as a ledger others can check).

Consequential turns:
- **Three losses, each longer** (1,200–2,000 each): **Brenna** (Shield R2, never resets — he concedes ground to watch; frustration as a tell); **Amrit Sole** (Ember R4, the air distorts before the heat); **Petra Voss** (Force R3, the shoulder lean he sees and cannot yet beat — "about speed, not about not seeing it"). Baro watched: every opening is bait.
- **The Log begins** — entries, then the private back section written only after the tactical entries. Teach the method as the series' engine.
- **Lira's mornings** become a curriculum: the deflection sequence with the skipped middle; bread split between combinations; "You're not a natural… the most efficient learner"; the shaded half of the yard.
- **The Book 3 plant** (the only required plant this book): mid-drill, a low sweeping evasion that drops his centre in a way he was never taught, arriving exactly in time — like wearing someone else's coat. "How did you know that was coming?" / "I don't know." It does not come again on call.
- **The dark Arbiter**, midnight, Torvin's courtyard: absence, not silence. The next morning Lira describes checking hers ("like checking a ledger"); she looked the word up after the first day; it becomes a shared, present problem; she reads the notice; **"The circuit doesn't care what the registry says."**
- **Cutaway — Lira** (~2.5k): after the plant — what she saw, the decision not to push, and what she wants out of these mornings for herself.
- Being noticed: the fish seller's nod, the errand boys' wager, a man asking Torvin after "the circuit kid".

## What must remain true / withheld

- The plant is instinct, unexplained. No theory of borrowing yet. Count it in the log as his **first instance**; the Renn exchange stays unsorted. Do not stage the source's duplicate second sparring incident.
- Lira's cutaway knows only what she saw; no mechanism; no backstory beyond source; no romance.
- Protected: the plant exchange; "The circuit doesn't care what the registry says."
- Keep Petra Voss out of any scene with Coss (audio). Opening: fix the source's "He waited four days" — this is weeks in, and there is no summons yet.

## What the author owns

The order of the three losses and the morning sessions; which loss gets the most room; the courtyard night; the shape of the log on the page (sparingly quoted); the humour in Lira's "I am *generous*"; where the Lira cutaway sits.

## Where we leave pressure

The first night he sleeps without being the only one who knows the whole shape of what he is. The next movement brings a silver pin into the market.
