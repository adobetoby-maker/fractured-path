## Where we enter

Closed-pending; forty-two days. Five bouts logged, one win. Lira: "Faster doesn't mean reckless. It means efficient."

## What this movement is for

The fight movement, and the book's first progression payoff. The reader enjoys getting better with him: six problems, six different lessons, and at the end a notice nobody else can see.

Consequential turns:
- **Six bouts, four wins, in order:** a Copper R2 he mapped (short, distrusted for being easy); **Renn II** (2,000 — Renn comes back with a plan built for the boy who outlasted him; Cael built his counter first from the crowd; won in the fifth exchange; pays Renn's booking and his "second look"); **Sarel** (2,500 — Bronze R1 Blade; speed outruns watching; he adapts after two exchanges; loses in the sixth; her crouched respect); **Talis** (1,500 — the ground-vibration tell he was not looking for; loses in the fourth); **Dellin** (1,200 — the plan that survives contact; "You knew what I was going to do before I did it"); **Corbin** (2,000 — forty fights, the measuring stick; the win people will repeat). Carry bruises between them.
- **"Gone."** Lira makes him say it aloud when a plan dies, forty times, until the word is not needed. She demonstrates his mistakes back at him; he names a habit of hers (over-committing on her dominant side when tired) that cost her at the academy without her knowing why — she is startled and glad.
- **Lira's room.** She has saved enough; she takes it, two streets over, and is proud. Small, real.
- **The notice.** The thirty-ninth night, **at Torvin's**, reviewing the log by lamplight: something changes before any word arrives. Then the protected text. He knows at once where it came from. "Not yet" — and he is not sure that sentence is honest. He tries the step in the narrow space by the cot: clumsy, slow, on purpose. Short letter to Hesk.
- **Assessed-Copper.** Vell: "It's not a real rank… inside this district, it's the difference…" and "For what it's worth. I do."
- **Cutaways:** **Lira** (~2.5k) — the six weeks from her side; the room; being seen accurately by someone she is training. **Hesk** (~3k) — letters about a sweep and a summons arriving a week late; the guild review sitting (pending or quietly survived — do not strip his standing); Joren calls at the workshop, working at patience; Alis is apprenticed at the healers'; Hesk does not tell either of them the word.

- Costs are felt, not itemized: no named "landing beat", no half-breath ledger, no deployment-range column (Book 2). The step works badly on purpose and well only under pressure.

- Hesk's cutaway: no name, no fourth-case end, no more history.
- Sarel is a woman (source). Talis's ground-vibration tell is the one Book 2 remembers.

## What the author owns

Which bouts are scenes and which are brisk; how the six weeks' time is felt; Renn II's plan and counter-plan; how Lira's room is shown; the notice's arrival in his body; the order of cutaways (neither interrupts a decisive exchange).

## Where we leave pressure

A designation where there was only a dark place, a word he has not said to Lira, and a senior evaluation coming. The next movement opens with two weeks of theory he cannot keep to himself much longer.
