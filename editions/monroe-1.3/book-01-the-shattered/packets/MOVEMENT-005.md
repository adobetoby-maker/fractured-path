## Where we enter

Days after the Dessa win. Coss is in the cramped district office with the wobbling desk, reading the file for the fourth time.

## What this movement is for

Let the reader stand inside the institution for a while, see how strange this file is from a competent man's chair — and then watch a fourteen-year-old beat the procedure with reading.

Consequential turns:
- **Cutaway — Coss** (~6k, the book's longest cutaway): attention over brilliance; what a [SHATTERED] file should contain and this one doesn't; the regional authorization code he cannot trace; **Halvern** (the sweep came from regional; the constable lent a pin); Torvin's legal minimum; two days walking a district that is a town his training cannot read; the word "circuit" sliding past him as local colour. Stage the irony: on his second afternoon, three streets away, **Lira fights a bout** (new, 1,500–2,000) while Cael watches from the rope — his months of watching her, on the page. The summons: the first "physical retrieval" clause he has written for a child; the risky note in his report.
- **The summons delivered** ("Compact left this. Didn't like the look of it."). Cael goes to Lira at once (the habit of telling begins); *Forty-eight hours. Find the seam.*
- **The archive** (Halden): three volumes, a footnote, a statute, an appendix; a hypothesis read four times aloud like a load calculation. Copy it twice.
- **Lira edits** the objection: cut the clause that braces for an argument; three flat sentences.
- **Coss concedes** — he checks the citation from memory; "This is going to be a very unpopular report to file."; closed-pending; six weeks. "What are you going to do with six weeks?" / "Get stronger. Build a reason for people to notice if I disappear." Coss's warning: he can buy time, not an answer.

## What must remain true / withheld

- Coss knows procedure and the departures from it; he does **not** know who set the authorization, nor anything about the Arbiter, flags beyond this file, or the four cases' truth. He suspects "someone above"; nothing non-human. His rank stays unstated beyond "field agent, eleven years".
- **Book 6 plant:** the untraceable authorization and the deliberately thin file.
- Coss first reads the file in **Ardenmere** (fix the source's later "in Denvash").
- Halden's archive and the district office must be reachable on Unranked access (Bible: City Access).
- Protected: *Forty-eight hours. Find the seam.*; "Get stronger. Build a reason for people to notice if I disappear."

## What the author owns

How the Coss cutaway is broken across chapters (it may be two passages); the shape of Lira's bout and its result; the archive as a puzzle with false starts; the meeting's rhythm. Humour: Halvern's permit disputes; Torvin.

## Where we leave pressure

Cael standing at the board with six weeks and a plan to make himself expensive. Coss filing a report he does not enjoy, on a desk above his own.
