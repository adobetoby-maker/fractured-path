## Where we enter

Weeks past the six-week deadline; Feryn's bruises fading; the senior evaluator's summons arrives on an ordinary day.

## What this movement is for

The procedure's ceiling. Cael wins the argument and learns that winning arguments will not be enough; then he chooses to stay. This movement holds the book's deliberate quiet week — keep it moving with a fight and a lesson.

Consequential turns:
- **Before dawn** he finds the temporary non-standard category himself and builds the answer; Lira trims it on the steps.
- **Ilsev**: the counter-procedure laid out exactly; his answer from the definitions section three pages earlier; her silence; "You're right."; legal review — months; Coss's frustration through the thin wall and Ilsev's dry reply. Then, privately: **the flag** she did not request and cannot trace, which activated when she applied standard procedure. "You deserve the truth even when I can't finish the sentence for you."
- **Dessa II** (1,500–2,000, new face for the bout he enters straight after): she comes to the table on her schedule; she knows him now; whether his reading survives a defender who has studied him back. Outcome is the author's; carry its costs.
- **The quiet week**: recognised without being explained ("Unclassified."); the apricots ("carry it; don't let it carry you"); the errand boys' heavy delivery day; an afternoon with the **old yard-owner** that becomes a lesson in a veteran's economy (no Path shown, nothing of his past); Torvin holds the rent; Sella named and funny; Lira on trust ("I'd rather not know and be here").
- **Pressure practice alone**: slow, a full breath to gather, visible in his stance, a leaden arm after.
- **Coss off the record** — plain clothes, a public table: policy not law; leave voluntarily; closure; "I can make it disappear"; the flag question and his stillness; "I don't know anything about a flag." Lira hears it raw and names it; he is **not leaving**. *He doesn't want me gone. Someone else wants me gone. He's delivering the message.*
- **Cutaways:** **Coss** (~2.5k) — Ilsev's report on his desk, the flag in her careful prose, the decision to sit at a public table as a person rather than a title. **Hesk** (~3k) — Cael's letter about Ilsev and a flag; the workshop's cursed timepiece and its one loose spring; Hesk writing the answer to Cael's earlier question — the letter that will arrive in Movement 9 (*Being seen is not the same as being safe. But it's not nothing.*). Hesk's "You're describing a home" reply may land here too.
