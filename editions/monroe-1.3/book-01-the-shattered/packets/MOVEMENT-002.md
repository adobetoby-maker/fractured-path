## Where we enter

Day 4, dusk. The open gate nobody guards. Hesk's money, three notebooks plus the leather one, a copied archive entry, and a body that stepped out of a cart's way on the road without asking him.

## What this movement is for

Arrival, the first ally, and the first real fight — a loss that is secretly a purchase.

Consequential turns:
- **Learning the district** as a map with people in it: Torvin's rules, Yeni's sewing kit, the market's haggling, the boy who will save him a week for a price, the information seller's three-sentence trade, the old man in the yard ("Watching's not nothing"), the crossroads board read for eleven minutes. Progression teaching continues: ratings on the board, Paths on every fighter.
- **Lira.** Let her be faster and funnier than expected; her academy story; the cart and the skewer vendor (Wind at Copper: "the world gets slightly more negotiable"); "the gap". He says [SHATTERED] himself, choosing the order of things. Her question makes him remember the road.
- **The Rules** in the Cinder House yard: the circuit as a habit with rules; the grappler's deliberate wound; the Bronze pair. Lira offers to vouch and he resolves to find out what that costs her.
- **Renn** (2,000–2,500): Vell's terms said back once (stage the registration **once** — the source doubles it); the probes, the heel, the shoulder bought; the sweep that misses; the third exchange that is not his and is not reconstructable; the fourth's figure; down. "It doesn't miss. It missed." The booking a month out. "Instinct." / "tonight's answer."
- **Cutaways:** **Hesk** (~3k): the house after, the guild-standing review letter, Ressa's bread, Garrik who would not look, the first letter he starts and restarts. **Vell** (~2k): the ledger entry that runs past two lines, and why she writes it.

- No Brom, no Ironyard, no Dace. The board's notices may come from the source (Thirdweek, Fenrow, Dava, the Ashwood boy) — do not mint new named fighters.

## What the author owns

How many days the district takes; what Lira and Cael actually say beyond the protected lines; the grappler and Bronze-pair choreography; Renn's figure in detail; the humour (Lira's "professional evaluator of new arrivals"; Torvin's economy of words). Where the two cutaways fall, provided neither interrupts the Renn bout.

## Where we leave pressure

Walking home beside Lira in the dark, four exchanges beaten, shoulder and ribs paying, and one stone in the pile he will not look at. The next movement is three weeks of losing on purpose.
