- **Cael** (formally Caelen Hesk-ward, registration 41-7843-V) is fourteen — the birthday was

  instances"). He does **not** know the archive's dispositions or Hesk's history.

### Ending (Movement 9, chapter 60 — about seven months later, late spring)

- Cael is still **fourteen** (no autumn crossed; no birthday staged). He lives at **Torvin's**
  boarding house in Ardenmere's Unranked District (a shared room). Lira has her own small room
  two streets over. (Book 2 opens a year after arrival with both in two adjoining rooms near

### 3b. Learning and training arcs (~35k)

- **Hesk's workshop (M1).** Load calculations, "never trust a number you can't trace back to
  where it came from," a housing that fails its stress test. Cael's whole method begins here;
  show it as a craft he already has.
- **The community hall on Tuesdays (M1, new).** Pays the source's planted note "ask Hesk about
  Tuesdays": Hesk takes him once to the broadcast of circuit and Path athletics; they watch a
  fight together and Hesk shows him how to watch a stance. A craft lesson, a grandfather's secret
  hobby, and the origin of Cael's attention to "the third exchange".
- **Lira's curriculum (M3–M9, every movement).** A continuing syllabus, not repeated drills:
  her three-part deflection and the skipped middle; "the gap"; thinking about feet until you
  stop; the shaded half of the yard; "gone" said aloud until the letting-go needs no word;
  demonstrating his mistake back at him; finally precision that accumulates (Iron Path). Cael
  teaches back: he names a habit she did not know she had.
- **The Log as method (M3 on).** Entries, columns of claim/evidence/ruling, the private back
  section written only after the tactical entries. Teaching the log is teaching the progression
  engine of the whole series.
- **Corvane (M7).** An afternoon becomes a lesson with a body in it: she makes him stand while
  she "builds" and asks what he sees; the stick figure and the spiral.
- **Deliberate fragment practice (M6–M9).** Clumsy on purpose, reliable only under pressure;
  the step in the narrow space by the cot; sitting an hour with the texture; Pressure tried
  alone, slow and visible. Crude costs only (see §6 limits).
- **Iron Path precision (M9).** The padded post that records nothing; three, four, five strikes
  to a place "that hasn't finished healing yet."
